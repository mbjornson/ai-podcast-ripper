# Ben Tiggelaar - Buying IT Services Companies — Inspired Or Acquired

## Summary
Ben Tiggelaar is an entrepreneur specializing in the IT Managed Service Provider (MSP) sector. His background is highly strategic, having worked in private equity for four years before acquiring and operating a healthcare services company. He transitioned into the MSP space, acquiring DataTell, which he is currently scaling through a deliberate strategy of add-on acquisitions. The episode details his current operational metrics, including $8 million in revenue across 34-35 employees, and outlines his growth plan to reach $20 million in the next four to five years.

The core discussion centers on the mechanics of scaling a service business through M&A. Ben emphasizes the critical importance of shifting revenue composition from low-margin product sales to high-margin, sticky, recurring revenue services. While his initial focus was purely on the financial outcome (the exit), his current mindset is focused on the "journey of the here and now," prioritizing building a robust, enjoyable business that is scalable but not necessarily designed for an immediate sale.

## Key Points
- **Revenue Composition is Key:** The most significant financial shift is moving away from high-dollar volume product sales (low margin) toward recurring revenue services (high margin). While overall revenue may appear flat, the improved composition of revenue drastically increases profitability.
- **Strategic Debt Management:** Ben advises against aggressively paying down debt early. He views the interest paid on debt as a valuable tax benefit, preferring to retain the debt structure until the time of a planned exit or sale.
- **The Acquisition Flywheel:** His growth strategy is built on a continuous acquisition cycle: Figure out the business, execute the first 12 months, then acquire one new business each year. This process allows him to build capability and scale exponentially (e.g., the last acquisition expanded their server set capability by 2-3x).
- **Deal Flow Generation:** To maintain a pipeline, he uses two primary methods: 1) Direct outreach to MSP owners, emphasizing the benefit of selling to a long-term owner (10-20 years), and 2) Creating content by launching a podcast ("The MSP Owner") to build relationships and gather industry insights.
- **Mindset Shift from Exit to Journey:** He contrasts his first business, where the focus was solely on the financial outcome, with his current approach. Now, the focus is on the process, enjoying the work, and building a positive work environment, which is a critical element of long-term entrepreneurial satisfaction.

## Tools & Resources
- **Bison Business:** Firm specializing in nationwide M&A, helping companies in the $1M - $20M revenue range buy or sell.
- **Apple Tree Business Services:** Provides nationwide quality accounting and tax services for businesses.
- **The MSP Owner (Podcast):** Ben's initiative to interview other MSP owners, serving as both a content strategy and a deal flow generation tool.

## Action Items
- [ ] **Financial Strategy:** Review your current debt structure. If you are profitable, evaluate if the tax benefits of carrying debt (interest payments) outweigh the psychological benefit of paying it down early.
- [ ] **Revenue Mix Optimization:** Conduct a detailed audit of your revenue streams. Identify and prioritize shifting revenue from low-margin product sales to high-margin, recurring service contracts (e.g., managed services, retainer agreements).
- [ ] **Build a Deal Flow Engine:** Start a niche podcast or content series focused on your industry's founders/owners. This serves the dual purpose of networking and generating leads for future acquisitions.
- [ ] **Career/Business Move:** Develop a clear, multi-year acquisition strategy (e.g., "One acquisition per year") to ensure continuous growth and capability expansion.
- [ ] **Mindset Practice:** Intentionally shift your focus from the "exit number" to the "process." Define what "having fun" and "enjoying the work" means in your current role to prevent burnout and maintain motivation.
