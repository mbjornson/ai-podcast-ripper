# Mike Pedro - Scaling & Selling A Med Spa Group Platform — Inspired Or Acquired

## Summary
Mike Pedro is the founder and CEO of MD Aesthetics, a rapidly growing med spa group platform that started in 2018. The discussion centers on his journey from building an organically grown practice into a multi-state acquisition machine, culminating in him being actively engaged in a sale process to private equity (PE). The industry—including services like Botox and Hormone Replacement Therapy (HRT)—is highlighted as having tremendous tailwinds, attracting significant PE capital looking to capitalize on this growth.

The core narrative is the transition from "being inspired" by the business to strategically planning its exit. Pedro recounts how an initial call with a PE firm prompted him to professionalize his growth strategy. Instead of simply growing organically, he leveraged internal training academies and established operational efficiencies before pivoting to aggressive inorganic growth (acquisitions). This process allowed them to scale rapidly from two locations to 13 across three states in a short period, making the company an attractive target for larger capital groups.

## Key Points
- **Valuation Multiples are Scale Dependent:** PE firms value practices differently based on size and profitability. For small practices (1-2 locations), expect valuation multiples of 2x to 3x EBITDA. However, once a practice achieves higher EBITDA ($500k to $1M+), the multiple increases significantly into the 4x range or higher.
- **The Power of Inorganic Growth:** The decision to move from organic growth to aggressive acquisitions was prompted by market signals—the realization that either they would continue growing rapidly or risk being "eaten" by larger competitors.
- **Systematizing Acquisition/Integration:** Rapid scaling requires a dedicated, robust process. Pedro’s secret sauce for fast acquisitions wasn't just capital; it was building an entire integration team and playbook. This included specialized roles for operations, vendor management, and IT systems to ensure seamless onboarding.
- **Efficiency Gains in M&A:** By creating a structured integration process, the time required to onboard and integrate a new location dropped significantly—from an initial 8 weeks down to much faster cycles.
- **Strategic Alignment is Paramount:** Successful rapid growth relies on taking leaders across the organization and forcing alignment through daily planning meetings to ensure all teams are working toward common, measurable goals.

## Action Items
- [ ] **Develop a Financial Model Focused on EBITDA:** Prioritize revenue streams and operational efficiencies that directly boost Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation, and Amortization. This is the primary metric for valuation multiples.
- [ ] **Build an Integration Playbook:** Formalize a step-by-step process for acquiring new locations (or departments/processes). Assign dedicated roles (Operations Lead, IT Systems Manager, Vendor Coordinator) to ensure consistency and reduce onboarding time.
- [ ] **Mandate Cross-Functional Alignment Meetings:** Implement daily or bi-weekly planning sessions with key department leaders to maintain focus on shared priorities and eliminate siloed work.
- [ ] **Consult M&A Advisors (Bison Business):** If considering scaling, engage a professional broker early to understand current market multiples and assess the company's true valuation potential before making major capital decisions.
