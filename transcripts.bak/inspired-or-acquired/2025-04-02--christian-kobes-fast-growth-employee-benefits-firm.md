# Christian Kobes - Fast Growth Employee Benefits Firm — Inspired Or Acquired

## Summary
Christian Kobes is the founder of 1921 Consultants, an independent employee benefits firm specializing in providing comprehensive benefits solutions (medical, dental, vision, disability, life) to mid-sized employers. The company's core strategy revolves around serving the "underserved market" of companies with 50 to 500 employees, a segment that often lacks the high-level innovation and dedicated partnership offered by larger firms.

The discussion highlights the strategic shift from working within a large, captive insurance carrier (Aflac) to establishing an independent brokerage. This transition allowed the founders to expand their scope beyond single products, recognizing that true value lies in offering a holistic benefits package. The firm emphasizes that success in this industry requires not only deep industry knowledge but also a commitment to building robust client relationships and providing proactive, consultative service.

From a business growth perspective, the conversation details a rapid, intentional scaling model. The firm reported $2.5 million in revenue in 2024, projecting $4 million for 2025, and aiming for $10 million within the next two to three years by maintaining its niche focus and continually expanding its service capacity.

## Key Points
- **Targeting the Underserved Mid-Market:** The firm has carved out a niche in mid-sized companies (50–500 headcount). This segment is often overlooked by large enterprises, allowing 1921 Consultants to provide high-level innovation and dedicated partnership where larger competitors fail to engage.
- **The Danger of Single-Product Focus:** Kobes references the adage, "if all you have is a hammer, everybody looks like a nail." This means that relying on a single product (like health insurance) limits the perceived value and scope of the business. Success requires expanding into the entire benefits ecosystem (dental, vision, disability, etc.).
- **Intentional, Balanced Scaling:** The company manages growth by ensuring the service team headcount grows *ahead* of the revenue number. This proactive staffing strategy is crucial for maintaining service quality and ensuring that the client experience does not degrade during periods of rapid expansion.
- **Market Opportunity:** The industry suffers from a "lack of transparency" regarding who is doing good work. This gap presents a significant opportunity for specialized, high-value independent firms to establish trust and market leadership.
- **Revenue Trajectory:** The company demonstrated aggressive, managed growth: $2.5M (2024) $\rightarrow$ $4M (2025) $\rightarrow$ $10M (2-3 years).

## Tools & Resources

- **Apple Tree Business Services:** Provides nationwide quality accounting and tax services for businesses looking to operate or acquire.

## Action Items
- [ ] **Market Niche Analysis:** Identify an "underserved mid-market" within your industry (e.g., a specific headcount range or industry vertical) and develop a highly specialized, comprehensive service offering that addresses the gaps left by large, generalized competitors.
- [ ] **Product Diversification:** Audit your current service offerings to ensure you are not relying on a single "hammer" product. Systematically integrate adjacent, complementary services to increase the perceived value and scope of your solutions.
- [ ] **Operational Scaling Model:** Implement a growth metric that mandates that service/support capacity (headcount) increases by a predetermined percentage *before* revenue targets are set, ensuring quality remains high during expansion.
