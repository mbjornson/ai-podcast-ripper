# Martha Stavrou - Can you sell one division of a company? — Inspired Or Acquired

## Summary
This episode features Martha Stavrou, owner of Deja Vu Furniture and More, a business specializing in liquidating hotel furniture and offering installation services, alongside a retail furniture store. The episode focuses on the potential sale of her move-out and liquidation business, highlighting the strong relationships she’s built within the hotel industry and the business’s healthy growth trajectory. Stavrou emphasizes the personal connection she has with her customers and crew, and her husband’s passion for the work. The conversation delves into the financial health of the business, estimating potential value based on revenue, discretionary earnings, and the separability of the operations. The episode also includes a brief promotional segment for Patrick Dichter’s accounting firm, Apple Tree Business Services, and Clint Fiore’s business acquisition platform, Dealonomy.

## Key Points
- **Established Business Model:** Stavrou’s business began 20 years ago with liquidating used hotel furniture, evolving into a full-service operation including installation and retail sales. The core business is built on relationships within the hotel industry, securing contracts with major chains like Four Seasons and Charles Hotel.
- **Separable Operations:** The move-out and liquidation business is financially separable from the retail store, allowing for independent tracking and management. This separation is crucial for valuation purposes.
- **Strong Discretionary Earnings:** Based on preliminary calculations, the move-out business generates approximately $300,000 - $400,000 in discretionary earnings annually, a key metric for potential buyers. This is driven by a solid customer base and efficient operations.
- **Potential Valuation:**  The business could be valued at around $1 million - $1.2 million, assuming continued growth and the ability to transfer relationships to a new owner.
- **Personal Connection as Asset:** Stavrou’s husband’s strong customer relationships and “feel for his customers’ needs” are identified as a significant asset, potentially driving continued success for a new owner.
- **Growth Trajectory:** Revenue is growing, projected to exceed $1 million this year, indicating a healthy and expanding business.
- **Owner's Role:** Stavrou's husband is currently a hands-off operator, relying on his existing network and experience.  He's considering scaling the business further if he were to dedicate more time and effort.
