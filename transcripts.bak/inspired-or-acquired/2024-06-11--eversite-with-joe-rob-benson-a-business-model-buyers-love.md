# Eversite with Joe & Rob Benson - A Business Model Buyers Love — Inspired Or Acquired

## Summary
The episode features Joe and Rob Benson of Eversight, a digital marketing agency that successfully transitioned its core business model from traditional high-cost project work to a "Website as a Service" (WaaS) subscription model. The discussion centers on how this structural shift dramatically increased revenue predictability and stability, making the company highly attractive for valuation.

The conversation is framed around determining the company's worth. Joe and Rob demonstrated that by moving away from large, upfront project fees ($10k-$15k) to low monthly recurring fees, they significantly de-risked their income stream. Their credibility stems from running a digital marketing agency for 20 years and successfully implementing this scalable SaaS-like model across multiple states and countries.

The core takeaway is that the value of an established business is not solely determined by its current top-line revenue, but rather by the percentage of that revenue that is recurring (sticky) and the longevity of the customer relationships.

## Key Points
- **The Power of Recurring Revenue:** The company achieved 83% recurring revenue from a mix of services and the monthly website subscription. This high ratio signals stability to potential buyers, allowing for higher valuation multiples than traditional service agencies.
- **De-risking Through Model Shift:** They successfully transitioned their business model over two years, moving from large upfront project costs (e.g., $10k-$15k) to low monthly fees (e.g., $400/month). This shift is the primary driver of increased valuation and stability.
- **Valuation Metrics:** The hosts estimated a current enterprise value in the $2 million range, citing sticky customers and high recurring revenue as key multipliers. They noted that while discretionary earnings were estimated at $400k-$500k, the *predictability* (83% recurring) is what drives the higher multiple.
- **Retention Rate:** The company boasts a lifetime 97% retention rate on its subscriptions, which is an extremely powerful metric demonstrating product/service stickiness and low customer churn risk.
- **Growth Projection Strategy:** They project aggressive growth rates for the near term (40% this year), followed by sustained, but slowing, growth (35%, then 30%), indicating a sustainable path to scaling while managing costs.

## Tools & Resources
- **Eversight:** A Website as a Service platform that allows small businesses to manage their digital presence and marketing through low monthly subscriptions.
- **Proprietary Software:** The company has invested hundreds of thousands in proprietary software development, which is noted as a significant asset that enhances efficiency and client features—a key differentiator from competitors.

## Action Items
- [ ] **Analyze your own revenue mix:** Calculate the percentage of your total revenue derived from monthly/annual recurring subscriptions versus one-off projects or ad spend pass-through. Aim to increase this ratio above 80%.
- [ ] **Productize services:** Identify high-cost, upfront service offerings and restructure them into tiered, low-monthly subscription plans (SaaS model) to improve revenue predictability.
- [ ] **Focus on Retention Metrics:** Implement systems or features that make your product/service indispensable to the client (e.g., proprietary software integrations, unique data insights) to drive retention rates toward 95%+.
- [ ] **Develop a "Sticky" Asset:** Invest in internal tools or processes that are so efficient and valuable they become difficult for clients to leave, thereby increasing switching costs.
