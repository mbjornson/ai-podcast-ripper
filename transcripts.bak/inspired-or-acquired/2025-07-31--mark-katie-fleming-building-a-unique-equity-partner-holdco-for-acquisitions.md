# Mark & Katie Fleming - Building A Unique Equity Partner HoldCo For Acquisitions — Inspired Or Acquired

## Summary
The episode features Katie and Mark Fleming of Owner Actions, a firm specializing in small business acquisitions and building private equity holdings. Their background transitioned from traditional private equity and hedge fund work to assisting small business owners with the entire acquisition lifecycle. Owner Actions initially launched as a detailed resource website, but they found that the sheer volume of information was overwhelming to entrepreneurs. This led them to pivot their model from being a passive information source to an active, hands-on acquisition partner.

Their core expertise covers the entire M&A process: search, vetting, due diligence, quality of earnings analysis, and post-acquisition financial management (A/R, A/P, collections, and digital marketing). Crucially, they shifted their business model from charging traditional fees to working for equity, allowing them to become long-term partners in the businesses they acquire.

The discussion highlights their philosophy that the acquisition process must be centered on the *partner* first, not the business itself. They emphasize a structured "get to know you" process to ensure the business opportunity is the best fit for the individual's background and goals.

## Key Points
- **The "Whale Shark" Concept:** The host coined this term to describe successful individuals who operate as a hybrid of an investor (allocating capital) and an entrepreneur (possessing operational DNA). This signifies a high level of capability and dual expertise in both finance and execution.
- **Equity-First Model:** Owner Actions transitioned from a fee-for-service model to an equity-based partnership model. This allows them to be deeply invested in the long-term success of the acquired businesses, aligning their incentives with the partners.
- **Portfolio Metrics:** As of the discussion, the firm was involved in approximately 35 total deals, maintaining a portfolio of around 20 companies. Their typical ownership stake in these acquired businesses is a minority interest, usually around 10%.
- **The Acquisition Funnel Insight:** The M&A process is not linear. The host noted that a potential buyer's view of the world will typically *narrow* as they approach a transaction, and then *broaden* again as they move further away from the deal, which is a key indicator for advisors.
- **The "Partner First" Rule:** The guiding principle for acquisitions is determining "what business fits them the best, not what business fits us."

## Tools & Resources

## Action Items
- [ ] **Adopt the "Partner First" Mindset:** When evaluating investment or career opportunities, shift the focus from the inherent value of the asset/business to the compatibility and long-term fit with the individual's skills and goals.
- [ ] **Develop a "Whale Shark" Skillset:** Actively build dual expertise that combines financial/investor acumen (capital allocation, due diligence) with hands-on operational skills (marketing, collections, process optimization).
- [ ] **Re-evaluate Business Models:** If currently working on a fee-for-service model, explore ways to structure partnerships that involve equity or performance-based incentives to deepen commitment and long-term alignment.
- [ ] **Study the M&A Lifecycle:** Understand the stages of a deal beyond the LOI (Letter of Intent), specifically focusing on post-acquisition financial management (A/R, A/P, collections) and digital marketing integration.
