# David Barlev - Would you sell Goji Labs for $20M or hold on? — Inspired Or Acquired

## Summary
Goji Labs is a strategy-first product agency that assists companies—from early-stage startups to established organizations—with digital transformation and achieving core business goals. Instead of simply building an app, the agency focuses on auditing the client's existing problems, defining a clear roadmap, and then executing the necessary digital product development. The founder, David Barlev, highlighted that the company has been fully bootstrapped for over ten years, operating with a lean, distributed team of under 50 people.

The core value proposition is that they are partners, not just vendors. They help companies get "back on track" or launch entirely new ventures by pairing the right technology with the right use case. David’s role is highly strategic, focusing on high-level process improvement, managing partner relationships, and generating content to project the brand's expertise in getting products to market.

## Key Points
- **Strategy-First Approach:** Goji Labs emphasizes that their process is not coding; it is a cycle of **Audit $\rightarrow$ Clarity/Roadmap $\rightarrow$ Execution**. The most crucial insight is that "the roadmap never ends," implying continuous iteration and measurement are built into every solution.
- **Tech Stack Control:** They deliberately avoid being platform-locked (e.g., Salesforce-only or Webflow-only). Their preference for robust, scalable frameworks like **Ruby on Rails, Node, and Golang** is driven by the need for full control over the entire server infrastructure and architecture to handle high transaction volumes.
- **The Three Engagement Buckets:** Client engagements fall into three distinct models, which helps define the value and expected revenue cycle:
    1.  **Rapid/Accelerated:** Short-term fixes or realignments (1–3 months).
    2.  **Larger Builds:** New ventures or long-term growth (3 months – 2 years).
    3.  **Ongoing Retainers:** Low-intensity, continuous improvement partnerships (e.g., annual platform scaling for the WHO).
- **Bootstrapped Expertise:** The company's ability to remain fully bootstrapped for over a decade, starting with minimal capital, speaks to a highly efficient, expertise-driven business model where the team and knowledge are the primary assets.

## Tools & Resources

- **Accounting/Tax:** Apple Tree Business Services for quality accounting and tax nationwide.

## Action Items
- [ ] **Adopt a "Strategy-First" Mindset:** When approaching any business problem (personal or professional), mandate an initial "Audit" phase before proposing any solution or spending money on tools/services.
- [ ] **De-Risk Technology Choices:** When selecting a tech stack for a project, prioritize frameworks that offer maximum architectural control (e.g., Golang, Node) over proprietary, closed platforms, ensuring scalability and maintainability.
- [ ] **Structure Client Engagements:** If you are a consultant or service provider, categorize your services into distinct engagement buckets (e.g., rapid fixes, large builds, ongoing retainers) to better manage client expectations and revenue predictability.
- [ ] **Focus on Process Documentation:** Treat your internal processes (delivery, operations, client onboarding) as core intellectual property. Documenting and optimizing these processes is key to maintaining profitability and scaling a service business.
