# Kyle Ewing - Hire a CEO Or Sell a $12M business? — Inspired Or Acquired

## Summary
Kyle Ewing, founder of Terra Slate, runs a niche manufacturing business that produces durable, waterproof, and rip-proof paper substrates designed to replace traditional lamination. The company sells both blank paper and custom printed materials, serving high-volume industries like hospitality (menus) and specialized sectors (NFL play charts, submarine manuals). The core discussion revolves around the company's robust growth trajectory, which is projected to hit $10.5M to $15M this year, and the strategic philosophy that has driven its success. Ewing emphasizes that his business model is built on trust and consistent value, rather than aggressive, short-term revenue maximization.

Ewing's operational strategy centers on making the customer feel safe and valued. Instead of pushing customers to over-order, the company promotes a "order what you need" approach, backed by free overnight shipping and a one-day turnaround. This commitment to service and stable pricing—not raising prices since 2020—has built deep loyalty, allowing them to maintain a strong competitive edge against both traditional competitors and low-quality knockoffs.

## Key Points
- **Trust-Based Revenue Model:** Instead of relying on high-pressure sales tactics, the company builds loyalty by offering a superior product and maintaining stable pricing. This counter-intuitive approach (not raising prices when competitors do) is cited as a major competitive advantage.
- **Defensive Supply Chain Strategy:** To combat low-quality knockoffs from Asia, Terra Slate opened a facility in China. Their strategy is not to fight quality head-on, but to price-match and undercut the knockoffs, thereby making the knockoff market unprofitable and discouraging further imitation.
- **Focus on Self-Funded Growth:** Ewing explicitly stated his preference for funding growth internally rather than taking outside investment. He views outside capital as potentially "pouring fuel on the fire," suggesting that profitable, self-funded expansion ensures stability and control.
- **High Customer Retention via Convenience:** By offering free overnight shipping and a one-day turnaround, the company removes friction from the purchasing process. This encourages frequent, small orders rather than large, infrequent bulk purchases, strengthening the relationship with the client.
- **Financial Health:** The company reports a current EBITDA margin of 15% over the last 12 months, demonstrating strong operational efficiency even while planning for significant growth.

## Action Items
- [ ] **Develop a "Trust Pricing" Strategy:** Review your core product pricing. Identify areas where competitors raise prices aggressively, and consider maintaining stable pricing or offering value-adds to build customer loyalty over short-term margin gains.
- [ ] **Optimize Fulfillment for Frictionless Ordering:** Analyze your customer journey. Can you reduce the perceived effort of re-ordering consumables (e.g., implementing a "re-order in one click" system or guaranteeing 24-hour fulfillment) to encourage higher frequency of purchase?
- [ ] **Evaluate Ownership Structure:** If you are a founder, assess your reliance on outside capital. Model your growth plans assuming 100% self-funding to understand your true operational capacity and maintain control.
- [ ] **Research Niche Manufacturing:** Look for industries with high turnover of standardized, consumable materials (e.g., menus, play charts, signage) that can be improved with durable, low-maintenance materials.
