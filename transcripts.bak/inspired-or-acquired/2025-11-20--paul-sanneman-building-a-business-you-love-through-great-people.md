# Paul Sanneman - Building A Business You Love Through Great People — Inspired Or Acquired

## Summary
This episode of “Inspired or Acquired” features Paul Sanneman, a 75-year-old recruiting business owner who’s built a surprisingly successful operation from a garage-based startup. Sanneman’s credibility stems from 50 years of business coaching experience and a data-driven approach to finding and placing skilled tradespeople. He’s built a company employing 40 people (30 offshore) servicing contractors in industries like HVAC, plumbing, and home building, primarily targeting businesses between $1 million and $20 million in revenue. The core of his business model is a monthly fixed-fee service offering five placements for a set price, differentiating him from traditional headhunters who charge a percentage of salary. Sanneman’s approach is refreshingly pragmatic, emphasizing a lack of grand strategic planning and a focus on maintaining a happy, productive team and client base. He’s built a lifestyle around his business, incorporating activities like running, meditation, and travel, demonstrating a commitment to both work and personal well-being.

## Key Points
- **Problem-Solution Driven:** Sanneman started his business because existing coaching services couldn’t effectively address the staffing shortages faced by his clients. He identified a gap in the market and built a software-based solution.
- **Unique Business Model:** His monthly fixed-fee model, offering five placements for a set price, is a significant departure from the industry standard of 10-15% of salary. This approach caters to smaller contractors who don't want to pay headhunter fees.
- **Offshore Expertise:** Sanneman’s ability to manage a 30-person offshore team with zero turnover is a key differentiator. He attributes this to careful candidate selection and a focus on employee satisfaction.
- **Organic Growth & Pragmatism:** Sanneman’s growth strategy is largely organic, driven by word-of-mouth and a focus on client satisfaction. He deliberately avoids overly ambitious growth plans, prioritizing stability and a sustainable pace.
- **Lifestyle Integration:** Sanneman’s lifestyle is deeply intertwined with his business, incorporating daily routines like running, meditation, and travel, suggesting a deliberate choice to prioritize both work and personal fulfillment.
- **Success Without a Plan:** He admits to not having a formal succession plan, highlighting a relaxed approach to business management focused on maintaining the current operational state.
