# Ryan Bakke - Quickly Scaling a CPA Firm — Inspired Or Acquired

## Summary
Ryan Bakke is a sharp entrepreneur who runs a modern accounting and tax firm specializing in real estate investors. He details his rapid growth trajectory, moving from working at a "Big Four" firm to establishing his own practice. His initial goal was modest ($150,000 net income), but within three years, the firm achieved $800,000 in revenue, and is currently on track for over $2 million. The core of his success lies not just in volume, but in shifting the service model from basic annual tax compliance (prep services) to high-value advisory consulting.

The discussion highlights that modern CPA firms must evolve beyond being mere filing mechanisms. By focusing 50-60% of revenue on advisory and consulting fees—which include ongoing access, multiple one-on-one calls, and year-round tax planning—Ryan has achieved unusual profit margins. His growth strategy is built around establishing authority, building sticky communities, and leveraging niche expertise to create predictable, high-value client relationships.

## Key Points
- **The Value Shift:** The most critical move was pivoting from "annual compliance filing" (low margin) to "tax planning and hand-holding throughout the year" (high margin). Clients are now paying for access, strategy, and community, not just a tax return.
- **Authority Building via OPA:** To acquire clients, do not market directly; instead, provide value in niche online groups (e.g., Facebook groups for real estate investors) by answering questions daily without asking for anything in return. This builds organic authority, allowing established influencers (lenders, attorneys, etc.) to promote you using "Other People's Authority" (OPA).
- **Niche Down for Predictability:** Specializing deeply into a specific industry (e.g., real estate investors doing 1031 exchanges) ensures that the same complex questions are asked repeatedly. This allows the firm to build educational content (videos, podcasts) that answers those common queries, streamlining client interactions and building massive credibility.
- **The Power of Community:** Creating a "sticky" community around a shared goal or perceived enemy (e.g., the IRS, corporate America) increases client loyalty. The community itself becomes a value proposition, encouraging clients to upgrade their service level from basic tax prep to full advisory access.
- **Financial Metrics:** With $1.8 million in revenue last year, Ryan estimates that after paying himself a significant owner benefit (roughly 200k+), the remaining net profit allows the owner to retain approximately 50% of the total revenue as owner benefits—a phenomenal margin for a service business.

## Tools & Resources
- **Platforms:** Facebook Groups (recommended over starting new communities, as it utilizes existing high-density user bases).
- **Content Strategy:** Podcasting/Video Content creation (used to record answers to common niche questions, building authority years in advance of client contact).
- **Service Model:** Advisory Consulting Fees (charging for ongoing access and strategic planning, rather than just annual filing fees).

## Quotable
> "The best person to sell you is not you. It's somebody else." (Emphasizing the power of leveraging other people's authority.)
>
> "Community of people that are armed against a common enemy... it builds this community and it's very sticky where people don't want to leave."

## Action Items
- [ ] **Niche Down:** Identify a hyper-specific industry or problem set (e.g., 1031 exchanges for real estate investors) and become the undisputed expert in that niche.
- [ ] **Shift Value Proposition:** Stop selling compliance/filing services. Reframe your offering to be an *advisory service* that includes ongoing access, strategic planning calls, and community membership.
- [ ] **Implement OPA Strategy:** Dedicate 15-20 minutes daily to providing free, high-value answers in established niche Facebook groups (or similar platforms) without mentioning your services directly.
- [ ] **Build Evergreen Content:** Record short educational videos or podcast episodes answering the top 3-5 questions you hear repeatedly from your ideal client. Use this content as a lead magnet and authority builder.
- [ ] **Develop Community Structure:** Formalize a community around a shared goal (e.g., "Financial Freedom," "Tax Optimization") to increase stickiness and encourage clients to upgrade their service tier.
