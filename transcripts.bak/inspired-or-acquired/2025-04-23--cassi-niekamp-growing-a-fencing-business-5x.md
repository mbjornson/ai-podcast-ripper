# Cassi Niekamp - Growing A Fencing Business 5x — Inspired Or Acquired

## Summary
The episode features Cassie Kneekamp, owner of Bound and Fence, detailing her journey of scaling a traditional, blue-collar fencing business. Cassie acquired a 38-year-old company four years ago, which was initially generating under $700K in revenue. Through strategic modernization, operational efficiency, and aggressive growth, she has scaled the business to $3.3 million in revenue within that four-year period. Her background is rooted in white-collar consulting and digital marketing, making her pivot from corporate life to owning a physical, legacy trade business a central theme.

Financially, the discussion heavily focuses on the mechanics of acquisition and risk mitigation. The initial $1.2 million purchase was split 60% for the business and 40% for the real estate. A critical financial concept discussed is the "out of the woods" calculation: determining when the physical assets and inventory liquidation value exceed the outstanding debt, thereby protecting the owner from personal financial risk and the personal guarantee associated with SBA loans.

## Key Points
- **Exponential Growth in Trades:** Cassie grew the business from under $700K to $3.3M in four years, demonstrating that significant scaling is possible even in established, traditional trades.
- **Profit Center Identification:** The welding/fabrication department was identified and treated as a distinct profit center, achieving an impressive 55% gross margin. This highlights the importance of segmenting operations to maximize profitability.
- **Strategic Acquisition Structuring:** The initial purchase was structured with a 60% business / 40% real estate split, allowing the company to benefit from both operational growth and appreciating hard assets.
- **The "Out of the Woods" Threshold:** This critical financial milestone occurs when the liquidation value of physical assets (inventory, equipment) covers the entire business debt. Reaching this point significantly de-risks the owner by removing the immediate threat of personal financial liability (personal guarantees).
- **Real Estate Appreciation:** The discussion noted potential for substantial real estate appreciation, with conservative estimates suggesting at least $300K of appreciation on the land alone over four years, even in a slow market.
