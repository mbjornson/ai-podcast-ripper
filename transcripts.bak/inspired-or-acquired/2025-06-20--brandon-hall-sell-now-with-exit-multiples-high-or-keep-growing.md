# Brandon Hall - Sell Now With Exit Multiples High? Or Keep Growing? — Inspired Or Acquired

## Summary
Brandon Hall is the founder of Hall CPA, a rapidly growing accounting firm that has scaled from a small operation in 2015 to a global team of 105 people, generating approximately $14.5 million in revenue this year. The firm specializes in providing comprehensive advisory, tax, and compliance services, primarily to real estate investors, though its client base is diverse. Hall’s success is attributed to three core operational differentiators: maintaining a consistent organic growth rate of over 40% annually, implementing a results-based accountability model (measuring outcomes, not hours), and operating as a 100% remote firm. The discussion pivots to the classic "sell or grow" dilemma, where the hosts, who are M&A specialists, analyze the firm's financials to provide a preliminary valuation, revealing the significant market opportunity for mid-sized, profitable service businesses.

## Key Points
- **Organic Growth is the Gold Standard:** Hall CPA has maintained a top-line organic growth rate of over 40% per year since its inception, demonstrating exceptional scalability without relying on M&A deals.
- **The Power of Results-Based Metrics:** The firm rejects traditional time-tracking methods, focusing solely on whether a task was completed and delivered to the client ("Did the task get done and delivered to the client or not?"). This operational model is a key differentiator and efficiency booster.
- **Remote Operations as a Competitive Advantage:** Being 100% remote allows the firm to hire talent anywhere, eliminating geographical constraints and enabling them to tap into skilled workers in areas where traditional accounting firms cannot reach.
- **High Valuation Multiples in the Sector:** While the firm's estimated adjusted EBITDA is around $2.5 million (based on $13M T12 revenue and a 20% margin), the hosts noted that comparable, similar-sized companies are currently receiving offers at multiples of **10X or higher** the adjusted EBITDA.
- **The Mid-Market Bifurcation:** The accounting industry is currently bifurcated, with large players and very small players, leaving a highly valuable, underserved niche for mid-sized, profitable, independent firms like Hall CPA.

## Tools & Resources

## Action Items
- [ ] **Audit Your Metrics:** Stop tracking "time spent" on tasks and instead implement a strict "results delivered" metric for all team members and processes. Focus on the measurable outcome (True/False).
- [ ] **Evaluate Remote Scalability:** If running a service business, model how your current geographical limitations restrict your hiring pool. Develop a strategy to become 100% remote to unlock talent globally.
- [ ] **Understand Valuation Multiples:** When considering selling or raising capital, research the current market multiples (e.g., 8x-12x EBITDA) for your specific industry niche, rather than relying on general industry averages.
- [ ] **Focus on Organic Growth:** Prioritize strategies that drive consistent, high-percentage organic growth (40%+ annually) over relying on external funding or acquisitions.
