# Inspired Or Acquired - 10k downloads, listener questions, and our 2025 goals — Inspired Or Acquired

## Summary
The episode serves as a reflective, behind-the-scenes Q&A, marking the podcast's passage of 10,000 downloads and 30+ episodes. The speaker, an M&A specialist, details his journey from a classically trained sales role (telemarketing, insurance, network marketing) to becoming a successful entrepreneur and business broker. His professional arc was defined by a forced exit from his first company, Nature Blinds, which led him to realize the immense difficulty and opacity of the small business buying and selling process. This realization fueled his mission over the past decade: to systematically simplify and demystify M&A for owners and buyers alike.

His current venture, Bison Business, was initially focused on high-quality, low-volume deals. However, the conversation reveals a major pivot: partnering with Marshall Hatfield, an expert in scaling lead generation. Together, they are building "Dealonomy," a high-tech deal facilitation platform. This new platform aims to fundamentally change the industry by providing a systematic, scalable solution for accessing off-market sellers, moving the brokerage model from localized, relationship-driven deals to a national, tech-enabled system.

## Key Points
- **The M&A Pain Point:** The core thesis is that the process of buying and selling small businesses is currently too difficult and opaque ("too dang hard to navigate"). The market is underserved by technology and systematic processes.
- **The Evolution of the Brokerage:** The speaker transitioned from a traditional, relationship-based, regional brokerage model (Bison Business) to a scalable, tech-enabled lead generation and deal facilitation platform (Dealonomy).
- **The Value of Off-Market Leads:** The partnership with Marshall Hatfield focuses on lead generation for *off-market* sellers, which is identified as the critical missing piece needed to scale the brokerage from a high-quality, low-volume model to a national, high-volume system.
- **Experience as a Catalyst:** The speaker's forced exit from his first company (Nature Blinds) was not a failure, but the catalyst that defined his professional mission and expertise in solving the M&A problem.
- **The Business Model Shift:** The new model is designed to be a high-tech platform that facilitates deals, moving beyond simply connecting buyers and sellers to creating a systematic, scalable marketplace.

## Tools & Resources
- **Dealonomy:** The new high-tech deal facilitation platform being built to revolutionize the buying and selling of small businesses. (Expected public launch around March).
- **Bison Business:** The existing regional business brokerage firm that serves as the foundation for the expansion.

## Quotable
> "I love your vision except you're leaving all the money on the table because you can only sell a lead for so much; but the people that really make the money are the people doing the deals."

> "The authenticity and the realness of it and the demystification [of the process]—that's what we're doing."

## Action Items
- [ ] **Study M&A Mechanics:** Deepen knowledge of the Mergers & Acquisitions process, focusing specifically on the pain points of small business owners (the "demystification" aspect).
- [ ] **Develop a Systematic Approach:** If considering a business exit or acquisition, research systematic, tech-enabled platforms rather than relying solely on traditional networking/brokerage methods.
- [ ] **Analyze Sales Career Paths:** Recognize that diverse sales experience (retail, telemarketing, insurance, network marketing) builds a foundational understanding of human persuasion and sales cycles, which is transferable to any high-level business role.
- [ ] **Focus on Lead Generation Scaling:** For entrepreneurs, understand that scaling a business often requires solving the lead generation problem at a massive scale, especially for niche or high-value services.
