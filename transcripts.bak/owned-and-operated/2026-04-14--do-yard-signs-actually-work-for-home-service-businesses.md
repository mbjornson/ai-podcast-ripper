# Do Yard Signs ACTUALLY Work for Home Service Businesses? — Owned and Operated

## Summary
This episode analyzes the effectiveness and ROI of hyper-local marketing, specifically yard signs, for home service businesses (e.g., plumbing, roofing, landscaping). The discussion, led by industry veterans John Wilson (CEO of Wilson Plumbing Heating and Cooling) and Sam Preston (CEO of Service Scalers), establishes that while yard signs lack the "prestige" of a billboard, their cost-per-impression is dramatically lower, making them an economically superior choice for generating immediate, local leads. The core thesis is that the success of yard signs is driven not by advertising theory, but by neighborhood psychology—the "Keeping up with the Joneses" effect.

The hosts provide a detailed economic comparison, showing that yard signs offer massive reach for a fraction of the cost of traditional advertising. They advise that businesses should focus their yard sign strategy either on a strong, immediate *offer* (e.g., "20% off gutter cleaning") or robust *branding* to maximize impact. The conversation also touches on the necessity of scaling internal operations, recommending virtual staffing solutions to handle the increased lead volume generated by successful marketing campaigns.

## Key Points
- **Cost Efficiency:** Yard signs are cited as being 10 to 100 times cheaper per impression than billboards.
- **Economic Breakdown (CPM):**
    *   **Yard Signs:** Estimated cost is $5–$15 per sign, with a lifespan of 30–90 days. The estimated Cost Per Thousand Impressions (CPM) ranges from $0.50 to $5.00.
    *   **Billboards:** CPM is significantly higher, ranging from $5 to $20.
- **Strategic Focus:** Marketing efforts must be channeled into either the **branding route** (building long-term recognition) or the **offer route** (driving immediate sales/coupons).
- **Psychological Trigger:** The most effective placement leverages the "Keeping up with the Joneses" effect. Services that improve visible neighborhood aesthetics (driveway sealing, landscaping, roofing) are ideal because they create immediate, visible envy and demand from neighbors.
- **High-Value Niches:** The most successful yard sign campaigns target services that are high-cost, high-visibility, and aesthetically impactful, such as driveway sealing, landscaping, and window/roofing replacements.
- **Operational Warning:** While effective, the hosts caution that aggressive yard sign deployment can lead to neighborhood complaints and negative reviews on platforms like Google Voice Profile (GVP), requiring careful execution.
