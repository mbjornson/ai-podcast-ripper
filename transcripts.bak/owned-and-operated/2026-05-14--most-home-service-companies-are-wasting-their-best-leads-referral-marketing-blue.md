# Most Home Service Companies Are Wasting Their Best Leads (Referral Marketing Blueprint) — Owned and Operated

## Summary
This podcast episode, “Most Home Service Companies Are Wasting Their Best Leads (Referral Marketing Blueprint),” focuses on the critical importance of referral marketing for home service businesses and how many companies are failing to effectively leverage it. The episode highlights a significant gap – 83% of homeowners are willing to refer, yet only 29% actually do – and argues that this is due to a lack of proactive engagement and a failure to build trust and make the referral process frictionless. The conversation emphasizes the need for automation, particularly through systems like Voka, to manage inbound leads and nurture relationships. The guest, Murphy Nadal from ReferPro, stresses that lifecycle marketing – staying top-of-mind with customers – is crucial for maximizing the lifetime value of a customer and adapting to the rapidly changing digital landscape. The episode leans heavily on data and statistics to underscore the opportunity and the urgency for home service businesses to prioritize referral programs.

## Key Points
- **Referral Gap:** 83% of homeowners are willing to refer a business, but only 29% actually do, representing a significant 54% opportunity.
- **Automation is Key:** Manual referral tracking is administratively burdensome and ineffective. Automation is essential for managing the referral engine.
- **Trust is Paramount:** Building trust with customers is the foundation of successful referrals.
- **Lifecycle Marketing:** Staying top-of-mind with customers through ongoing communication and engagement is crucial for driving repeat referrals and increasing customer lifetime value.
- **SMS & Email Focus:** Utilizing SMS and email for lifecycle marketing is a key strategy for staying relevant and prompting referrals.
- **AI Integration:** The rapid advancements in AI and automation are transforming the referral landscape, demanding a proactive and adaptable approach.
- **Voka as a Solution:** Voka is presented as a specific tool that understands the needs of home service businesses, offering a comprehensive system for managing inbound calls, outbound follow-ups, and dispatching – particularly valuable for businesses using ServiceTitan.
