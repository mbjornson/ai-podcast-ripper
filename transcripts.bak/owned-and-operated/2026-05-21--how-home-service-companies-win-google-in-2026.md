# How Home Service Companies Win Google in 2026 — Owned and Operated

## Summary
This episode provides a deep dive into modern local search optimization, specifically focusing on how home service companies can dominate Google Business Profile (GBP) visibility in the increasingly competitive landscape of 2026. The discussion emphasizes that simply accumulating a high number of reviews is insufficient; Google's algorithm now prioritizes consistency, velocity, and recency. The core message is that GBP is not just a digital listing but the primary "storefront" and website for local service businesses. The guest provides actionable, technical advice on managing reviews, structuring the profile, and maintaining consistent activity to signal trust and relevance to both the algorithm and potential customers.

The conversation highlights a shift in focus from traditional SEO metrics (clicks, impressions) to tangible, bottom-line results (ringing phones and booked jobs). The expert stresses that while Google is constantly changing its rules—such as the recent concern over technician-specific reviews being deleted—the fundamental principles of building trust through consistent, high-quality local presence remain paramount.

## Key Points
- **Velocity and Consistency > Volume:** The most critical factor for ranking is not the total number of reviews (e.g., 10,000 reviews), but the consistent rate of new reviews (velocity). A company with 500 reviews acquired consistently over the last year will outrank a company with 20,000 reviews if that volume was achieved five years ago and has since stalled.
- **The 24-Hour Response Rule:** All reviews, regardless of star rating (even 1-star), must be responded to within 24 hours. Responses must be professional, bespoke (not copy-pasted), and include specific context (the service provided and the location).
- **GBP Activity Baseline:** To maintain a strong profile, aim for a baseline of 3 to 5 posts or photos per week. Regular updates signal to Google that the business is active and relevant.
- **Strategic Profile Expansion:** When expanding service verticals (e.g., adding "Electric" to "Plumbing, Heating, Cooling"), do not simply keyword stuff. Instead, legally establish a DBA (Doing Business As) to update the official business name across all GBP listings.
- **The Goal is the Phone Ring:** The ultimate measure of success is not SEO metrics (clicks/impressions) but the ability to drive real, high-quality calls and booked jobs.

## Tools & Resources
- **Service Scalers:** Mentioned as a service specializing in home service companies, focusing specifically on driving high-quality calls and booked jobs through LSA management, GBP optimization, and website builds.
- **DBA (Doing Business As):** A legal mechanism recommended for updating the official business name on GBP when expanding service verticals, ensuring consistency and avoiding keyword stuffing penalties.

## Quotable

>

## Action Items
- [ ] **Implement a 24-Hour Review Response Protocol:** Respond to *every* review (good or bad) within 24 hours, ensuring the response is unique, professional, and mentions the specific service and location.
- [ ] **Establish Content Cadence:** Commit to posting 3-5 photos or updates on the GBP profile weekly to maintain high activity and relevance signals.
- [ ] **Audit GBP Consistency:** Review all GBP listings to ensure the business name, address, and phone number (NAP) are identical across all platforms and legally reflect any expanded services (using a DBA if necessary).
- [ ] **Focus on Local Service Scaling:** If the business is growing its service offerings, consult with a legal professional about filing a DBA to properly update the official business name on Google.
- [ ] **Track Call Volume:** Shift marketing focus away from vanity metrics (clicks/impressions) and instead track the direct correlation between GBP activity and the number of booked jobs/calls received.
