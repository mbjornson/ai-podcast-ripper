# AI Is Creating a MASSIVE Gap Between Contractors — Owned and Operated

## Summary
The episode details the rapid and profound integration of Artificial Intelligence into the home services and contracting industry, arguing that AI is creating a massive operational gap between early adopters and laggards. The discussion, featuring John Wilson (who runs a plumbing, HVAC, and electric business) and Tyson Chen (founder of Avoca), moves beyond simple AI tools (like ChatGPT) and focuses on "agentic" and "autonomous" workflows. The core thesis is that AI is transitioning from being a helpful assistant to being a self-managing, multi-agent workforce capable of handling complex, end-to-end business processes.

The guest highlights that the current state of AI deployment is "surreal," noting that many contractors are actively building and deploying AI solutions. The conversation emphasizes that the most significant gains are found in automating historically manual, high-friction processes—specifically in procurement, accounts receivable, and capacity planning—areas where human error and manual effort have traditionally slowed growth.

## Key Points
- **The Shift to Autonomy:** The industry is moving past basic AI tool usage and toward "autonomous" systems. The goal is to build agentic workflows that manage complex tasks (like running an RFP or managing marketing campaigns) without constant human intervention.
- **Exponential Productivity Gains:** The productivity difference between those who use AI tools and those who don't has jumped from an estimated 30-50% difference to nearly 5x.
- **AI Development Speed:** AI agents can now develop complex products that previously required a team of people and weeks of effort, completing the work in a single day.
- **Autonomous Capacity Management:** A sophisticated workflow can leverage external data (like weather patterns) and historical customer data to predict future demand, and then autonomously trigger targeted marketing and maintenance outreach to existing customers to keep the job board full.
- **Procurement Automation (RFP):** AI can conduct an entire Request for Proposal (RFP) process: reaching out to multiple suppliers, gathering quotes, comparing them, and narrowing down the best options. This process could potentially drive 20% material cost savings.
- **Operational Blind Spots:** Accounting (AP/AR) is a major area ripe for automation. Implementing an AI Accounts Receivable agent is critical, as many contractors struggle with manually updating credit card information and managing collections.

## Tools & Resources
- **Avoca:** Mentioned as a tool powering call center dispatch and speed-to-lead capabilities for home service businesses.
- **Claude/OpenAI/Perplexity:** The underlying AI platforms used to build custom, integrated automation (e.g., Claude coding a personalized outreach system that integrates with Gmail and Google Maps).
- **AI Agents:** The concept of multi-agent systems (e.g., a product team using eight AI software engineer agents) that collaborate to develop products, simulating an "AI scrum master."

- [ ] **Build an Agentic RFP Workflow:** Develop a system that automatically conducts Requests for Proposals (RFPs) for parts. This involves having the AI reach out to 10+ suppliers, collecting quotes, and comparing them to identify the best pricing and materials.
- [ ] **Implement Predictive Marketing:** Use historical data and external factors (like weather patterns) to build a continuous, autonomous marketing workflow that predicts demand and triggers targeted outreach to existing customer bases for maintenance jobs.
