# How We Built a 30+ Person Offshore Team (And Why It Changed Everything) — Owned and Operated

## Summary
The episode centers on the strategic use of offshore talent as a scalable growth lever for modern businesses, arguing that it has fundamentally changed how companies can operate and scale in today's market. The discussion highlights that while many small operators are learning about offshoring, major corporations (including Amazon and Google) already utilize this model across various functions.

The guest provides a critical framework—a 2x2 matrix—to determine *what* processes should be outsourced versus kept in-house. He advises against outsourcing high-judgment roles initially, instead focusing on low-judgment, high-process tasks. This approach allows businesses to rapidly fill talent gaps and scale operations faster than traditional hiring methods.

## Key Points
- **The Offshoring Advantage:** Offshore staffing is described as "outrageously fast" for filling talent gaps (e.g., call centers, dispatch). The speed of deployment significantly outperforms local job postings.
- **The 2x2 Outsourcing Matrix:** This framework dictates where to allocate resources:
    *   **Low Judgment / High Process (Ideal Starting Point):** Tasks that are highly repeatable and can be condensed into Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs). Examples include inbound/outbound customer service, AR/AP processing, data entry, maintenance plan scheduling.
    *   **High Judgment / Low Process (Avoid Initially):** Roles requiring real-time judgment, local context, or high decision-making (e.g., General Managers, high-level sales). These should generally be kept in-house until processes are highly defined.
- **The Filtering Challenge:** When hiring offshore, the challenge is not finding candidates, but filtering a massive volume of applications. Effective systems require advanced screening methods, such as mandatory voice recordings reading specific scripts or answering unique questions, to condense hundreds of applicants into a manageable pool (e.g., 100 down to 15).
- **Business Necessity:** The initial push into offshore talent was driven by pain points, specifically the inability to hire local call center staff during periods like COVID-19, proving its immediate utility and necessity for business continuity.

## Tools & Resources

- **CFO Made Easy (Tyler):** Financial consulting service specializing in helping home service owners understand their financials to drive decisions on pricing, labor, overhead, and cash flow.
- **2x2 Matrix Framework:** The conceptual model for determining outsourcing suitability (Judgment vs. Process).

## Action Items
- [ ] **Audit Processes:** Map out your current operational tasks and categorize them using the 2x2 matrix (Judgment vs. Process) to identify immediate candidates for outsourcing.
- [ ] **Develop SOPs:** For all identified "Low Judgment, High Process" tasks, create detailed Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs). The quality of the process documentation is more important than the initial hiring effort.
- [ ] **Implement Advanced Filtering:** If considering offshore talent, build mandatory screening tests that require candidates to submit voice recordings or answer unique situational questions to filter out low-quality applicants efficiently.
- [ ] **Financial Review:** Schedule a consultation with a financial partner (like CFO Made Easy) to ensure you have clear control over cash flow and profit margins, rather than just relying on bookkeepers/CPAs.
