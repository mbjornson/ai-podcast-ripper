# Google PPC Isn’t Dead — You’re Just Running It Wrong — Owned and Operated

## Summary
The episode tackles the often contentious topic of Google Ads and Pay Per Click (PPC) marketing for home service businesses, arguing that while PPC is highly effective, its complexity means it cannot be run by novices. The discussion highlights a common industry pitfall: dismissing successful lead sources (like PPC or direct mail) because they didn't work in the past. Instead of discarding channels, the hosts emphasize asking, "How do I get this to work for me?"

The core message is that professional-grade PPC requires deep control and systematic optimization—far beyond what basic local service ads (LSA) offer. The guest emphasizes that successful campaigns are data-intensive, requiring continuous testing of ad copy, keywords, negative terms, and landing pages. Ultimately, the episode serves as a warning: because PPC is so powerful and complex, it should be treated as an externalized function managed by experts to ensure maximum return on investment (ROI).

## Key Points
- **PPC Offers Superior Control Over LSA:** Unlike Location Service Ads (LSA), which are limited by predefined location and service structures, PPC gives the user granular control over every element: ad copy, search terms, landing page content, and specific offers.
- **Data Integration is Critical for Optimization:** The most advanced campaigns connect paid advertising data directly to CRM systems (like ServiceTitan). This allows businesses to move beyond simple lead tracking and prove which keywords are actually driving *revenue*, not just calls or leads.
- **The Danger of Dismissing Lead Sources:** Do not dismiss a marketing channel (be it direct mail, Nextdoor, or PPC) simply because you have had bad experiences. The focus should be on understanding the mechanics: "How is someone else winning with this source?"
- **PPC Requires Professional Management:** Due to its complexity and the sheer number of variables involved, the hosts strongly advise against attempting advanced PPC campaigns internally (DIY). It is a specialized game that requires continuous testing and optimization.
- **The Power of Negative Keywords:** Successful accounts constantly audit their keywords, not just for positive terms, but also for negative keywords—terms that trigger ads but are irrelevant to the business—to prevent wasted budget.

## Tools & Resources
- **Google Ads/PPC:** The primary tool discussed; requires expert management for optimal results.
- **CRM Systems (e.g., ServiceTitan):** Essential for integrating paid ad data with actual revenue tracking, allowing businesses to calculate true keyword ROI.

## Action Items
- [ ] **Audit Your Lead Sources:** Instead of dismissing a marketing channel (e.g., direct mail, Nextdoor), dedicate time to researching how successful competitors in your niche are utilizing it.
- [ ] **Evaluate Data Integration:** If you currently use paid ads, assess whether your ad spend data is being linked back to your CRM system to prove actual revenue generated per keyword/campaign.
- [ ] **Consider Outsourcing PPC Management:** Given the complexity and high potential for waste, treat advanced Google Ads management as a specialized service rather than an internal department function.
- [ ] **Focus on Testing:** If managing ads yourself or with an agency, prioritize continuous testing of ad copy, landing page variations, and negative keywords to maximize efficiency.
