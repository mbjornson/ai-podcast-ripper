# How We Doubled a Plumbing Business in 90 Days | Cash Flow, Sales & Buying Businesses Explained — Owned and Operated

## Summary
This podcast episode, “How We Doubled a Plumbing Business in 90 Days,” features John Wilson, CEO of a $40 million home service company, sharing a straightforward strategy for rapid growth. The core message is that significant results can be achieved by focusing on foundational business practices rather than radical overhauls. Wilson emphasizes a lead-driven marketing approach, rapid lead response technology, 24/7 call coverage, strategic pricing adjustments, consistent technician training, a tweaked commission structure, and removing administrative burdens. The episode’s credibility stems from Wilson’s direct experience scaling a successful home service business, offering a pragmatic, results-oriented approach. It’s a practical guide for business owners looking to accelerate growth without complex transformations.

## Key Points
- **Focus on Fundamentals:** The key to doubling a business quickly isn't revolutionary strategy, but consistent execution of basic business principles – lead generation, rapid lead response, efficient operations, and competitive pricing.
- **Lead-Driven Marketing:** Wilson advocates for a lead-driven marketing approach, prioritizing channels like Google, Angie, and direct outreach over broad branding campaigns. This emphasizes immediate action and filling the sales pipeline.
- **Speed to Lead Technology is Crucial:** Implementing technology to bridge the gap between lead acquisition and contact is paramount.  A delay of even a few minutes can result in lost leads.
- **24/7 Call Coverage:**  Immediate response to calls is critical, highlighting the importance of adequate staffing and potentially leveraging AI call center solutions.
- **Strategic Pricing:**  A 15% price adjustment, based on market research, can significantly boost revenue without impacting customer retention.
- **Consistent Technician Training:**  Reinforcing core sales and customer service skills through structured training is a key component of scaling the team’s effectiveness.
- **Commission Structure Optimization:**  Adjusting compensation to be more competitive can incentivize technicians and drive sales.
- **Removing Admin Burden:**  Delegating administrative tasks allows the core team to focus on revenue-generating activities.
