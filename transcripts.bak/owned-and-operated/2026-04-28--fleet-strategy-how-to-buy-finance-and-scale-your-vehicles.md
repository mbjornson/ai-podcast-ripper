# Fleet Strategy: How to Buy, Finance, and Scale Your Vehicles — Owned and Operated

## Summary
This episode provides a deep dive into advanced fleet strategy for service businesses, moving beyond conventional methods of buying or leasing vehicles. The discussion centers on how scaling a fleet—a major expense—can be done in a way that supports rapid business growth without crippling the balance sheet. The speakers, who operate scaled service businesses, emphasize that the financing structure is more critical than the purchase method itself.

The core argument revolves around utilizing sophisticated financial tools, such as commercial lines of credit (LOCs) and understanding the nuances between different lease types. They detail how these methods allow businesses to acquire large numbers of vehicles quickly (e.g., using a $0.5 million LOC to buy 20 vehicles), providing the necessary runway for growth that traditional individual loans or small-scale leasing programs cannot match.

## Key Points
- **The Power of Lines of Credit (LOCs):** LOCs provide massive, flexible capital for fleet expansion. The speakers noted that a $500,000 LOC was sufficient to purchase approximately 20 vehicles, demonstrating a high capital efficiency for scaling.
- **Capital Lease vs. Operating Lease (Crucial Distinction):**
    - **Operating Lease:** Should be avoided for business assets. It is a fixed liability that does not build equity and negatively impacts business valuation (like renting a printer).
    - **Capital Lease:** Functions essentially as a loan. It increases the equity within the fleet and is accounted for on the balance sheet as a depreciating asset/loan, thus supporting business valuation.
- **The Goal of Financing:** When acquiring any vehicle, the primary consideration must be: "How does this add to the business value, and does the financing structure impact or not impact your valuation?"
- **Scaling Threshold:** The speakers suggest that to access the most robust financing options (like large fleet LOCs), a business needs to reach a certain scale (e.g., needing to be in the $2M-$3M top-line category to access a $500k-$600k LOC).
- **Marketing Signal:** For service businesses, the most critical marketing metric is not clicks or impressions, but "driving real high quality calls and book jobs."

## Tools & Resources
- **Ford Line of Credit:** Highlighted as a highly effective, flexible financing tool for commercial fleet expansion.
- **Service Scalers:** A marketing agency specializing in home service companies, focusing on driving high-quality calls and booked jobs.
- **Financial Advisors/Accountants:** Essential for understanding the complex accounting rules surrounding capital leases and LOCs (especially regarding GAAP/IFRS changes).
