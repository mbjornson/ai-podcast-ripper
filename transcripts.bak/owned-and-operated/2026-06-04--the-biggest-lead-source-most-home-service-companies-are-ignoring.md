# The Biggest Lead Source Most Home Service Companies Are Ignoring — Owned and Operated

## Summary
The episode features a discussion with Katie Leitsey, Service Operations Lead at Yelp, focusing on how the platform has evolved from a simple search directory into an advanced lead generation and service ecosystem for home service businesses. The conversation highlights Yelp's strategic pivot to address modern consumer behavior, particularly the increasing reliance on AI and complex digital research tools.

Katie details Yelp’s investments in technology, including "Request a Quote" and the new AI-powered "Yelp Assistant." These tools aim to move beyond basic search results by actively matching consumers with appropriate service professionals based on detailed needs. The discussion also addresses the massive scale of leads—claiming 10 million monthly leads nationwide—and how businesses can leverage Yelp's data visibility, especially as major AI platforms like ChatGPT and Gemini are increasingly citing Yelp for home service discovery.

## Key Points
- **The Value of Undecided Buyers:** The core benefit of an audience coming through Yelp is that they are "undecided buyers." They don't know exactly who to call or what solution they need, so they use the platform to narrow down their choices, making them highly receptive to vetted local services.
- **AI Integration as a Lead Funnel:** Yelp is strategically positioning itself as a primary data source for AI models (ChatGPT, Gemini). The platform has direct data integrations with OpenAI and Perplexity, meaning that managing an updated and comprehensive listing on Yelp helps businesses reach consumers even when they are searching through AI interfaces rather than traditional search engines.
- **Platform Evolution from Search to Matching:** Yelp has transitioned from a passive "search-driven platform" (where users had to sift through lists) to an active matching service using tools like Request a Quote, which allows for detailed consumer input and smarter pro matching.
- **Lead Cost vs. Value:** The volume of leads is cited as 10 million nationwide monthly in the home services space. The key financial takeaway is that even one high-ticket lead can far outweigh the cost of running continuous paid ads.
- **Software Fatigue Warning (Sponsor Insight):** For owner-operators, complex enterprise software often becomes a "full-time job" to manage. Simplicity and ease of rollout are critical for operational efficiency.

## Tools & Resources
- **Yelp:** The primary platform discussed; key features include Request a Quote and Yelp Assistant (AI tool).
- **Field Pulse:** Mentioned as an alternative business management software designed specifically for owner-operators, focusing on simplicity over complex dashboards.
- **Major AI Platforms:** ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity—These platforms are cited by Yelp as sources of data that businesses must optimize for.
