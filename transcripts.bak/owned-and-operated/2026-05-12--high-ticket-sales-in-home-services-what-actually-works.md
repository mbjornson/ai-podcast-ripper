# High-Ticket Sales in Home Services: What Actually Works — Owned and Operated

## Summary
The episode centers on fundamentally redefining sales within the home services industry, arguing that high-ticket sales are achieved not through aggressive "pushiness," but through expert consultation and presenting comprehensive options. The core thesis is that a home service company must operate as a marketing and sales business first, and a plumbing/HVAC business second. The discussion emphasizes that the goal is to guide the customer toward the *best possible outcome* for their property, which often involves recommending a full replacement or a systemic upgrade rather than merely performing a quick, expensive repair.

The speakers stress that the biggest hurdle is cultural: the industry often views sales as inherently manipulative or "bad." They propose a systemic shift where technicians are trained to educate the customer on their options, thereby building trust and positioning the company as a trusted advisor rather than just a repair service. Furthermore, the discussion highlights the critical role of reputation management and digital presence (specifically Google Business Profiles) in generating high-quality, scalable leads.

## Key Points
- **The Options Framework:** High-ticket sales are driven by presenting choices, not dictating solutions. Instead of saying, "You must replace this," the approach must be, "You have three options: 1) A minor repair for $X, 2) A preventative maintenance plan for $Y, or 3) A full replacement for $Z, which offers the best long-term value."
- **The Three Core Sales Questions:** Any effective sales process in home services must solve these three questions for the customer: 1) Why (is this needed?), 2) Why now (is there urgency?), and 3) How can I afford you (is there a payment plan or alternative)?
- **Sales is a Core Competency, Not an Afterthought:** The business model must be understood as a marketing and sales operation that *happens* to perform trades (plumbing, electrical). Failing to treat it as a sales business limits revenue potential.
- **Ethical Transparency:** The most effective sales tactics are those built on radical transparency. Technicians must educate the customer on the full scope of their options, even if those options are cheaper or require less work than the company's preferred solution. Failing to present alternatives is considered deceptive.
- **Reputation as a Lead Machine:** Google Business Profiles (GBP) are critical assets. By implementing automated review generation, fast response protocols, and location health monitoring, a company can make its reputation "compound over time," generating leads without increasing manual effort.

## Tools & Resources

- **GBP (Google Business Profiles):** The primary digital asset for local lead generation and reputation building.
