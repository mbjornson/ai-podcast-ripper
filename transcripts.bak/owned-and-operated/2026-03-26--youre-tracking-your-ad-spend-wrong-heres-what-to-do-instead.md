# You're Tracking Your Ad Spend Wrong (Here’s What to Do Instead) — Owned and Operated

## Summary
The episode dives deep into the complex topic of marketing attribution, specifically addressing the common pitfall of relying solely on "last-click" data. The discussion argues that attributing success based only on the final touchpoint (e.g., the last Google search) severely misrepresents the true return on ad spend (ROAS) and the value of brand-building channels. The guest, Tony Castucci, specializes in traditional marketing for home service companies, giving him unique insight into channels like billboards, TV, and truck wraps—methods that are difficult to tie directly to a single lead.

The core message is that modern attribution requires moving beyond simple digital metrics. Instead of relying on a single channel, businesses must adopt a "blended" attribution model. This approach integrates data from multiple sources—including CRM records, Google Analytics, and historical benchmarking—to build a holistic picture of the customer journey, proving the value of brand awareness and sustained marketing efforts.

## Key Points
- **The Fallacy of Last-Click Attribution:** Relying on the last click (e.g., PPC) is highly misleading because it gives credit to the final touchpoint, even if the customer was already in the market due to prior brand exposure (e.g., seeing a billboard or TV ad).
- **Focus on ROAS/ROI, Not CPL:** Cost Per Lead (CPL) is an insufficient metric because it doesn't account for the quality of the lead or the overall profitability. The focus must shift to Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) and overall Return on Investment (ROI).
- **The Power of Branded Traffic:** High organic search volume for your company name (branded search) is strong evidence of prior marketing success (TV, radio, etc.), proving that the initial investment in brand building is working, even if the click was paid.
- **Blended Attribution Model:** The most accurate method is combining data from multiple sources: CRM data (tracking the full customer lifecycle), Google Analytics (website behavior), and historical benchmarking (knowing your baseline conversion rates *before* a new channel is introduced).
- **Traditional Marketing Value:** Channels like broadcast TV and billboards are critical for building brand awareness, even if their direct ROI is hard to measure. They set the stage for the digital conversion.

## Action Items
- [ ] **Audit Your Attribution Model:** Stop relying solely on last-click data. Implement a "blended" attribution model that incorporates CRM data and historical benchmarking alongside digital metrics.
- [ ] **Track Branded Search:** Monitor your organic search volume for your company name. High branded search volume indicates successful top-of-funnel brand building (TV, billboards, etc.).
- [ ] **Shift Key Metrics:** When evaluating marketing channels, prioritize ROAS and overall conversion rates over simple Cost Per Lead (CPL).
- [ ] **Review Tech Stack:** Investigate AI-powered lead management tools (like Avoca) that specialize in your industry and integrate deeply with your core business software (like ServiceTitan) to ensure no leads are dropped.
