# The Real Reason Everyone in the Trades Is Starting a Podcast — Owned and Operated

## Summary
The episode analyzes the massive shift in content creation within the trades industry, arguing that podcasting, newsletters, and digital content are no longer optional but essential for modern business growth. The discussion centers on how content serves as a powerful vehicle for building authority, generating leads, and negotiating better vendor terms, rather than being a direct pipeline generator.

The guest, Jackie Aubell, is associated with ServiceTitan and hosts "Tools of the Trade," a podcast that shares tips and tactics from successful contractors. Aubell's background includes running a comedy podcast and working within the tech side of the service industry, giving her deep insight into the mechanics of content scaling. She details how ServiceTitan leveraged content to modernize the traditionally slow-adopting trades sector, transforming the company's website into a 24/7 sales and qualification engine.

## Key Points
- **Content as a Vehicle, Not a Pipeline:** Podcasting should be viewed as a content generation machine and a networking tool to disseminate your message, not a guaranteed direct source of revenue.
- **Transparency is the New Baseline:** Homeowners and customers now expect to see pricing and understand their options *before* they even call, forcing trades businesses to modernize their sales process.
- **The Website as a Sales Engine:** To capture demand that would otherwise "bounce," a website must be optimized to guide the customer through a sales experience, allowing them to explore options and see price ranges before engaging with a representative.
- **The Tech Adoption Curve Has Flipped:** The historical narrative that the trades are technologically behind is outdated. The industry is now at the forefront of adopting advanced technologies, including AI and LLMs, in its business operations.
- **The Ecosystem is Maturing:** The industry is moving toward a full content ecosystem, with multiple specialized shows (e.g., "Home Pros" for news, "Owned and Operated" for top industry leaders) building on each other for maximum reach.

## Tools & Resources

- [ ] **Content Strategy:** Commit to creating content (podcast, newsletter, video) with the primary goal of building authority and visibility, rather than immediate sales conversion.
