# How to Hire a GM or Operator for Your Home Service Business — Owned and Operated

## Summary
The episode provides a deep dive into the strategic art of hiring high-level operators and General Managers (GMs) for scaling home service businesses, drawing heavily on the experience of recruiting military veterans. The discussion emphasizes that successful leadership recruitment is less about technical skills and more about process management, cultural fit, and understanding complex human motivations. John Wilson, who runs a $40 million plumbing/HVAC company across multiple states, frames the conversation around scaling operations efficiently.

The guest, Brendan Aronson of TMV (Military Vet), details sophisticated hiring strategies that move beyond simple job postings. A core theme is the value of *passive talent*—highly skilled professionals who are currently happy in their roles but can be convinced to move. The discussion also pivots to modern operational efficiency, providing a strong case for specialized AI tools designed specifically for contracting and home service industries to handle high-volume lead management and dispatching.

## Key Points
- **Hiring Funnel Depth:** For high-stakes roles (GM, CFO), the process should be robust; the speakers suggest talking to *dozens* of candidates, with a minimum of **seven** interviews required before making a final decision.
- **Passive Talent Acquisition:** The most valuable talent is often passive—individuals who are currently employed and not actively job hunting. Successful recruiting requires "prying them out where they are," which necessitates building trust and demonstrating genuine value beyond just the salary.
- **The Two-Way Street of Hiring:** The interview process must allow the candidate to evaluate *your* company as much as you evaluate them. Poor communication (e.g., ghosting candidates or delayed responses) signals operational chaos, regardless of the role's importance.
- **Understanding Candidate Motivation:** Before interviewing, a manager must accurately suss out what drives the candidate: Is it compensation, location/family proximity, equity/long-term incentives, or personal passions (e.g., skiing)? Ignoring this can lead to poor retention.
- **Operational AI Integration:** For home service businesses, specialized AI solutions like Avoca are recommended because they understand industry workflows. They handle inbound calls, outbound follow-ups, texts, web leads, and dispatching *within one system*, preventing the need for "duct taping five tools together."
- **Measurable ROI from Tech:** Implementing proper operational tech can yield tangible revenue increases; owners using specialized AI are seeing hold times disappear and booking rates jump by **more than 30%**.

## Tools & Resources
- **Avoca:** An AI partner specifically designed for growing home service companies. It handles the full spectrum of lead management (calls, texts, web leads) and integrates deeply with existing systems like ServiceTitan.
- **ServiceTitan:** Mentioned as a key industry platform; integration compatibility is crucial when selecting operational tech to avoid workflow breaks during busy seasons.

- [ ] **Develop a "Why Us" Pitch:** Craft a compelling narrative that sells the opportunity, focusing on long-term growth potential and culture, rather than just listing job duties.
- [ ] **Map Candidate Motivations:** Before starting interviews, define the top 3 non-salary motivators (e.g., flexibility, equity stake, location) to better tailor your pitch.
- [ ] **Evaluate Operational Tech Stack:** Research specialized AI platforms (like Avoca) that integrate deeply with existing industry software (ServiceTitan) rather than using general-purpose tools.
- [ ] **Focus on Process Building vs. Running:** When interviewing candidates, assess their comfort zone: are they better at building a repeatable process from scratch, or optimizing an already established one?
