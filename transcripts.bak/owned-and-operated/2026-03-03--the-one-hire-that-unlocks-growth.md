# The One Hire That Unlocks Growth — Owned and Operated

## Summary
The episode centers on the critical topic of identifying and hiring "operators"—leaders who are actively running a company day-to-day—rather than passive investors or CEOs. The guest, Brendan Aronson, founder of TMV (Military Vet), argues that military veterans possess unique and highly valuable leadership skills that translate directly to private sector success. These skills include robust decision-making under extreme pressure, leading diverse teams in challenging environments, and mastering complex technical systems.

Aronson provides deep insight into the nuances of military service, explaining that both enlisted personnel (Non-Commissioned Officers) and officers develop distinct, high-level leadership capacities. The discussion emphasizes that a veteran's value lies not just in their experience, but in their ability to adapt their disciplined structure and problem-solving skills to civilian business needs, making them ideal candidates for scaling businesses.

The conversation also touches on general business growth strategies, noting the importance of professional digital reputation management (Google Business Profiles) as a modern lead engine, demonstrating that operational excellence applies both internally (company leadership) and externally (marketing/sales).

## Key Points
- **Defining "Operator":** The most critical distinction in company leadership is between an active "operator" (GM/President who runs the business) and a passive investor or CEO. Hiring for operator capacity is key to scaling growth.
- **Veteran Leadership Value:** Veterans bring proven abilities in leading diverse workforces, maintaining performance in austere environments, and executing complex tasks under high stress—skills often superior to those developed solely in the private sector.
- **Military Skill Translation:** The skills are highly transferable: Submariners running nuclear power plants translate to managing mission-critical infrastructure; Special Operations personnel demonstrate adaptability and extreme problem-solving capability.
- **Digital Reputation Management (Signal):** For local service businesses, maintaining a robust Google Business Profile is crucial for lead generation. Using specialized tools can automate review management and response, leading to measurable increases in call volume (e.g., 27% increase in calls within 30 days).

## Tools & Resources
- **TMV (Military Vet):** A recruiting firm specializing in placing military veterans into leadership roles.
- **Big Reputation.ai:** A tool designed to manage and optimize Google Business Profiles, turning them into a dependable lead engine without requiring constant manual effort.
- **The Military Veteran Startup Conference:** An event that hosts networking and educational opportunities for veterans transitioning into entrepreneurship/business ownership.
