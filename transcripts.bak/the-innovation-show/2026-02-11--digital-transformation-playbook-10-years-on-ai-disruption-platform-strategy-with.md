# Digital Transformation Playbook (10 Years  On) AI, Disruption & Platform Strategy with David Rogers — The Innovation Show

## Summary
David Rogers, recognized as a leading expert and "OG" of Digital Transformation (DX), is a faculty member at Columbia Business School and author of the landmark bestseller, *Digital Transformation Playbook*. The episode reviews the impact of his work over the last decade, arguing that DX is fundamentally a strategic challenge involving leadership and new ways of thinking, not merely a technology upgrade. Rogers reframes the entire concept of digital change, arguing that the industry often treats DX as a single, finite project—a "new plateau"—when in reality, it is a continuous, accelerating series of successive waves of technological disruption.

The discussion emphasizes that the current focus on AI is simply the latest wave building upon decades of change (Internet, mobile computing, cloud, IoT). The true difficulty, according to Rogers, is not adopting the technology itself, but the organizational challenge of transformation: redesigning the fundamental boundaries and structures of the business to accommodate the next wave of disruption.

## Key Points
- **DX is a Continuous Process, Not a Project:** The misconception that companies can complete "the transformation" and then be set is incorrect. History shows a successive, accelerating wave of digital change (Internet $\rightarrow$ Social Media $\rightarrow$ Mobile Computing $\rightarrow$ Cloud $\rightarrow$ IoT $\rightarrow$ AI).
- **The Core Challenge is Organizational Redesign:** The most important technology today is not AI itself, but the *ability to transform*. Companies must redesign their organizational structures and rethink the very definition of their business model to survive the next wave.
- **AI is the Latest Wave, Not the First:** While AI is the biggest current disruption, it is not a sudden event. The field of AI has been studied for 70 years, with the first major practical breakthroughs occurring over a decade ago (neural networks). The current wave is made accessible to everyone via consumer-facing tools.
- **The Danger of Myopia:** Many companies fall into the trap of looking at the business of "today" or "yesterday," leading to myopic strategies. The classic failure stories (like Kodak or Blockbuster) are examples of this failure to look ahead.
- **The Power of Platforms and Coopetition:** Rogers highlights frameworks like platform business models and "coopetition" (cooperation + competition) as essential strategies for navigating modern markets.

## Tools & Resources
- **Books:** *Digital Transformation Playbook* (David Rogers)
- **Frameworks:** Platform business models, Coopetition, Updated theory of disruption (updating Clayton Christensen's work).
- **Institution:** Kyndryl Institute (Mentioned for resources and learning).

## Action Items
- [ ] **Mindset Shift:** Stop viewing digital transformation as a finite project with a defined end date. Adopt the mindset of continuous, accelerating adaptation.
- [ ] **Strategic Focus:** When evaluating business strategy, shift the focus from "What technology should we buy?" to "How must we redesign our organizational structure to handle the next wave of disruption?"
- [ ] **Skill Development:** Study the principles of **Platform Business Models** and **Coopetition** to build adaptable, multi-stakeholder revenue streams.
- [ ] **Reading:** Review David Rogers' work, particularly the concepts surrounding the updated theory of disruption, to understand systemic change.
- [ ] **Career Move:** Seek out roles or projects that require cross-functional leadership, as the challenge is fundamentally one of *leadership* and *strategy*, not just technical execution.
