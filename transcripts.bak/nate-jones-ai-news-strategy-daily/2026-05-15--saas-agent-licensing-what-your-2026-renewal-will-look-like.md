# SaaS Agent Licensing: What Your 2026 Renewal Will Look Like — Nate Jones AI News & Strategy Daily

## Summary
This episode of Nate Jones AI News & Strategy Daily focuses on the evolving pricing models for SaaS agent licensing, particularly in the context of rapidly increasing AI adoption. The guest, Nate Jones, argues that the traditional “seats” model for SaaS is becoming obsolete as AI agents increasingly automate workflows. Salesforce’s AgentForce data highlights this shift, moving from counting “tokens” to measuring completed “work units.” The core message is that companies need to proactively understand and negotiate the costs associated with agentic workflows, as vendors will increasingly seek to monetize access and usage. The episode stresses the importance of questioning pricing structures, understanding the unit of billing (tokens vs. work units), and anticipating the scale of agentic deployments.

## Key Points
- **Shift to Work Units:** Salesforce’s AgentForce data demonstrates a move from token-based billing to measuring completed “work units,” reflecting the automation of AI agents. This is a fundamental change in how value is assessed.
- **Vendor Tollbooths:** Vendors are increasingly implementing “toll booths” to capture value created by agents, leading to potentially higher costs beyond model usage fees.
- **Pricing Complexity:** The rise of AI agents is creating a complex pricing landscape, particularly for Microsoft’s Copilot and similar tools, where costs can scale significantly based on usage (e.g., premium reasoning credits).
- **End of the Seat Model:** The traditional SaaS model of charging based on the number of users is becoming irrelevant as AI agents can perform tasks without human intervention.
- **Cost Awareness is Crucial:** Companies need to be acutely aware of the costs associated with agentic workflows, including token usage, workflow execution, and potential API policy restrictions.
- **Negotiation is Key:** Proactive negotiation of agent access and pricing is essential before deployments to avoid being locked into unfavorable contracts. Waiting until usage is embedded significantly reduces leverage.
- **Operation Taxonomy & Cost Dashboard:** The need for a detailed operation taxonomy and a cost dashboard to manage agentic workflows and optimize for cost efficiency.
