# Marketing for Humans and AI Agents in 2026 — Nate Jones AI News & Strategy Daily

## Summary
This episode centers around a significant shift in the internet economy – a move from an “attention economy” to an “interpretation economy.” Nate Jones argues that AI agents, like ChatGPT, are increasingly becoming the gatekeepers of information and influence, and individuals and marketers need to adapt. The core idea is that people will soon be asking AI for recommendations and trust assessments, rather than relying on traditional marketing channels. The guest emphasizes that marketers need to build “truth layers” – structured, verifiable data about their products – to enable AI agents to accurately interpret and understand their offerings. This isn’t just about technical SEO; it’s a fundamental change in how value is created and delivered online.

## Key Points
- **Shift from Attention to Interpretation:** The internet has historically been built on capturing attention (Google’s model), but we’re moving towards an economy where AI interprets information and makes decisions based on that interpretation.
- **AI as the New Gatekeeper:** Individuals will increasingly be asking AI for trust assessments (“Should I trust this brand?”) rather than relying on traditional marketing signals.
- **Truth Layers are Crucial:** Marketers must build “truth layers” – detailed, structured data about their products – to provide AI agents with the information they need to make informed decisions and recommendations. Without this, brands will be flattened into the average for their category.
- **Marketers Need to Understand AI Intent:** Marketers need to focus on how their products can answer the questions that AI agents will ask, not just on generating attention.
- **Back-Office Automation is Table Stakes:** Simply automating marketing tasks with tools like ChatGPT is no longer sufficient; marketers need to think strategically about how their products fit into the interpretation economy.
- **Prove Your AI Skills:** Individuals, particularly in the AI talent market, need to demonstrate verifiable AI skills (e.g., MLOps pipeline development) to be considered credible.

## Tools & Resources
- **Claude & ChatGPT:** Mentioned as examples of AI agents used for shopping and information gathering.
- **Co-work:** A tool mentioned for automating marketing tasks with ChatGPT.
- **Talent Board Project:** A personal project created by Nate Jones to vet and demonstrate AI skills for individuals seeking roles in the AI economy (https://www.talentboardproject.com/).
- **JSON Schema:** A data format that agents can readily ingest and understand.

> “You see the internet economy is changing from I need to get attention I need to shout I need to get people to pay attention to me to sell my product to sell myself as someone who can work right to have a resume to get out there on LinkedIn whatever all of that was an attention economy now” – This highlights the fundamental shift in the core value proposition.
> “If you're not opinionated Then you are going to be flattened into the internet average for your category.” – This is a critical takeaway: brands need to be specific and authoritative to stand out in an AI-driven world.

## Action Items
- **Develop a Truth Layer:** For your business or product, start documenting the key features, benefits, and data points that an AI agent would need to understand.
- **Understand AI Intent:**  Think about the questions people (and AI agents) will ask about your product and proactively provide answers.
- **Invest in Technical Skills:**  If you’re a marketer, start learning about MLOps, data engineering, and how AI agents work.
- **Build a Portfolio of AI Projects:**  The Talent Board Project is a good starting point for individuals to showcase their AI skills.
- **Follow Nate Jones:**  Subscribe to his podcast for ongoing insights and updates on the interpretation economy.
