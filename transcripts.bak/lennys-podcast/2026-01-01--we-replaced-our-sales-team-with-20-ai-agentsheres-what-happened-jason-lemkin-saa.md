# We replaced our sales team with 20 AI agents—here’s what happened | Jason Lemkin (SaaStr) — Lenny's Podcast

## Summary
The episode features Jason Lemkin, founder and CEO of SaaStr, a highly credible figure in the B2B founder and community space, and a deep expert in go-to-market strategy. Lemkin's core thesis is that AI is fundamentally disrupting the sales and GTM function, making human labor redundant for many routine, mid-tier tasks. He details how SaaStr replaced its human sales team (SDRs and AEs) with 20 specialized AI agents, achieving comparable performance while gaining massive scalability and consistency.

Lemkin argues that the traditional sales structure—relying on junior SDRs for basic lead qualification and outreach—is becoming obsolete. The shift is not just about efficiency, but about the ability to scale operations without the high cost, unpredictability, or turnover associated with human employees. He positions AI as the necessary evolution for any company aiming to operate at the bleeding edge of modern business.

## Key Points
- **AI Agents are Replacing Mid-Tier Human Labor:** AI is specifically displacing the "mid-pack and the mediocre" in sales. Roles like junior SDRs (who send basic emails) and lead qualification specialists are predicted to become "extinct next year."
- **The Scale Advantage:** SaaStr replaced 10 human GTM desks with 20 AI agents (e.g., Repli, Quali, Arti). The key benefit is that while net productivity might not be *better* than the human team, the process is vastly more *efficient* and *scalable* because software scales.
- **The Cost of Human Failure:** A major catalyst for the shift was the high cost and risk associated with human turnover. Lemkin noted that he could not afford to pay a junior SDR $150,000 a year only to have them quit. This financial risk pushed the company to adopt AI.
- **AI Requires Training, Not Just Deployment:** AI agents do not work out of the box. Success requires taking the best human scripts and processes and training the agents on them to make them function as a "version of your best salesperson."
- **The Future of Sales Roles:** Future sales professionals will not be focused on basic lead qualification. Instead, they will be managing and optimizing the AI agents, requiring a higher level of strategic oversight.

## Tools & Resources
- **SaaStr:** The platform/community for B2B founders, generating eight figures of annual revenue through sponsorships and tickets.
- **AI Agents (Internal):** Repli (for Replit), Quali (for Qualified), Arti (for Artisan). These are examples of specialized AI agents deployed in GTM.
- **DX:** A developer intelligence platform that provides data and insights on how AI is providing value to developers in large companies (Dropbox, Booking.com, etc.).
- **VZero (Vercel):** A web development assistant that allows non-technical people (PMs, designers) to build full-stack applications and prototypes without writing code.

## Action Items
- [ ] **Career/Skill Development:** Focus on developing skills that manage, optimize, and train AI systems, rather than performing routine tasks. Become proficient in prompt engineering and workflow automation.
- [ ] **Business Strategy:** Audit your current GTM/Sales process. Identify the 20% of tasks that are repetitive, low-complexity, and high-volume (e.g., initial lead qualification, basic follow-up emails). These are prime candidates for AI replacement.
- [ ] **Tool Adoption:** Experiment with specialized AI agents (like those used for lead qualification or content drafting) to replace the lowest-level human labor in your workflow.
- [ ] **Mindset Shift:** View AI not as a cost-cutting measure, but as a scalability multiplier. Focus on building systems that can operate 24/7 without human fatigue or turnover risk.
