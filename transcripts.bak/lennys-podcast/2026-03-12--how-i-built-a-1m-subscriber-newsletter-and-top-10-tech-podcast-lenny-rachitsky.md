# How I built a 1M+ subscriber newsletter and top 10 tech podcast | Lenny Rachitsky — Lenny's Podcast

## Summary
This episode features Lenny Rachitsky, the host of the wildly successful Lenny’s Podcast, discussing the journey of building his 1.2 million subscriber newsletter and top 10 tech podcast. The conversation is deeply personal, with Rachitsky sharing vulnerabilities and insights into his decision-making process. He emphasizes the importance of experience-driven advice, highlighting the value of practitioner knowledge over theoretical concepts. The episode’s core message revolves around the unexpected path of building a successful content business, driven by genuine curiosity and a willingness to experiment. Rachitsky’s credibility stems from his demonstrable success – a thriving newsletter and podcast – and his candid approach to sharing his struggles and learnings. The episode also incorporates product sponsorships, specifically for Work OS and MetaView, demonstrating Rachitsky’s entrepreneurial mindset and ability to leverage his audience.

## Key Points
- **Experience over Theory:** The most valuable advice comes from practitioners actually doing the thing, not just reading about it. Rachitsky’s success is built on sharing insights gleaned from his own experiences, both positive and negative.
- **The “Pull” of Content:** Rachitsky initially started writing on the internet simply because he felt a “pull” to share his learnings and experiences, regardless of potential financial gain. This intrinsic motivation is key to his sustained success.
- **The Lindy Effect:** The concept of the Lindy effect – that something’s likely to continue succeeding for as long as it has already succeeded – heavily influenced Rachitsky’s decision to continue with the newsletter after nine months.
- **Trigger Points for Success:** Several key moments contributed to the newsletter’s growth, including a positive initial response to a Medium post, advice from VC Lee Jacobs, launching a paywall, and the unexpected success of the paywall itself.
- **Startup Struggles:** Rachitsky openly admits that he would likely be stuck on the “struggle bus” of a failed startup if he hadn’t pursued the newsletter. This honest self-assessment adds to his relatability.
- **The Treadmill Effect:** Maintaining a consistent content schedule (newsletter and podcast) is described as a “treadmill,” requiring ongoing effort and commitment.
