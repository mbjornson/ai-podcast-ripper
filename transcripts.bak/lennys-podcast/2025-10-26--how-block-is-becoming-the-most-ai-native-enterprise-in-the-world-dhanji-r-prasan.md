# How Block is becoming the most AI-native enterprise in the world | Dhanji R. Prasanna — Lenny's Podcast

## Summary
The episode discusses the current state of AI adoption in large enterprises, moving beyond the hype cycle to focus on tangible, systemic productivity gains. The guest, Donji R. Prasanna, CTO at Block, provides a deep dive into how Block has become one of the world's most "AI-native" companies. Prasanna's background is highly relevant, having overseen a team of over 3,500 people and leading the transformation of Block's culture and structure to prioritize technology and AI.

The core thesis of the discussion is that the greatest gains from AI are not simply achieved by implementing new tools, but by making fundamental, often "boring," organizational and cultural changes. While Block's internal agent, "Goose," is cited for saving employees an average of 8 to 10 hours per week, Prasanna emphasizes that the shift in company DNA—from a financial services identity to a technology-first identity—was the true catalyst for this massive productivity boost.

## Key Points
- **AI Value is a Baseline, Not a Peak:** The current productivity gains from AI are not the maximum potential; they represent a new, higher baseline. Companies must continuously adapt to ride this changing value wave.
- **Non-Technical Users are the Biggest Beneficiaries:** The most significant impact from AI agents and programming tools is being seen by non-technical employees who are able to optimize their specific workflows and tasks, rather than solely by engineering teams.
- **The Power of the Functional Structure:** For large companies aiming to be deeply technology-focused, the organizational structure must shift from a General Manager (GM) model (where units operate as independent, siloed companies) to a functional structure. In this model, all engineers report to a single head of engineering, and all designers report to a single head of design.
- **Re-establishing Tech DNA:** A critical step in transformation is consciously shifting the company's self-perception and internal focus from being a "financial services company" (Fintech) to being a "technology company," even if the core business is finance.
- **LLMs Should Be Proactive:** Instead of being idle overnight or on weekends, LLMs should be designed to work constantly, anticipating user needs and building features in anticipation of future requirements.

## Tools & Resources

- **Figma Make:** A tool allowing users to turn ideas or designs into fully functional prototypes or apps using prompts, all within the Figma environment.
- **Lenny's Newsletter:** A resource offering deep dives into product and technology, with an annual subscription providing access to numerous tools (e.g., Replit, Superhuman, Linear, Perplexity).

## Action Items
- [ ] **Develop Functional Thinking:** When evaluating your current company structure, identify if your teams are siloed by product/business unit (GM model) or if they are organized by core function (engineering, design, data science). Advocate for a functional structure to deepen technical expertise.
- [ ] **Audit Your Workflow for AI Agents:** Identify 3-5 repetitive, low-leverage tasks in your current role (e.g., drafting initial PRDs, summarizing meeting notes, basic code scaffolding). Treat these as immediate candidates for automation via an internal agent or tool.
- [ ] **Shift Identity Focus:** If you lead a team in a non-tech industry (e.g., finance, logistics), lead an internal initiative to re-frame the company's core identity as a "technology enabler" rather than just a service provider.
- [ ] **Explore Low-Code/No-Code Prototyping:** Experiment with tools like Figma Make or similar platforms to rapidly prototype ideas without relying on full engineering cycles, validating product concepts faster.
