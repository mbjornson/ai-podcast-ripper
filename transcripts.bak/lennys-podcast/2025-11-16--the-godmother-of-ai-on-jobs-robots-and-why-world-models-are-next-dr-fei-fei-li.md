# The Godmother of AI on jobs, robots, and why world models are next | Dr. Fei-Fei Li — Lenny's Podcast

## Summary
Dr. Fei-Fei Li, recognized as the "Godmother of AI," provides a comprehensive look at the history, current state, and future trajectory of artificial intelligence. Her background is highly influential, including spearheading the creation of ImageNet—the foundational dataset that fueled the modern AI revolution—and serving as a director at Stanford’s AI lab and co-creator of the Human-Centered AI Institute.

Philosophically, Dr. Li is an AI optimist, but crucially, she is not a utopian. She frames AI as a "net positive for humanity," but emphasizes that technology is a double-edged sword. The ultimate impact of AI on jobs and society is not predetermined by the technology itself, but is entirely "up to us"—the people and the society.

Technically, the discussion highlights the shift toward "World Models." She explains that the breakthrough required moving beyond simple algorithms and providing machines with massive amounts of clean, labeled data. The current focus is on building these comprehensive world models, which represent a major leap in AI's ability to understand and predict complex real-world scenarios.

## Key Points
- **AI is a Humanist Endeavor:** The most critical takeaway is that AI is not inherently artificial. Dr. Li stresses that "there's nothing artificial about AI. It's inspired by people, it's created by people, and most importantly, it impacts." The focus must remain on human impact and responsibility.
- **The Breakthrough Was Data:** The AI revolution was not sparked by a single algorithm, but by the realization that AI needed vast amounts of clean, labeled data. The creation of ImageNet was the pivotal moment that pulled the field out of its "AI winter."
- **The New Normal is AI-Integrated:** The industry landscape has fundamentally changed. The historical fear of AI was inaccurate; today, "Every company is an AI company." Businesses must integrate AI into their core operations, not treat it as an optional add-on.
- **The Future is World Models:** The next major frontier is the "World Model." These models aim to give AI a comprehensive understanding of how the world works—how objects interact, how systems function, and how to predict outcomes—moving beyond simple pattern recognition.
- **Responsible Stewardship is Paramount:** For individuals and technologists, the most important action is to become "responsible individuals." This means actively caring about how AI is developed and deployed, ensuring that the technology serves human flourishing rather than simply maximizing efficiency.
