# Inside the expert network training every frontier AI model | Garrett Lord (Handshake CEO) — Lenny's Podcast

## Summary
The episode details a massive strategic shift occurring within the frontier AI industry, moving from general data ingestion to highly specialized model refinement. Garrett Lord, CEO of Handshake (LinkedIn for college students), explains that initial AI models focused on "pre-training"—sucking up the entire corpus of human knowledge available on the internet. However, as these models approached saturation in terms of raw data, the industry has pivoted toward "post-training." This new phase requires high-quality, domain-specific data to improve capabilities in areas like coding, law, or finance.

Handshake capitalized on this shift by recognizing that their proprietary network—comprising tens of thousands of PhDs and Master's students—represents an extremely valuable resource for creating and labeling this specialized training data. They successfully launched a new business line focused on data services, achieving rapid growth (hitting $50M ARR in four months) and demonstrating how to disrupt one’s own core business model by monetizing unique human capital access.

## Key Points
- **AI Model Training Shift: Pre-training vs. Post-training:** AI gains are moving from the pre-training phase (feeding the entire internet's knowledge base) to the post-training phase. The latter involves augmenting and improving models across specific, high-value domains (e.g., mathematics, law, finance).
- **The New Moat is Expertise:** As generalist AI models become highly capable, they no longer require basic data labeling from low-cost international labor. Instead, the demand has shifted to *experts*—individuals who can provide deep knowledge and context in specific, economically valuable capability areas.
- **Three Pillars of Post-Training Data:** The most significant gains are coming from three types of specialized data: 1) Reinforcement Learning with Human Feedback (RLHF), which involves preference ranking; 2) Step-by-step instruction/trajectory data (e.g., end-to-end accounting processes); and 3) Prompt-response pairs for reasoning models.
- **The Value of Proprietary Networks:** Handshake’s unique advantage is its hyper-targeted access to academic knowledge, particularly PhD candidates who have proven they can extend the world's knowledge in a specific domain—a resource that cannot be easily replicated by general data providers.
