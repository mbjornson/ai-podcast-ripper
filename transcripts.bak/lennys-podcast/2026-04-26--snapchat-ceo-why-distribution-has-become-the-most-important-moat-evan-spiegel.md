# Snapchat CEO: Why distribution has become the most important moat | Evan Spiegel — Lenny's Podcast

## Summary
This episode features an interview with Evan Spiegel, CEO of Snapchat, focusing on the critical importance of distribution in building a durable, lasting consumer product. Spiegel argues that the industry’s overemphasis on product market fit and technological innovation has led to a graveyard of failed social apps, highlighting that distribution is the true differentiator. He emphasizes a shift in perspective, arguing that humanity – specifically, how people adopt and engage with technology – is far more important than simply building a “good” product. The conversation touches on the challenges of launching new consumer products in a crowded market, the role of network effects, and the potential impact of AI on distribution strategies. Spiegel’s credibility stems from his successful scaling of Snapchat, a rare achievement in the notoriously difficult social media landscape.

## Key Points
- **Distribution is the New Moat:** Spiegel argues that technological innovation (like AI) will eventually be copied, but distribution remains a uniquely challenging barrier to entry. Building a strong distribution network is paramount for long-term success.
- **Human Adoption Matters More Than Technology:** The success of Snapchat hinged on understanding how people actually *use* technology – connecting with close friends – rather than simply creating a technically impressive product.
- **AI’s Impact on Distribution:** Spiegel anticipates that AI will likely excel at generating ideas and strategies, but will struggle with the core challenge of getting products in front of users – distribution. This suggests distribution will become the new “moat” to defend.
- **Network Effects are Crucial:** Early in Snapchat’s growth, the belief in network effects – the more users, the stickier the app – was a key driver of growth. Connecting users to their closest relationships was more valuable than simply having the largest network.
- **Copying is Inevitable:** Spiegel acknowledges the reality that ideas will be copied, and that building a durable business requires creating a platform or ecosystem that’s difficult to replicate.
