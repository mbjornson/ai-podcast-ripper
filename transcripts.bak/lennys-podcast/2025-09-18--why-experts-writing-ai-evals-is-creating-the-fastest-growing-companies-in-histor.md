# Why experts writing AI evals is creating the fastest-growing companies in history | Brendan Foody (CEO of Mercor) — Lenny's Podcast

## Summary
The episode centers on the "Era of Evals" (evaluations), arguing that the ability to systematically measure model capabilities is the single most valuable bottleneck in the current AI landscape, making it a massive business opportunity. Brendan Foody, CEO of Mercore, details how his company capitalized on this shift by providing expert labor to top AI labs and application companies needing high-quality data for rigorous model testing.

Foody's background highlights an unprecedented entrepreneurial trajectory: Mercore grew from $1 to $400 million+ in revenue run rate in just 16 months, establishing the company as a leader in the specialized field of AI training and validation. He reframes the narrative around AI, arguing that instead of focusing solely on job displacement, attention should be paid to the entirely new category of jobs being created—specifically those involved in defining, testing, and measuring complex automated workflows within enterprise value chains.

## Key Points
- **Evals as the New Bottleneck:** The primary limitation for advanced AI adoption is not model training itself, but determining *how* to measure success. Foody asserts that if "the model is the product, then the eval is the product requirement document (PRD)."
- **Market Shift from Crowdsourcing to Expertise:** The market has transitioned away from low-skilled crowdsourcing (writing grammatically correct sentences for early LLMs) toward sourcing and vetting highly experienced professionals—such as software engineers, investment bankers, doctors, and lawyers—to evaluate complex capabilities.
- **Enterprise Application Focus:** For businesses, the core task is building a systematic test or measurement framework to gauge how well AI automates their *core value chain*. This requires deep domain knowledge far beyond general LLM testing.
- **Exceptional Growth Metrics (Mercore):** The company achieved an extraordinary growth rate of $1 to $400 million+ in revenue run rate within 16 months, demonstrating the massive market demand for specialized AI evaluation services. They also maintain a net retention rate exceeding 1600%.

## Action Items
- [ ] **Develop Measurement Frameworks:** Identify a core value chain within your current industry or target niche. The immediate goal should be to design a systematic, measurable test (an "eval") that proves how AI can automate success in that specific process.
- [ ] **Focus on High-Skill Data:** Shift professional focus from general prompt engineering/data labeling toward acquiring domain expertise and the ability to vet complex outputs (e.g., financial models, legal analyses, architectural diagrams).
