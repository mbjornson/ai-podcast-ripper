# Head of Growth (Anthropic): “Claude is growing itself at this point” | Amol Avasare — Lenny's Podcast

## Summary
This episode features Amol Avasari, Head of Growth at Anthropic, discussing the company’s unprecedented growth trajectory – from $1 billion ARR to $19 billion in just 14 months. The conversation highlights Anthropic’s rapid expansion as a result of innovative product development, particularly the integration of memory from ChatGPT and the automation of growth experimentation through the “Cache” platform. A key takeaway is the company’s reliance on its world-class research team and inference capabilities, alongside the growth team’s efforts. Amol’s background as a founder, investment banker, and someone who overcame a severe brain injury adds a compelling layer to his story and perspective. The episode emphasizes the speed and scale of Anthropic’s growth, showcasing a future where AI products can experience exponential value increases.

## Key Points
- **Unprecedented Growth:** Anthropic has experienced explosive growth, going from $1 billion ARR in early 2025 to $19 billion in a remarkably short 14 months. This represents a 10x year-over-year growth rate, and recent revenue has doubled in the past few months.
- **Automation & Experimentation:** The growth team is driving a “Cache” platform focused on automating growth experimentation using Claude, aiming to leverage the product’s value for rapid iteration and scaling.
- **Exponential Product Value:** Anthropic anticipates a thousand-fold increase in product value within two years, reflecting the potential for AI advancements.
- **Logarithmic Scaling:** The team prioritizes log-linear scaling charts for data visualization, emphasizing exponential growth trends.
- **Team-Driven Success:** While the growth team is crucial, the episode stresses that Anthropic’s success is largely driven by its top-tier research team, inference capabilities, and other internal teams.
- **Unorthodox Hiring:** Amol landed the role by cold emailing Mike Krieger, the Chief Product Officer, showcasing the effectiveness of a targeted, personalized approach.
- **Cold Email Mastery:** Amol’s cold emailing strategy involves a highly optimized subject line, a concise message, and persistent follow-up – a skill honed through years of experience as a founder.

## Tools & Resources
- **Claude:** Anthropic’s flagship AI model.

- **Work OS:** A B2B SaaS platform that simplifies integration for large companies (mentioned by Amol’s investor).
