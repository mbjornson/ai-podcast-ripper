# Why ChatGPT will be the next big growth channel (and how to capitalize on it) | Brian Balfour (Reforge) — Lenny's Podcast

## Summary
Brian Balfour argues that the current technological shift driven by AI—specifically ChatGPT—is poised to be the next major distribution platform, creating a massive opportunity for early adopters. He frames growth not merely as product development, but as winning the "escape velocity" game: getting market traction before incumbents can copy your solution. Historically, truly impactful shifts required both a new technology *and* a corresponding new way to distribute products; Balfour predicts we are at this inflection point.

Balfour's background is highly credible, having led growth at HubSpot and witnessing the rise and fall of major channels (Facebook Ads, Google Ads, SEO). He emphasizes that while building a great product is necessary for success, it is not sufficient—the true separation lies in mastering distribution. The core message is one of urgency: traditional channels are saturated, making early identification and aggressive adoption of new platforms critical to survival and growth.

## Key Points
- **The New Distribution Platform:** ChatGPT/AI represents the next powerful distribution channel that startups must capitalize on. This shift provides a window for fast-moving companies to gain market share before large incumbents can react.
- **Distribution > Product (Necessary but Not Sufficient):** The most critical factor in startup success is not just having a great product, but building superior distribution channels.
- **The Shrinking Window of Opportunity:** The "escape velocity" game has become significantly harder because: 1) Incumbents can copy features faster than ever; 2) Organic distribution (SEO, social media) is declining/shrinking; and 3) AI increases the overall level of competition (infinite increase of competition).
- **The Pattern of Power:** The most impactful technological shifts are those that come with a corresponding *distribution* shift. Simply having new technology (like AI) without a new way to distribute it limits its power.
- **Strategic Playbook by Company Stage:** For late-stage companies, the strategy should be to place multiple bets across emerging channels. For early-stage startups, the advice is to choose one major channel and go "all in."
