# Spec-driven development: The AI engineering workflow at Notion | Ryan Nystrom — Lenny's Podcast

## Summary
This episode features Ryan Nystrom, engineering manager at Notion, discussing his team’s approach to development using AI, specifically leveraging Notion AI’s custom agent capabilities. The core idea is to shift from traditional, often inefficient, stand-up meetings and code reviews to a more streamlined workflow where AI pre-populates meeting agendas and provides context, allowing for more focused and productive discussions. Nystrom’s credibility stems from his experience managing a team of engineers and his outspoken criticism of slow development processes. He’s clearly embracing AI as a tool to combat these inefficiencies and boost team productivity. The episode highlights a shift in mindset – moving from reactive problem-solving to proactive, data-driven decision-making.

## Key Points
- **Spec-Driven Development:** Nystrom’s team utilizes AI (Notion AI’s custom agent) to generate specifications (“specs”) from initial prompts, drastically reducing the time spent on documentation. He starts with an empty markdown document and “yaps” ideas to Codex, then uses the generated spec to build.
- **Automated Stand-Ups:** The team has created a Notion AI agent that automatically compiles a pre-read for their daily stand-up meetings, pulling data from Slack conversations, task updates, and previous meeting transcripts. This eliminates the need for engineers to spend time preparing for the meeting.
- **Meeting Efficiency:** The goal is to transform meetings from unproductive “I did this” updates into focused discussions about problems, decisions, and next steps. The pre-read allows for a more efficient use of meeting time.
- **Quantifiable Improvements:** Nystrom mentions a 13% improvement in test performance after fixing a mock server environment, demonstrating the potential for AI to identify and address technical issues quickly.
- **Democratizing Information:** Nystrom acknowledges the challenges of working with introverted team members, suggesting that AI can act as a proxy for communication, facilitating information sharing and collaboration.

## Tools & Resources
- **Notion AI:** The central tool being utilized, particularly its custom agent functionality.
- **Codex:** OpenAI’s code generation model used for initial spec creation.
- **Whisper:**  Used for initial prompt generation.
- **Slack:** Used for real-time communication and data aggregation for the AI agent.
- **Notion:** The platform used to build the project hub ("Afterburner") and manage the workflow.
- **Stripe (Analogous):** Used to illustrate the concept of a platform offering enterprise-grade features as a service.

## Quotable
> “I literally don't know what I'm doing here. You got to explain it like I'm a five-year-old.” - Ryan Nystrom, highlighting the need for clear communication and accessible explanations when introducing new technologies.
> “It's more fun and I feel like I'm getting more done. It's weird to have this like win-win-win.” - Ryan Nystrom, capturing the positive emotional impact of embracing AI-driven workflows.

## Action Items
- **Experiment with AI-Assisted Documentation:** Try using AI tools to generate initial specifications or documentation for your projects.
- **Streamline Stand-Ups:** Analyze your team’s stand-up meetings and identify opportunities to automate or reduce the amount of time spent on updates.
- **Explore Custom Agents:** Investigate the potential of custom agents within tools like Notion or Slack to automate data aggregation and pre-read preparation.
- **Embrace Experimentation:**  Be willing to try new tools and workflows, even if they seem unconventional.

## Health, Fitness, or Mindset Practices Mentioned
- **Mindfulness:** The episode subtly emphasizes the importance of being present and focused during meetings, rather than simply going through the motions.
