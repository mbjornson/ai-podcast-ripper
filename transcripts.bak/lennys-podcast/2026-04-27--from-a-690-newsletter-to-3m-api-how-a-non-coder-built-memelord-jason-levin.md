# From a $6.90 newsletter to $3M API: How a non-coder built Memelord | Jason Levin — Lenny's Podcast

## Summary
This episode features Jason Levin, the founder of Meme Lord, a platform that generates memes using AI agents via API. Levin’s background is unconventional – he’s a non-coder who built Meme Lord from a $6.90 newsletter, highlighting a focus on rapid iteration and leveraging unconventional approaches. He argues that agents, unburdened by human biases and overthinking, are uniquely positioned to tap into trending content and create engaging experiences. The core of the discussion centers around the potential of agents as users for marketing and content creation, emphasizing the importance of reducing friction and embracing a “no UX is the best UX” philosophy. Levin’s approach is characterized by a willingness to experiment, a focus on capturing trends, and a surprisingly human-centric marketing strategy.

## Key Points
- **Agent-Driven Content Creation:** The central thesis is that agents, particularly those leveraging APIs, can be more effective at generating content (specifically memes) than humans due to their lack of bias and overthinking.
- **Lossy Marketing:** Levin advocates for a “lossy” marketing approach – directly delivering ideas to agents without the friction of traditional marketing processes.
- **No UX is the Best UX:**  A key principle championed by Levin and his team is that a minimal, almost non-existent user experience (UX) is actually the most effective, particularly when working with agents. This reflects a belief that agents require minimal guidance and can operate autonomously.
- **Trend Capture & Remixing:** Meme Lord’s core value proposition is its ability to quickly identify and remix trending memes, providing brands with a way to stay relevant in a rapidly changing digital landscape.
- **API-First Approach:** The platform’s success is driven by its API, allowing developers to integrate Meme Lord’s meme generation capabilities into their own applications and workflows.
- **Human-Agent Collaboration:** Levin envisions a future where humans and agents collaborate, with humans providing the initial ideas and agents executing them with speed and efficiency.

## Tools & Resources
- **Bubble:** The no-code platform used to build Meme Lord.
- **Telegram (via BotFather):** Used to interact with and control the AI agents.
- **Open Claw:** A specific AI agent architecture that Levin is focused on.
- **Work OS:** An enterprise API platform that provides drop-in integrations for applications, allowing them to become enterprise-ready.
- **iOS App Learnings (Lenny’s Article):** A deep dive into the design and development process of the iOS app, highlighting the “no UX is the best UX” philosophy.

## Quotable
> “Agents don't get in their mind about being funny or not funny. They don't overthink. They just go straight to the tokens and YOLO something out.” – Jason Levin
> “No UX is the best UX.” – Jason Levin (repeated throughout the episode)

## Action Items
- **Experiment with AI Agents:** Explore using AI agents for content creation, marketing, or other tasks – even if it feels unconventional.
- **Embrace Rapid Iteration:**  Don't be afraid to quickly test and refine your ideas, mirroring Meme Lord’s approach.
- **Understand Trend Dynamics:**  Develop a system for tracking and analyzing trending topics and memes.
- **Consider API-Driven Solutions:**  Look for ways to integrate APIs into your workflows to automate tasks and access new capabilities.
- **Focus on Human Connection:** Despite leveraging AI, remember the importance of human connection and storytelling in marketing.
