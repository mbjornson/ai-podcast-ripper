# Hard truths about building in the AI era | Keith Rabois (Khosla Ventures) — Lenny's Podcast

## Summary
This episode features Keith Raboi, a seasoned operator and investor with a remarkably diverse background including stints at Stripe, Square, LinkedIn, and PayPal, discussing the evolving role of product management and the critical importance of building world-class teams in the age of AI. Raboi’s credibility stems from his extensive experience building and scaling successful companies, making him a compelling voice on the future of work and talent acquisition. The conversation centers around a contrarian perspective on traditional hiring practices, advocating for a focus on identifying and leveraging existing talent within an organization rather than relying solely on external recruitment.

## Key Points
- **PMs are Evolving:** The traditional product management role is becoming less relevant, shifting towards a CEO-like function focused on strategic direction and “what are we building and why?” This reflects the increasing influence of AI and the need for leaders to define overall company strategy.
- **CMO Token Consumption:** Chief Marketing Officers (CMOs) are the biggest consumers of “tokens” (likely referring to internal resources and approvals) within organizations, highlighting the need for streamlined decision-making processes.
- **Ruthless Talent Assessment:** Raboi emphasizes the paramount importance of ruthlessly assessing talent, arguing that a founder’s ability to do this early on is the single most important skill for success, eclipsing other abilities.
- **Density of Talent is Key:**  He draws on his experience at PayPal, stating that the company’s success was driven by a high density of talented individuals, suggesting that concentrated talent pools are more effective than broad, diluted teams.
- **Leverage & Non-Linear Returns:**  He highlights the importance of demonstrating “leverage” – producing disproportionate returns with each hire – as a key metric for promotion and success, advocating for a system where one plus one equals three or more.
- **Internal Talent Sourcing:** Raboi’s strategy for identifying talent involved focusing on people he knew personally – building a network of trusted advisors to assess internal capabilities, a tactic he believes is more effective than traditional interviews.
- **Reference-Driven Assessment:** He advocates for extensive reference checks (20 per hire) to gain a deeper understanding of a candidate's abilities and performance, suggesting this is a skill that can be honed and improved.

## Action Items
- **Re-evaluate Your Role:**  Consider how the rise of AI is impacting your current role and responsibilities, particularly if you’re in a traditional product management function.
- **Focus on Team Building:** Prioritize building a strong, high-performing team – this is the single most important factor in a company’s success.
- **Develop Ruthless Assessment Skills:**  Actively work on your ability to identify and assess talent, focusing on identifying individuals who can drive disproportionate results.
- **Build a Strong Network:** Cultivate a network of trusted advisors and peers who can provide feedback and insights on potential hires.
- **Master Reference Checks:** Implement a rigorous reference-checking process, aiming for at least 20 references per senior hire.
- **Experiment with Remote Collaboration Tools:**  Consider tools like Work OS to streamline integrations and improve collaboration within your team.
