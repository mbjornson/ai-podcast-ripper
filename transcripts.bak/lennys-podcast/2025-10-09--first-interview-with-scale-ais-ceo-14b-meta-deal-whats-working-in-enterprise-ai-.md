# First interview with Scale AI’s CEO: $14B Meta deal, what’s working in enterprise AI, and what frontier labs are building next | Jason Droege — Lenny's Podcast

## Summary
The episode provides a deep dive into the current state of enterprise AI, arguing that the industry is transitioning from theoretical promise to complex, practical implementation. The core shift in AI capability is moving "from models knowing things to models doing things," necessitating the development of sophisticated agents capable of making decisions for users. Scale AI's role is highlighted as critical infrastructure—pioneering data labeling and evaluation (evals)—which is now essential because advanced tasks (like building a website or explaining complex medical topics) require expert human input to guide model behavior and fill knowledge gaps.

Jason Droege, CEO of Scale AI, brings credibility through his pioneering work in the data labeling space and his history of leading multi-billion dollar product launches, notably Uber Eats. He discusses how improving frontier models requires integrating domain experts (doctors, engineers) into the training loop to define "what good looks like" across various professional domains. Furthermore, Droege offers high-signal business lessons drawn from founding Scourer and navigating complex deals, emphasizing that in business, nothing is fixed; everything is negotiable.

## Key Points
- **AI Capability Shift:** The industry focus has shifted from simply having models *know* information to building systems where models can actively *do* things (i.e., autonomous agents making decisions).
- **The Value of Expert Data:** AI improvement is no longer solely about scaling compute or improving model code; the primary driver is hiring domain experts (doctors, engineers) to provide high-quality data and correct understanding—a category Scale pioneered.
- **Business Negotiation Principle:** The most enduring lesson from early startup experiences is that "everything is negotiable." Success in business requires mastering the art of negotiation rather than relying on fixed rules or structures.
- **Scale's Position & Meta Deal:** Scale remains a fully independent company despite Meta investing over $14 billion for 49% non-voting stock, solidifying its position as a critical infrastructure layer in the AI ecosystem.

## Tools & Resources
- **Merge:** A B2B SaaS API that allows companies to embed and connect dozens of customer-facing integrations (accounting, HR, CRM) with ease, positioning it as an essential tool for building AI-ready data pipelines.
- **Figma Make:** A "vibe coding" tool within Figma that enables users to turn design ideas or prompts into fully functional prototypes or apps, accelerating the validation process without writing complex code.

## Quotable
> "The general trend right now is going from models knowing things to models doing things."
>
> "In business and in startups, everything is negotiable... There is just the way that you can negotiate your way through the world."

## Action Items
- [ ] **Develop Agentic Thinking:** Focus on building or understanding systems where AI doesn't just retrieve information but autonomously makes decisions (moving from knowledge to action).
- [ ] **Master Negotiation Tactics:** Treat every business contract, partnership, and deal as a negotiation. Assume no term is fixed and focus on aligning incentives rather than following established rules.
- [ ] **Build Integration Infrastructure:** For B2B SaaS products, prioritize solutions that connect disparate customer data sources (like Merge) to power AI workflows safely and quickly.
- [ ] **Identify Domain Expertise Gaps:** If building an AI product, don't just rely on general LLMs; identify specific domain experts (doctors, engineers, etc.) whose knowledge is required to define "what good looks like" for your model.
