# Buying an Excavation Business: $4.2M Revenue Deal Reviewed — Acquisitions Anonymous

## Summary
The episode centers on the process of evaluating potential small business acquisitions, using a live deal review format. The hosts analyze three potential deals, ultimately focusing on a site grading, demolition, and hauling business in North Carolina. The business, established in 1993, specializes in comprehensive site preparation services—a critical, foundational step for any new construction project. The discussion highlights the difference between simply looking at gross revenue and understanding the underlying market fundamentals and operational stability of an essential B2B service.

The analysis emphasizes that the value of the business lies in its role as an infrastructure provider, serving developers and home builders. The seller is retiring, offering a smooth transition, and the business boasts a strong, established reputation over three decades. The hosts provide a detailed look at the equipment fleet and the consistent, high demand for these services, positioning the company as a stable, recession-resistant asset within the local construction economy.

## Key Points
- **B2B Essential Service Model:** The business is a B2B service provider, meaning its revenue stream is tied to the fundamental need for development (site prep, grading, clearing). This makes it less cyclical than consumer-facing businesses.
- **Financial Snapshot:** The deal has a gross revenue of **$4.2 million** and is being offered at an asking price of **$3.6 million**.
- **Due Diligence Warning:** The listing fails to disclose the **EBITDA**, which is a critical red flag. The hosts implicitly stress that cash flow and profitability (EBITDA) are far more important than top-line revenue when evaluating an acquisition.
- **Operational Scope:** The services are comprehensive, including land clearing, structural demolition, professional grading, material hauling, and property development preparation. The equipment list (3 excavators, 4 dozers, 9 dump trucks, etc.) indicates a robust, multi-site operational capacity.
- **Market Positioning:** The business benefits from being tied to local economic activity (Wilmington, NC area), suggesting consistent, long-term demand for infrastructure services.
