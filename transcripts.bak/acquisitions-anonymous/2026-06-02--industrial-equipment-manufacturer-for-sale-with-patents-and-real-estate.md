# Industrial Equipment Manufacturer for Sale with Patents and Real Estate — Acquisitions Anonymous

## Summary
The episode focuses on a deep dive into a specific, high-value acquisition opportunity: an American manufacturer of advanced HVAF (High Velocity Air Fuel) thermal spray systems and precision industrial powder feeders. The business is niche, operating in the aerospace and oil field services sector, and is being sold by the technology's inventor. Financially, it presents strong stability with 70%+ gross margins and a significant portion of its revenue derived from repeat purchases (spare parts and maintenance), which mitigates risk associated with new sales cycles.

The core value proposition lies in the proprietary nature of the technology—HVAF is positioned as an environmentally superior replacement for older processes like hard chrome plating, giving it strong regulatory tailwinds and market adoption potential. The business structure minimizes capital intensity by assembling complex systems rather than manufacturing all components internally, while its assets include two active U.S. patents (expiring 2035/2037), proprietary software, and a global installed base of 165 systems.

## Key Points
- **High Recurring Revenue Model:** The business boasts that 60% to 70% of its annual revenue comes from repeat customers purchasing spare parts and system upgrades, indicating strong customer retention and predictable cash flow.
- **Strong Financial Metrics:** Current financials include $2.5 million in revenue and an EBITDA of $1 million, resulting in a healthy 40% EBITDA margin. The Seller’s Discretionary Earnings (SDE) is approximately $1.4 million.
- **Favorable Valuation Multiples:** The asking price for the business ($4.95M) represents roughly a 3.8x to 5x multiple on current cash flow/EBITDA, which is considered reasonable given the asset quality and niche market position.
- **Technological Superiority & Market Shift:** HVAF technology utilizes compressed air instead of oxygen (unlike older methods like HVOF), resulting in lower operating costs, superior bond strength, and being environmentally friendly—a critical factor replacing hazardous materials like hexavalent chromium (CR6).
- **Asset Strength & IP Protection:** The company holds two active U.S. patents protecting core powder feeder mechanisms (expiring 2035 and 2037), providing a decade of intellectual property protection. Furthermore, the real estate ($1.4M) is available separately, allowing for strategic negotiation on assets vs. operational cash flow.
- **Low CapEx/High Margin Operation:** The company operates with minimal capital expenditure requirements by utilizing an assembly-focused model and outsourcing precision machining, maintaining high gross margins (mid-60% to high-70%).
