# How to Find Hidden Specialty Pharmacy Deals – Broker Secrets Explained — Acquisitions Anonymous

## Summary
This episode centers on a potential acquisition of a specialty pharmacy in Beverly Hills, California, presenting an opportunity within a rapidly expanding sector. The business boasts $1.5 million in EBITDA and is being offered at a 4.7x multiple based on current figures, though the broker suggests a much wider range (5.5x to 15.5x). The primary appeal lies in the macro market trend: the U.S. specialty pharmacy sector is projected to grow by 700% over five years, reaching an estimated $1 trillion from its current $129 billion valuation.

The discussion pivots on differentiating between standard compounding pharmacies and high-margin specialty drug providers (biologics, pain management). The speaker highlights that the true value of such a business is not just its location or revenue, but its established contractual relationships with major referral sources—such as hospitals and large physician groups—and its ability to dispense high-margin drugs that are difficult for independent pharmacies to access.

The deal's compelling nature is amplified by two factors: it is a forced sale due to the owner’s acute health issues, which reduces the risk of the seller holding back proprietary information; and the current reported margin (nearly 25%) significantly exceeds typical industry margins (10–15%), suggesting access to lucrative, high-value services.

## Key Points
- **Market Hypergrowth:** The U.S. specialty pharmacy market is projected to increase by 700% over five years, reaching $1 trillion from its current estimate of $129 billion. This massive growth rate suggests significant consolidation and opportunity for strategic buyers.
- **Margin Anomaly (Signal):** The business's reported margin is nearly 25%, which is significantly higher than the typical industry range of 10–15%. This high margin strongly implies that the current operation has established access to lucrative, hard-to-obtain revenue streams (e.g., biologics and specialized pain management drugs).
- **Critical Value Drivers:** The true value lies in durable, contractual referral sources (hospitals, major physician groups) and payer access for high-margin specialty drugs. Without these contracts, the business is relegated to lower-margin compounding/DME services.
- **Valuation Discrepancy:** The current 4.7x multiple is noted as potentially low compared to the broker's stated range of 5.5x–15.5x for similar specialty pharmacy comps, suggesting potential negotiation leverage or a discount due to the forced sale status.
- **Operational Risk/Due Diligence Focus:** Due diligence must focus on confirming the historical margin stability (i.e., what did the prior year look like?) and verifying the durability of the contractual relationships with major healthcare providers.
