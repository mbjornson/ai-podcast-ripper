# The $18M GovCon Business — Acquisitions Anonymous

## Summary
The episode reviews a potential acquisition opportunity: Generational Group, a management consulting firm specializing in procurement and sourcing for government and commercial clients. The company reports $18 million in revenue in 2024 and projects significant future revenue from SDVOSB contracts and repeat services ($58.4 million). While the business is classified as "management consulting"—a designation used to achieve higher valuation multiples—the hosts analyze its actual function, concluding it operates primarily as a specialized **Supply Chain-as-a-Service** provider within the highly lucrative GovCon (Government Contracting) niche.

The core value proposition of the company is solving a major pain point for defense manufacturers: managing inputs and consumables. Instead of requiring manufacturers to maintain direct relationships with dozens of individual vendors, Generational Group acts as a single procurement facilitator ("one throat to choke"), sourcing necessary parts from large vendors (like Grainger or MSC) and dropshipping them directly to the client site. The discussion highlights that understanding this operational model is key to assessing the true value and transferability of the business beyond its misleading title.

## Key Points
- **The Misclassification Multiplier:** The company labels itself a "management consulting firm" because this classification achieves higher valuation multiples than accurately describing its function as a supply chain or procurement facilitator.
- **Niche Focus & Stability:** The business is deeply entrenched in the GovCon sector, specifically supporting defense manufacturing support. Its status as an SDVOSB (Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business) provides a significant competitive advantage for winning government contracts.
- **The "One Throat to Choke" Model:** The true service provided is not consulting advice, but rather seamless supply chain management. It aggregates commodity and specialized inputs from 222 active vendors (e.g., Grainger, MSC) and dropships them directly, offering manufacturers a single point of contact for all operational consumables.
- **Financial Metrics:** Last year's revenue was $18 million (2024), with projected future lifetime revenue potential reaching $58.4 million from repeat services and SDVOSB contracts. The historical revenue has been stable in the $16M–$18M range, indicating sticky recurring revenue streams.
- **Market Insight:** Government contracting businesses often have a heavy emphasis on government contracts with little commercial mix; this deal appears to successfully blend both while maintaining its GovCon core.
