# $34M Freight Brokerage Deal Breakdown: Would You Buy This Business? — Acquisitions Anonymous

## Summary
The episode centers on a deep dive into a potential acquisition: a freight brokerage named Project Overland, which handles specialized, high-complexity logistics. The discussion highlights the massive financial appeal of the business, citing $34.3 million in 2025 revenue, $8.7 million in net revenue, and $5.5 million in adjusted EBITDA. The core thesis is that the company is exceptionally well-positioned for a Private Equity (PE) rollup, given its asset-light structure and its ability to manage complex, niche freight movements.

The value proposition is not simply moving goods, but solving problems that require specialized knowledge and reliability. The brokerage specializes in "mission critical" loads across diverse sectors like distilled spirits, oversized equipment rental, seafood distribution, and disaster response. This specialization allows them to charge a premium for expertise, making them a "liquidity provider" that takes a significant spread/markup between the cost of hauling and the final customer price.

The discussion also points to the operational sophistication that underpins the deal, including a best-in-class AI-enabled technology platform (Turbo TMS) and a robust Business Process Outsourcing (BPO) platform located in Medellin, Colombia. The hosts conclude that the business checks too many boxes—high revenue, low assets, specialized tech, and international operational scale—to be left on the table, suggesting it is a prime candidate for institutional investment.

## Key Points
- **The Value of Complexity (The Spread):** The company's value lies in its ability to manage operational complexity across seemingly random sectors. The common denominator is the need for specialized permits, licenses, and expertise (e.g., TTB compliance for spirits, cold chain for seafood, permits for oversized loads). This complexity allows the broker to charge a premium for reliability, which is the true source of the $8.7 million net revenue spread.
- **Asset-Light, High Margin Model:** The business is a pure brokerage, meaning it does not own the trucks or assets. This keeps overhead low and maximizes the margin potential. The calculation shows a significant markup: $34M (booked revenue) vs. $8.7M (net revenue/spread).
- **Geographic and Operational Arbitrage:** The use of a BPO platform in Medellin, Colombia, provides 24/7 support (dispatch, accounting, carrier relations) while maintaining a time zone that is highly compatible with US operations. The hosts note a cultural advantage in dealing with Latin American partners, suggesting a scalable, cost-effective operational model.
- **The PE Rollup Thesis:** The deal is viewed as a perfect "PE platform" acquisition. Its combination of tech enablement, specialized niche markets, and scalable operational infrastructure makes it highly attractive to Private Equity firms looking to consolidate similar businesses in the logistics space.
- **Financial Metrics:** The deal boasts a 25% net revenue margin profile and a 46% net revenue CAGR over the past three years, indicating strong, consistent growth and operational efficiency.

## Action Items
- [ ] **Analyze Your Niche for Complexity:** Identify a service or industry you are in that requires multiple, highly specialized licenses, permits, or regulatory compliances (e.g., HIPAA, TTB, FSMA). This complexity is your premium pricing opportunity.
- [ ] **Research BPO Outsourcing Models:** Investigate setting up a 24/7 back-office support center in a time zone that offers significant cost arbitrage (e.g., Latin America, Southeast Asia) to handle administrative tasks (dispatch, accounting) for a US-based business.
- [ ] **Study the PE Rollup Model:** If you own a small, successful business, research how it could be scaled by integrating it into a larger, multi-vertical platform. Focus on standardizing tech and operations across different acquired entities.
- [ ] **Focus on Tech-Enabled Infrastructure:** When evaluating any business, prioritize those with best-in-class, AI-enabled technology platforms (like Turbo TMS) over those relying solely on manual processes.
