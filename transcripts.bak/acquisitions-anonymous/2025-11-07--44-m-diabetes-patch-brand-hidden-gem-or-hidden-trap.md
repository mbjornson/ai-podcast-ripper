# $4.4 M Diabetes Patch Brand: Hidden Gem or Hidden Trap? — Acquisitions Anonymous

## Summary
The episode centers on a deep dive into a potential acquisition: a brand selling proprietary adhesive patches for Continuous Glucose Monitor (CGM) sensors, marketed under the name "Sticking with Success." The discussion is framed around evaluating whether this niche medical device accessory represents a hidden gem or a trap. The hosts analyze the financials and market dynamics of the business, which sells through Amazon FBA, its own website, and crucially, two major U.S. pharmacy chains (brick-and-mortar distribution).

The core value proposition discussed is not the sticker itself—which is easily replicated—but the established, high-margin distribution network. The hosts emphasize that while the product appears simple, the fact that it has secured significant annual purchasing commitments from large pharmacy chains provides a critical level of defensibility (a "moat") far beyond what pure e-commerce sales could offer.

## Key Points
- **Financial Metrics:** The business is listed with strong TTM margins: 64% Gross Profit and 41% Net Profit, based on $3.5 million in gross revenues and $1.46 million in cashflow (April 2025). The asking price is $4.4 million.
- **The True Moat:** The primary value driver is the established B2B relationship with two major U.S. pharmacy chains, which are reportedly purchasing significant annual volumes (estimated at $800,000/year) and requesting expansion into new SKUs. This institutional validation mitigates the risk of a low-barrier product.
- **Market Expansion:** The market is expanding beyond Type 1 diabetes management; there is growing use of CGM sensors for non-diabetics, suggesting a robust long-term addressable market (TAM).
- **Operational Efficiency:** The business utilizes modern e-commerce fulfillment models (Amazon FBA and 3PL), allowing the owner to manage operations with minimal time commitment (currently only 10 hours per week).
- **Due Diligence Warning:** Caution is advised regarding international sales structures. The initial figures showed potential confusion between Australian Dollars (AUD) revenue/location and U.S. market positioning, requiring thorough diligence on entity structure and currency conversion methods.

## Tools & Resources
- **High Level:** An all-in-one CRM recommended for small businesses, handling emails, texts, funnels, and more (mentioned as a sponsor).
- **Amazon FBA/3PL Model:** The standard fulfillment model discussed, where inventory is shipped directly to Amazon's third-party logistics facilities for stocking and customer fulfillment.

## Action Items
- [ ] **Investigate Niche Medical Supply Chains:** Focus due diligence on finding high-margin, low-tech accessories for regulated medical devices (e.g., CGM sensors). The value lies in the distribution channel, not the product complexity.
- [ ] **Analyze B2B Channel Validation:** When evaluating small businesses, prioritize revenue streams derived from established institutional buyers (pharmacy chains, hospitals) over pure direct-to-consumer e-commerce sales, as this indicates a stronger moat.
- [ ] **Review International Sales Structures:** If considering cross-border acquisitions, immediately verify the entity structure and currency conversion methodology to ensure reported financials accurately reflect local revenue streams.
- [ ] **Study High-Margin Consumer Health Products:** Research product categories that are consumables or accessories for expensive, regulated hardware (e.g., stickers for sensors; filters for machines).
