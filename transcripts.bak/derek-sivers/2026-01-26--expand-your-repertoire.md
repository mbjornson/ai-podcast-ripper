# Expand your repertoire — Derek Sivers

## Summary
The episode focuses on the critical need to deliberately expand one's "repertoire"—the range of skills, perspectives, and approaches used in life and work—to move beyond natural defaults and solve complex problems. The core message is that stagnation occurs when we rely on familiar patterns of thought or action. To achieve breakthrough thinking, one must actively seek external guidance and adopt radically different worldviews.

The speaker outlines several structured methods for achieving this expansion, moving from creative constraints (like the Oblique Strategies) to deep philosophical shifts (like studying foreign cultures). The overall goal is to train the mind to view problems not through a single lens, but through a vast, diverse collection of extreme, alternative perspectives.

## Key Points
- **The Principle of Repertoire Expansion:** To change reach and move past natural limitations, one must actively avoid defaults and seek guidance outside of one's immediate self.
- **Oblique Strategies:** This is a framework for creative problem-solving that involves using randomized, counter-intuitive prompts (like a deck of cards) when stuck. Examples include: "not building a wall making a brick," "use an unacceptable color," or "honor thy error as a hidden intention." This forces lateral thinking by introducing artificial constraints.
- **The Power of Diverse Perspectives:** The speaker advocates for approaching life and problems by adopting the philosophies of foreign cultures or different worldviews, rather than judging them. The focus should be on understanding the *benefits* of their perspective.
- **The "27 Approaches" Framework:** The concept of viewing life through 27 vastly different, extreme approaches (detailed in the book *How to Live*). This framework suggests that adopting an extreme, non-default perspective is the fastest way to generate novel solutions or insights into one's own life.

## Tools & Resources
- **Oblique Strategies:** A specific creative prompt deck used to break mental blocks by applying random, counter-intuitive constraints to a problem.
- **Book: *How to Live***: A resource presenting 27 vastly different approaches to life, each taken to an extreme. It is offered as a free resource for reframing and finding new perspectives.

## Action Items
- [ ] **Implement a Random Constraint System:** When facing a creative or business block, use a randomized prompt system (like a list of random nouns/verbs/adjectives) to force a non-obvious connection, mimicking the Oblique Strategies approach.
- [ ] **Adopt a "Foreign Culture" Mindset:** For the next week, approach a personal or professional problem by researching and adopting the core values or assumptions of a culture or philosophy vastly different from your own (e.g., Stoicism, Japanese Wabi-Sabi, etc.).
- [ ] **Read *How to Live***: Utilize the free book resource to systematically explore 27 extreme life frameworks, forcing a shift in your default assumptions about success, failure, and purpose.
- [ ] **Develop a "Challenge Repertoire":** Identify 3 areas of your life (career, fitness, relationships) where you are currently relying on defaults. For each area, research and commit to trying a radically different, non-obvious approach for 30 days.
