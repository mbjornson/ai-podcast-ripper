# AI's Killer-App Isn't Chatbots, It's Social | Linkedin Founder Reid Hoffman — Johnathan Bi

## Summary
Reid Hoffman, a highly credible figure in technology—known for founding SocialNet and building LinkedIn, alongside investments in PayPal, Facebook, and Airbnb—argues that the true "killer app" of Artificial Intelligence will not be single-player chatbots (like ChatGPT), but rather sophisticated, multiplayer social agents. He posits that AI will fundamentally mediate our existing human social networks, transforming how we interact with groups, societies, and even individuals.

Hoffman argues against the fear that AI will replace human relationships. Instead, he suggests that AI agents will become a surrounding "field" of intelligence—listening to conversations and providing mediated feedback on social dynamics. The key differentiator in this new era is moving beyond mere Intelligence Quotient (IQ) training toward Emotional Quotient (EQ). Companies like Inflection are focusing on training models for kindness and compassion, ensuring the technology supports, rather than displaces, genuine human connection.

## Key Points
- **The Killer App is Social Mediation:** AI's greatest impact will be in mediating our existing social structures (groups, societies), much like LinkedIn mediates professional networking today. It moves beyond simple one-to-one interaction into a complex "field of agents."
- **Focus on EQ over IQ:** The most advanced models are being trained not just for knowledge, but specifically for emotional intelligence (EQ). This is achieved through careful human feedback and instruction during the training process to ensure empathetic responses.
- **The Danger of Unsophisticated Relationships:** While humans naturally anthropomorphize (forming bonds with pets, dolls, etc.), Hoffman warns that this relationship must be handled with sophistication. The goal is not replacement, but enhancement—avoiding pathological or overly simplistic social dependencies on AI.
- **RLHF Defines the Character:** The difference between various generative AI models (e.g., Pi vs. ChatGPT) lies in their Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF). This process involves specific instructions and examples given to human trainers, which fundamentally determines the model's character—whether it is designed to be empathetic, kind, or merely factual.
- **Sociality as a Core Need:** The underlying drive for social connection remains paramount ("The central thing of the meaning of life is friendship"). AI will simply provide new tools and ways to manage this fundamental human need.
