# No One is Ready for This Coming War - Navy SEAL Andy Stumpf - #1089 — Modern Wisdom

## Summary
The episode features a discussion with Navy SEAL Andy Stumpf, focusing on the rapidly evolving nature of modern warfare and the impact of technology, particularly AI. Stumpf provides a highly credible, first-hand perspective, drawing from his operational experience to analyze how technology is reshaping tactics, techniques, and procedures (TTPs). He notes that modern warfare presents a blend of high-tech electronic capabilities (like drone warfare and advanced surveillance) alongside primal, close-quarters combat, creating a complex operational environment.

A major theme is the debate over AI's role in conflict. Stumpf outlines distinct phases of AI integration—human in the loop, human on the loop, and the feared human out of the loop. He argues that while AI excels in planning and analysis (especially in the electronic spectrum), the critical "go-no-go" decision, which relies on human judgment and the ability to cross a physical threshold, remains fundamentally human. The discussion also touches on the extreme physical realities of combat, such as the trauma from modern explosive wounds and the brutal physics of emergency ejections.

## Key Points
- **The Dual Nature of Modern Warfare:** Technology is viewed as an "equal measure" of both making warfare more humane and more dangerous. The shift from traditional combat to remote, internet-enabled kinetic options (like drone strikes) is a major concern.
- **AI Decision-Making Hierarchy:** Stumpf defines three phases of AI integration:
    1. **Human in the Loop:** AI assists, but a human makes the final decision.
    2. **Human on the Loop:** A human monitors and oversees the AI.
    3. **Human Out of the Loop:** The most concerning phase, where AI makes autonomous decisions, potentially putting operators at a tactical disadvantage.
- **The Primacy of the Human Element:** Despite advanced tech, the core of conflict remains physical. The ability for a human to make the decision to "cross the threshold of a door" is something AI cannot replicate, making human judgment the ultimate deciding factor in mission success.
- **Tactical Blending:** Modern battle spaces are characterized by a blend of high-level electronic innovation (advanced surveillance, drones) and low-tech, close-quarters fighting (fighting in trenches with AKs), illustrating a regression to basic human conflict patterns.
- **Survival Physics (Ejection):** Ejecting from an aircraft at high speed (e.g., three knots under the speed of sound) is described as an extreme physical trauma, requiring the body to perform the "opposite of breaking bones" to survive. Cold water, in this context, is noted as a life-saving mechanism because it shunts blood vessels, preventing fatal blood loss.

## Tools & Resources
- **TTPs (Tactics, Techniques, and Procedures):** The concept of maintaining established military operational procedures is emphasized as critical, especially when facing rapid technological change.
- **AI Integration Models:** Understanding the difference between "human in the loop," "human on the loop," and "human out of the loop" is a framework for analyzing technological risk.
