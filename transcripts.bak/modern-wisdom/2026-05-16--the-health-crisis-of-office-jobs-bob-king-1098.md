# The Health Crisis Of Office Jobs - Bob King - #1098 — Modern Wisdom

## Summary
This episode features Bob King, a self-described “observer” and former tech executive, discussing the significant health risks associated with modern office jobs. King argues that the primary problem isn't simply sitting, but rather *sitting still* – a state of inactivity that’s detrimental to musculoskeletal health and overall longevity. He emphasizes a preventative approach, advocating for movement and conscious posture correction rather than relying on reactive treatments like surgery. The episode highlights the alarming prevalence of back pain and related health issues among office workers, backed by substantial data on sitting time, injury rates, and mortality risks. King’s credibility stems from his personal experience with chronic back pain, which led him to seek guidance from Dr. Stu McGill, a renowned lower back pain specialist, and his willingness to share his observations and challenge conventional wisdom.

## Key Points
- **Sitting Still is the Problem:** The core argument is that the issue isn’t just sitting, but the prolonged, static posture adopted by many office workers. This inactivity leads to muscle atrophy and increased strain on the spine.
- **Data-Driven Risks:** The episode presents compelling statistics: 80% of office workers sit for 4-9 hours daily, musculoskeletal disorders account for a third of workplace injuries (costing $50 billion annually), and prolonged sitting is linked to a 16% higher risk of all-cause mortality and a 34% higher risk of cardiovascular disease.
- **The McGill Effect:** King references Dr. Stu McGill’s work, highlighting his expertise in lower back pain and advocating for a strong core to counteract the effects of prolonged sitting.
- **Ronnie Coleman’s Case:** The anecdote about Mr. Olympia Ronnie Coleman’s chronic back pain and reliance on opioids underscores the potential for medical interventions to exacerbate the problem.
- **Lack of Awareness:** King observes a widespread lack of awareness among office workers about the detrimental effects of their posture, noting that many simply don’t understand why they’re hunched over their desks.
- **Movement is Key:** The episode stresses the importance of incorporating movement throughout the workday, advocating for sit-stand desks and regular breaks to combat the negative effects of prolonged static sitting.

## Action Items
- **Assess Your Posture:** Regularly check your posture while sitting and standing. Be mindful of slouching and hunching.
- **Invest in a Sit-Stand Desk:**  Consider a sit-stand desk to allow for frequent postural changes.
- **Take Movement Breaks:** Schedule short breaks (every 30-60 minutes) to stand, stretch, and move around. Aim for at least 5-10 minutes of movement every hour.
- **Read:** “Back Mechanic” by Dr. Stu McGill – to understand the biomechanics of the spine and how to strengthen your core.
- **Follow:** Bob King on social media (if available) for further insights and observations.
- **Prioritize Movement:** Incorporate more movement into your daily routine – walking, cycling, taking the stairs – to combat the effects of prolonged sitting.
