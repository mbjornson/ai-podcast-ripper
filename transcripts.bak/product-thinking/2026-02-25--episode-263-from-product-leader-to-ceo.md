# Episode 263: From Product Leader to CEO — Product Thinking

## Summary
This episode of Product Thinking explores the transition from product leadership to CEO roles, focusing on the gaps and skills required for product leaders to successfully operate at the highest level of a business. The conversation features three expert guests: Mercedes Chapfield Taylor, Sean Kim, and Fabrice de Mazury. Mercedes outlines the three primary paths to CEO – go-to-market, finance, and product – and highlights the critical gaps that product leaders often face when transitioning to a CEO role, particularly around board communication and stakeholder management. Sean Kim emphasizes the importance of product empowerment at companies like Amazon and TikTok, advocating for clear metrics, real ownership, and accountability for results. Fabrice de Mazury reframes product work as investment decision-making, stressing the need for risk assessment, ROI analysis, and treating stakeholders as co-investors. The episode stresses proactive board engagement and succession planning as key elements for aspiring CEOs.

## Key Points
- **Three Paths to CEO:** The most common routes to CEO are through go-to-market (sales), finance, and product leadership.
- **Gap Identification:** Product leaders transitioning to CEO roles often lack experience in board communication, financial strategy, and stakeholder management.
- **Product Empowerment:**  Sean Kim argues for a shift towards product leaders having true ownership – defining metrics, securing resources, and driving results, as seen at Amazon and TikTok. This goes beyond simply building features.
- **Investment Decision-Making:** Fabrice de Mazury advocates for viewing product work as investment decisions, requiring careful consideration of risk, ROI, and payback periods.
- **Board Engagement is Crucial:** Mercedes emphasizes the importance of proactively engaging with the board, seeking a board seat early, and participating in board meetings to gain experience and influence.
- **Succession Planning:**  All speakers stress the importance of CEOs having a robust succession plan in place, and for CPOs to actively participate in that plan.
- **Lean Team Collaboration:**  The need for efficient, collaborative teams across sales, marketing, product, engineering, and finance is highlighted, emphasizing a "do more with less" approach.

## Action Items
- **Develop Board Communication Skills:** Actively seek opportunities to engage with the board, understand board dynamics, and practice communicating strategic insights.
- **Embrace Product Empowerment:**  Take ownership of key metrics, proactively negotiate for resources, and drive accountability for product outcomes.
- **Master Investment Decision-Making:**  Learn to assess the risk and ROI of product investments, and understand the financial implications of product decisions.
- **Build a Succession Plan:**  Start planning your own succession early, ensuring a smooth transition when the time comes.
- **Seek a Board Seat:**  Actively pursue a non-competitive board seat to gain strategic experience and influence.
- **Focus on Collaboration:**  Prioritize building strong relationships and fostering collaboration across all departments within the organization.
