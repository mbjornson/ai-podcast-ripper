# Episode 259: Simplifying AI for Global Customer Impact with Srinivasan Raghavan — Product Thinking

## Summary
This episode features Srinivasan Raghavan, Chief Product Officer at Freshworks, discussing how AI is fundamentally reshaping the role of product managers and driving a shift towards “product builders.” Raghavan’s background, starting as a support software engineer and progressing through finance, banking, and product strategy, provides a unique perspective on the evolution of product management. He emphasizes a shift from traditional, linear product development processes to a more agile, outcome-driven approach, fueled by AI tools. The core argument is that AI is collapsing the silos between product management, design, and development, empowering product managers to take on a broader range of responsibilities and directly contribute to product creation and delivery. Raghavan highlights the importance of customer obsession, seamless user experiences, and rapid prototyping – all facilitated by new technologies like Gemini and Figma.

## Key Points
- **AI as a Catalyst:** AI is fundamentally changing product management, enabling a shift towards “product builders” who can handle user research, design, prototyping, and even initial product development.
- **Collapse of Silos:** Traditional product development processes (research, architecture, engineering, testing) are being streamlined by AI, allowing product managers to take on more responsibility.
- **Outcome-Driven Mindset:** The core principle remains customer obsession and delivering measurable outcomes, regardless of the tools used.
- **Hybrid Roles Evolving:** The distinctions between roles like UX designer and product manager are blurring, with individuals developing a broader skillset – a “product builder” – capable of handling multiple aspects of the product lifecycle.
- **Rapid Prototyping:** AI tools like Gemini enable rapid prototyping and experimentation, dramatically reducing the time to market for new features and products.
- **B2B SaaS Context:** The discussion is heavily rooted in the B2B SaaS landscape, where complex integrations and collaboration between teams are crucial.
- **Customer-Centric Philosophy:**  Freshworks’ approach centers on delivering “uncomplicated” experiences to end-users, prioritizing seamlessness and ongoing innovation.

## Tools & Resources
