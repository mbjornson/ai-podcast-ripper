# Episode 264: Product at Scale Inside the World’s Largest Financial Institutions — Product Thinking

## Summary
This episode of “Product Thinking” features a discussion with Jameson Troutman, Head of Product for Small Business at Chase, and Vishal Kapoor, Senior VP of Product Management at a firm, focusing on scaling product development within large financial institutions. The core argument revolves around shifting from project-based funding to funding product capacity, empowering teams to continuously deliver outcomes. A key theme is the importance of systemic change, recognizing that digital transformation isn’t just about agile methodologies or experimentation, but a fundamental shift in operating models. The speakers emphasize the need for clear goals, transparent communication, and aligning product strategy with broader business objectives, particularly through incentivizing teams to obsess over customer needs and driving measurable outcomes. The episode also touches on the critical role of client modernization and leveraging data insights to improve investor experiences.

## Key Points
- **Shift from Projects to Capacity:** The central argument is a move away from funding individual projects towards investing in sustained product capacity, allowing teams to continuously deliver outcomes and iterate quickly. This is particularly crucial in large organizations where traditional project-based funding often leads to slow, disjointed efforts.
- **Systemic Transformation:** Digital transformation isn’t just about agile or experimentation; it’s a fundamental shift in the operating model. This includes rethinking processes, organizational structure, and how teams are empowered.
- **Clear Goals & Outcomes:** Establishing clear, measurable goals (OKRs) is paramount, and teams must consistently revisit these to ensure alignment. The examples given – transforming CX, building resilient platforms, and driving agility – highlight the importance of a shared vision.
- **Customer Obsession:** Product teams need to be laser-focused on solving customer problems and driving desired behaviors. This is exemplified by Vanguard’s focus on “CX alpha” – nudges and experiences designed to improve investor decision-making.
- **Alignment of Strategy & Product:** Ensuring product strategy aligns with business strategy is achieved through incentivizing teams to focus on outcomes, not just features. This involves empowering teams to solve problems themselves and avoid prescriptive feature requests.
- **Client Modernization:** Modernizing client experiences is critical for competitiveness, particularly in the financial sector, driven by the need to deliver contemporary experiences and leverage data insights.
- **Data-Driven Insights:** Utilizing client data to derive insights and translate them into actionable experiences is a key driver of value creation.
