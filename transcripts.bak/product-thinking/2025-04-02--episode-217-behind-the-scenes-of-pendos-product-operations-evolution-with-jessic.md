# Episode 217: Behind the Scenes of Pendo’s Product Operations Evolution with Jessica Soroky — Product Thinking

## Summary
The episode details Pendo’s evolution of its Product Operations framework, focusing on how structured processes and organizational shifts can elevate product development from a purely technical function into a holistic business driver. Jessica Soroky, Senior Director of Product Operations at Pendo, shares her experience leading this transition after conducting an objective assessment of the company's Product Development Lifecycle (PDLC). Her background is rooted in being a Chief of Staff, which allowed her to objectively analyze and propose significant structural changes that moved the organization beyond traditional silos.

The core discussion revolves around expanding product ownership from merely managing the Software Development Lifecycle (SDLC) to owning the entire PDLC—from initial ideation through customer adoption and go-to-market execution. Jessica outlines how this required redefining roles, most notably shifting "Scrum Masters" into Program Managers with a vastly expanded cross-functional mandate. The overall message is that great products require robust systems, processes, and cultures that support the entire value chain, not just product managers and developers.

## Key Points
- **Shift from SDLC to PDLC Ownership:** Product Operations must own the entire Product Development Lifecycle (PDLC), not just the Software Development Lifecycle (SDLC). This means taking responsibility for ideation, go-to-market strategy, and customer adoption alongside engineering execution.
- **The Program Manager Mandate:** The role of the program manager was significantly expanded from merely coordinating technical sprints to owning the idea *from its inception through the customer's hands*. This requires heavy engagement with Product Marketing, Customer Success, Technical Support, and Revenue Enablement.
- **Structured Cadence for Alignment:** Pendo implemented specific operational cadences to maintain cross-functional alignment:
    *   **Product Impact Meeting:** Held every six weeks (cross-functional view of customer usage).
    *   **Monthly Roadmap Reviews:** Focus on moving toward a "rolling planning" model rather than point-in-time planning.
- **Internal Tools Must Serve External Trust:** When using strategic frameworks like the Product Strategy Canvas for internal tools (e.g., auditing dashboards), you must frame their value proposition by showing how they contribute to overall *customer trust* and business compliance, thereby linking internal efficiency back to external customer value.

## Tools & Resources
- **Product Strategy Canvas:** A framework useful for mapping product goals across multiple business units, especially when those units are semi-autonomous or operate in regulated environments (e.g., banking/auditing).
- **LiveBlocks:** Mentioned as a platform for supercharging products with ready-made collaborative features (AI co-pilots, multiplayer experiences) to drive daily active users.
- **Product Institute:** Course mentioned by the host regarding product strategy.

## Quotable

> "We found that we were really good at the engineering part of the product development lifecycle... We had completely ignored ideation through to engineering. [...] And once engineering was done, the go-to-market motion... was also an area that we hadn't completely ignored..."

## Action Items
- [ ] **Develop Cross-Functional Influence:** Seek roles or projects that require you to own a process end-to-end (from ideation/business need through GTM/adoption), rather than being confined to a single technical silo.
- [ ] **Implement Structured Review Cadences:** Establish mandatory, cross-functional "Impact Meetings" (e.g., every 6 weeks) focused purely on customer usage data and business outcomes, forcing strategy owners to stay aligned with reality.
- [ ] **Reframe Internal Tools for External Value:** When building internal tools or dashboards, always map the feature's benefit back to a tangible improvement in *customer trust* or compliance that saves the company money/risk.
- [ ] **Study Program Management Expansion:** Study how your current "Scrum Master" or project coordination role can be expanded to include mandatory engagement with Product Marketing, Customer Success, and Sales Enablement—making you responsible for the idea's journey across all departments.
