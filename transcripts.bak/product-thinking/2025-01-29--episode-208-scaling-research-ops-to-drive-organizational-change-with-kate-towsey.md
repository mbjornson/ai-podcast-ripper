# Episode 208: Scaling Research Ops to Drive Organizational Change with Kate Towsey — Product Thinking

## Summary
This episode provides a deep dive into establishing robust organizational systems, specifically through the lens of Research Operations (Research Ops). The discussion moves beyond simply building features to focusing on the underlying processes, culture, and knowledge infrastructure that allow a company to deliver value reliably over decades. Kate Towsey, an independent research ops advisor who previously worked at Atlassian and founded the Research Ops Community, details how structured research practices can solve the "evergreen problem"—where valuable institutional knowledge disappears into the ether.

A significant portion of the episode addresses common product scaling pitfalls: when a company is constantly pulled in multiple directions by demanding clients. The core message delivered is that growth requires establishing firm boundaries and shifting the organizational mindset from being a reactive, custom-build consultancy to a strategic, scalable product provider with a clear Ideal Customer Profile (ICP).

## Key Points
- **Operationalizing Knowledge:** Instead of treating knowledge as a monopolized asset, organizations must "operationalize" it. The goal is to build systems that capture and distribute reliable knowledge so the company isn't always "thirsty" for information when making big decisions.
- **The Danger of Sales Debt:** When custom features are built for clients outside of the core product roadmap (off-roadmap), this creates "sales debt." This debt must be quantified, forcing hard conversations with clients about whether they are willing to pay a premium price for non-strategic customizations.
- **Defining Boundaries is Strategic:** To avoid being perceived as an agency or consultancy that builds custom software for every client, the company must establish and communicate a strong vision: what it *will* handle and, equally important, what it *will not* handle.
- **Focus on Scalable Jobs to Be Done (JTBD):** When faced with diverse client requests, do not build piecemeal. Instead, analyze if all clients are solving the same fundamental "jobs to be done" in a way that can scale to thousands of users, or if you are just building for the loudest few.
- **The Research Ops Framework:** Research Ops formalizes the processes around research—from content strategy and user research blogging (GDS example) to tooling and data management—ensuring that insights become systemic assets rather than one-off reports.

## Tools & Resources

- **Research Ops Community:** A professional community dedicated to formalizing the field of research operations.

## Action Items
- [ ] **Audit Your Knowledge Leakage:** Implement a formal process (a "dam") for capturing all institutional knowledge gained during projects. Document processes, decisions, and findings immediately after they occur to prevent them from being lost.
- [ ] **Calculate Sales Debt:** Audit your current client commitments. Quantify the revenue or time spent on custom features that fall outside of your core product roadmap. Use this data point in strategic conversations with clients.
- [ ] **Define Your Strategic Boundaries:** Write a clear, concise statement defining who your Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) is and what specific problems you are committed to solving for them (and equally important, what you will *not* solve).
- [ ] **Develop Product Thinking Skills:** Focus on understanding the "Jobs To Be Done" rather than just listing features. Always ask: Are we solving the same core problem for a scalable group of users?
- [ ] **Explore Research Ops:** Investigate formal training or resources in Research Operations to professionalize your company's approach to gathering, structuring, and utilizing customer insights.
