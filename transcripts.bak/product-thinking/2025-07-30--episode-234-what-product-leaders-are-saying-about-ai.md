# Episode 234: What Product Leaders Are Saying About AI — Product Thinking

## Summary
The episode argues that AI is a profoundly transformative force across business, society, and product development, but emphasizes that its value lies in solving fundamental, persistent customer and business problems, not in the technology itself. The discussion centers on how AI is fundamentally changing the role of the Product Manager (PM), not by eliminating it, but by significantly increasing its expectations. PMs are now expected to operate with near real-time intelligence, monitoring vast, disparate data sources (like competitor movements or 10K filings) to proactively inform strategy.

A major theme is the shift from "bad admin" work to high-value problem-solving. AI is poised to automate repetitive, non-value-added tasks—such as writing weekly updates or preparing executive decks—allowing PMs to focus on strategic execution. Furthermore, the conversation highlights the rapid escalation of risk in cybersecurity, warning that traditional security methods ("security by obscurity") are obsolete against automated, advanced threats like deepfakes and self-modifying code.

## Key Points
- **PM Role Evolution:** AI will increase the expectations of the PM function, requiring them to move from loosely following market trends to proactively monitoring and analyzing sources (e.g., customer feedback themes, competitor movements, 10K forms) in near real-time.
- **Cybersecurity Escalation:** The threat landscape is rapidly escalating. Automated attacks (deepfakes, self-modifying code) are now 10,000 times faster than those requiring human hackers, making quick adoption of advanced security technologies a critical competitive advantage.
- **Operational Efficiency Gains:** AI can significantly reduce "bad admin" work—tasks like writing status updates or preparing strategy decks—allowing teams to reallocate time to core customer problems.
- **Hyper-Personalization:** The next frontier in customer experience is hyper-personalization, moving beyond simple recommendations to understanding complex user states (e.g., "I'm feeling tired, I'd like comfort food or something healthy").
- **Data Onboarding Automation:** AI can drastically reduce manual data entry time. One example cited was reducing the time required to onboard a restaurant partner (setting up a menu) from up to four hours to a fraction of that time by using AI menu upload tools.

## Tools & Resources
- **AI Assistants:** Tools capable of building complex user journeys directly within a chat interface (e.g., building a basket in the chat).
- **Product Ops Systems:** Structured systems and toolings necessary to capture, measure, and analyze real-time data inputs (customer feedback, market news, 10K forms) to inform product strategy.
- **AI Menu Upload Tools:** Specialized tools that can scan physical menus and automatically create structured data entities for digital platforms, drastically improving operational efficiency.

## Quotable
> "The biggest thing in my mind is that I think that AI is really actually going to increase the expectations of the PM function."
>
> "Your security by obscurity isn't going to work anymore because the things that used to require human hackers are going to be automated and 10,000 times as fast."

## Action Items
- [ ] **Audit "Bad Admin" Tasks:** Identify the top 3 most repetitive, non-value-added administrative tasks (e.g., status reports, basic deck preparation) in your current role and research AI solutions to automate them.
- [ ] **Develop Real-Time Market Monitoring:** Implement a system (Product Ops) to proactively monitor and analyze external, unstructured data sources (e.g., competitor press releases, industry 10K filings, niche customer forums) to spot emerging market needs before they become obvious.
- [ ] **Focus on Problem, Not Tech:** When proposing new features or products, force yourself to articulate the core *human* problem being solved first, and then determine if AI is the most efficient tool for that solution.
- [ ] **Study Hyper-Personalization Flows:** Research how leading companies are using AI to create multi-step, context-aware customer journeys that anticipate needs (e.g., combining mood, time of day, and dietary restrictions for recommendations).
