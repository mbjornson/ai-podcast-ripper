# Episode 213: How Vanguard is Modernizing Finance Through Digital Innovation with Marco and Amber — Product Thinking

## Summary
The episode discusses digital transformation through two lenses: Vanguard's mission-driven modernization and a broader strategic framework for adopting product thinking in large, non-SaaS organizations. Marco de Fridas and Amber Brastowski, both CPOs at Vanguard, detail how their company—a client-owned financial institution—structures its digital products around a core mission: making investors more successful. Their approach emphasizes aligning business strategy directly with the product experience to enhance user success.

The second half of the discussion, featuring Melissa Perry's "Dear Melissa" segment, provides critical advice for CIO-led IT organizations (common in finance, pharma, and insurance). The central thesis is that adopting a product mindset must transcend commercialization. Organizations should not view software development merely as a cost-saving measure; instead, they must strategically treat *all* internal software—even tools used only by employees—as a core competitive differentiator and strategic advantage.

## Key Points
- **Strategic Shift from Cost Center to Advantage:** The biggest mistake in digital transformation is viewing technology solely through the lens of cost savings. Organizations must reframe software development (internal or external) as a source of strategic advantage that makes them "10 times better" than competitors.
- **Product Thinking for Non-SaaS Industries:** Product management principles are not limited to companies whose core business *is* selling tech products. In industries like pharmaceuticals, optimizing internal processes (e.g., running clinical trials) using software can create a massive competitive edge by speeding up time-to-market or improving efficacy—even if the end user never sees that specific software.
- **The Importance of Mission Alignment:** For financial services, product modernization must be anchored to the client's best interest (Vanguard’s mission). This ensures that every digital feature and process improvement is designed not just for efficiency, but to actively improve the investor's outcome.

## Action Items
- [ ] **Audit Internal Processes:** Identify one critical internal process (e.g., reporting, data aggregation, compliance checks) and treat its optimization with the same rigor you would apply to a customer-facing product. Ask: *How can making this 10x better give us an advantage over our competitors?*
- [ ] **Reframe Tech Spend:** When proposing new software or tech investments in your current role, shift the justification from "cost reduction" or "efficiency gain" to "strategic differentiator" or "competitive capability."
- [ ] **Adopt a Product Mindset for Non-Tech Roles:** If you are not in product management, practice viewing every operational bottleneck as a solvable user experience problem. Focus on *user outcomes* rather than just process completion.
