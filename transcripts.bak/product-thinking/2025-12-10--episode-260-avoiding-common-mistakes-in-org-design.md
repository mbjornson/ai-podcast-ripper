# Episode 260: Avoiding Common Mistakes in Org Design — Product Thinking

## Summary
This episode of Product Thinking delves into the critical aspects of organizational design for product teams, focusing on how to avoid common pitfalls and build a structure that truly supports product delivery. Melissa Perry, a product strategist with a background in scaling product organizations, argues that a successful org design starts with a deep understanding of product strategy and value streams. She emphasizes the importance of short layers of control, balanced teams, and aligning teams around customer needs rather than architectural concerns. A key takeaway is the danger of organizing solely around architecture, which can lead to over-optimization and a lack of strategic focus. The episode advocates for a flexible, customer-centric approach to team structure, prioritizing speed and responsiveness.

## Key Points
- **Value Stream Focus:** The core of effective org design is mapping out value streams – from customer need to delivered value – to ensure efficient product delivery.
- **Short Layers of Control:** Reducing layers of management is crucial for quick decision-making and preventing strategic compression. The example of 15-person reporting chains highlights the negative impact of excessive bureaucracy.
- **Balanced Teams:** Teams should be composed of individuals with the right skills and time commitment to deliver value, avoiding bottlenecks and handoffs.
- **Customer-Centric Organization:** Organizing by customer personas or jobs to be done is preferred over organizing by architecture, which can lead to over-engineering and a disconnect from customer needs.
- **Platform Teams - Strategic Alignment:** Platform teams are valuable for scaling common services, but they require strong collaboration and strategic alignment with customer-facing product teams to avoid becoming isolated order-takers.
- **Strategy First:**  The most important step in designing an organization is to first define the product strategy and portfolio before designing the team structure.

## Action Items
- **]** Conduct a value stream mapping exercise for your current product or service.
- **]** Analyze your team structure to identify layers of control and potential bottlenecks.
- **]**  Assess your team's skillsets and ensure they align with customer needs and product strategy.
- **]**  Consider organizing your team around customer personas or jobs to be done.
- **]**  If you have a platform team, ensure it has a strong collaborative relationship with customer-facing product teams.
- **]**  Prioritize defining your product strategy and portfolio before designing your organizational structure.
