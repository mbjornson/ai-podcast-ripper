# Episode 268: Rethinking What Done Means in Product Ops — Product Thinking

## Summary
This episode of Product Thinking delves into the critical shift needed in product organizations – moving beyond a traditional focus on code completion to a “whole product complete” approach. The core argument is that true product success hinges on aligning all go-to-market functions (product ops, marketing, sales, customer success) around a shared understanding of customer value and actively driving adoption. The episode features insights from Tricia Price (Chief Product Officer at Pendo), Kate Towsie (research ops advisor), and Jessica Soroki (Senior Director of Product Operations at Pendo), highlighting the importance of systems thinking, knowledge management, and proactive process design. A key contrarian point is the often-overlooked issue of product marketing being treated as a mere “bridge” rather than a strategic partner deeply embedded in product strategy.

## Key Points
- **Shift from Code-Complete to Whole Product Complete:** The fundamental change is redefining “done” not just as code deployed, but as customers actively using and loving the product. This requires a holistic view encompassing go-to-market activities.
- **Knowledge as a Strategic Asset:** Kate Towsie argues that knowledge is the *most* valuable asset in a product organization, and many companies are failing to manage and leverage it effectively, leading to wasted effort and missed insights.
- **Product Marketing as a Strategic Partner:** The episode challenges the conventional view of product marketing as a simple “bridge” between product and go-to-market, advocating for a deeply integrated role focused on understanding customer buying behavior, competitive landscapes, and value propositions.
- **Process Rationalization:** The importance of establishing clear, consistent processes (cadences) across teams – product, marketing, ops – is emphasized. This isn't about rigid templates, but about facilitating communication and accountability.
- **Experimentation & Iteration:** The team at Pendo utilizes a structured experimentation approach, focusing on quantifiable metrics (free-to-paid conversion, retention) rather than relying on intuition.
- **Systemic Knowledge Management:** The need for robust knowledge management systems – including actively recruiting customer research participants – is highlighted as a critical component of successful product operations.  Lack of this leads to duplicated effort and missed opportunities.

## Tools & Resources
- **Pendo:**  A product analytics platform used by the guests, showcasing their approach to data-driven product operations. (https://www.pendo.io/)
- **Research Ops:** Kate Towsie’s area of expertise, representing a growing field focused on systematically managing and leveraging customer research.
- **Scrum:**  While the episode critiques a purely Scrum-focused approach, it acknowledges its value as a framework for iterative development.

## Quotable
> “Done is customers using and loving it.” – Tricia Price
> “Knowledge is the most valuable asset in any product organization.” – Kate Towsie

## Action Items
- **Reframe “Done”:**  Challenge your team’s definition of “done” – is it truly aligned with customer value and adoption?
- **Implement Knowledge Management:**  Establish a system for capturing, sharing, and leveraging customer research and insights.  Start small – even a shared document repository can make a difference.
- **Embed Product Marketing:** Advocate for product marketing to be a core member of the product team, involved in strategic discussions and customer understanding.
- **Define Clear Cadences:**  Develop and document consistent quarterly, monthly, and weekly cadences for key product operations activities.
- **Prioritize Experimentation:**  Adopt a structured experimentation approach, focusing on measurable outcomes and iterating quickly.
- **Follow Kate Towsie:**  (https://www.katetowsie.com/) – For insights into research operations and knowledge management.
