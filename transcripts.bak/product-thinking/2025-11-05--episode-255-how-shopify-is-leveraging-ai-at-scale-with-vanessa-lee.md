# Episode 255: How Shopify Is Leveraging AI at Scale with Vanessa Lee — Product Thinking

## Summary
This episode features Vanessa Lee, VP of Product at Shopify, discussing Shopify’s approach to leveraging AI, specifically with their “Sidekick” assistant. The core discussion revolves around the challenges of building conversational AI – the difficulty of creating a robust, reliable assistant that can handle a wide range of user queries – and how Shopify tackled this through a highly iterative, data-driven approach. A key insight is their “judge” system, where an LLM is used to evaluate the performance of the assistant against simulated conversations and live user interactions. Lee emphasizes Shopify’s focus on empowering merchants through Sidekick, moving beyond simple task automation to providing strategic advice and insights. Her background as a founder and product manager with experience across various Shopify products (developer platform, online store, and now AI initiatives) provides a strong foundation for this discussion.

## Key Points
- **The Difficulty of Conversational AI:** Building a truly useful conversational AI is incredibly complex. The sheer volume of potential user queries makes it difficult to provide accurate and relevant responses consistently. It's easy to create a demo but extremely hard to build a robust, reliable assistant.
- **The “Judge” System:** Shopify’s innovative approach to evaluation involves creating an LLM “judge” that constantly assesses Sidekick’s performance. This judge is trained to embody product intuition and provides feedback to the development team, ensuring the assistant aligns with desired behavior.
- **Live Conversation Monitoring:** Shopify doesn’t just rely on simulated conversations; they actively monitor live user interactions with Sidekick to identify trends and areas for improvement. This provides real-time data for refining the assistant’s capabilities.
- **Merchant-Centric Focus:** Shopify’s primary goal with Sidekick is to empower merchants, particularly small business owners, by providing assistance with complex tasks and strategic insights. They see Sidekick as a co-pilot or advisor, not just a simple chatbot.
- **Evolving Use Cases:** Initially focused on basic task automation (product creation, collection management), Sidekick is now being used for higher-order tasks like data analysis, trend identification, and strategic recommendations – essentially acting as a data analyst or financial advisor.
- **Product Management Culture:** Shopify’s product management culture is characterized by deep involvement and a love for detail, stemming from Toby’s leadership and extending throughout the organization. They prioritize a hands-on approach and a willingness to get “in the weeds.”

## Tools & Resources
