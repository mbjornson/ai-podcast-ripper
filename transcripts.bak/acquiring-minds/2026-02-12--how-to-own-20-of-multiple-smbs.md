# How to Own 20% of Multiple SMBs — Acquiring Minds

## Summary
The episode centers on Evan DeLeonardi's journey into acquiring and investing in Small-to-Medium Businesses (SMBs). His background is marked by a continuous search for true entrepreneurial freedom, moving from consulting to Airbnb hosting before committing fully to business acquisition. He details his initial experience buying a commercial cleaning service ($500k SDE), which was challenging but provided crucial lessons in managing weak leadership and family dynamics while being a remote "nomad."

Evan's current professional focus is as an SBA buy-side advisor/investor. His model involves partnering with other searchers, helping them source and close acquisitions, and receiving equity—approximately 20%—in return for his advisory work. He emphasizes that this strategy allows him to build a diversified portfolio of passive income streams across multiple businesses without incurring personal guarantees in any single venture.

The discussion also touches on the lifestyle component of modern entrepreneurship. Evan advocates for prioritizing "lifestyle first," combining high-output work (up to 90-hour weeks) with digital nomadism, citing locations like Tulum and Chiang Mai not just for leisure, but because they facilitate a motivated, low-cost environment conducive to intense professional grinding.

## Key Points
- **Diversified Passive Investing:** The core strategy is acquiring equity (around 20%) in multiple SMBs rather than betting on one single business. This diversification provides passive income and risk mitigation without personal guarantees.
- **De-Risking Through Community:** Evan stresses that the most critical step was joining specialized, paid communities (like Ben Kelly's acquisition ACE). These groups provide education, networking, and guidance necessary to move past initial fears and mistakes associated with buying a business.
- **Advanced Financing Structure:** For larger deals, the financing structure can be complex: utilizing SBA 7A loans (up to $5 million) combined with "peri-pursued debt" (which can unlock an additional up to $3 million in conventional debt).
- **The Entrepreneurial Grind is Location-Dependent:** Digital nomad locations are not just for relaxation; they provide a practical advantage. They allow entrepreneurs to live in cheaper locales, maximizing dollar value, and maintain a high level of motivation due to favorable environments (e.g., consistent sunshine, active community energy).
- **Work Ethic vs. Lifestyle:** The model is not "easy money." Evan maintains that achieving the desired lifestyle requires extreme dedication, citing periods of 90-hour work weeks while traveling.

## Tools & Resources
- **Pioneer Capital Advisory:** A firm mentioned for its expertise in structuring complex acquisitions and offering peri-pursued debt options.
- **SBA 7A Loans:** The primary federal loan program discussed for business acquisition financing (up to $5 million limit).
- **Peri-pursued Debt:** A specialized form of conventional debt that can be layered onto an SBA loan, increasing the total capital available for acquisitions (up to $3 million extra).
- **Ben Kelly's Acquisition ACE:** A specific community/group mentioned as a resource for education and support in the acquisition process.
