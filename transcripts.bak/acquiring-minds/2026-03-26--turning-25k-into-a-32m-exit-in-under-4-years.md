# Turning $25k into a $3.2m Exit in Under 4 Years — Acquiring Minds

## Summary
Corey Mullins, the guest, is an acquisition entrepreneur who leveraged deep, hands-on knowledge of the HVAC trade to execute a rapid and highly profitable business acquisition. After his original plan to take over his family's HVAC business fell apart in late 2021, he was forced to pivot and "hit the ground running." He started his own small HVAC operation while simultaneously acquiring a much smaller, declining, decades-old HVAC business that generated less than $200,000 in revenue.

His success hinged on recognizing the true, undervalued asset of the acquired business: the phone number displayed on thousands of installed units throughout South Florida. This created a massive, built-in lead source. By combining his two decades of practical trade experience with aggressive business building, he grew the company and successfully exited it to private equity for $3.2 million, mostly in cash, in under four years.

The episode emphasizes that the combination of deep industry knowledge (being a tradesman) and business acumen is a "killer combination," allowing the operator to build value far beyond what a typical, inexperienced searcher could achieve.

## Key Points
- **The Value of the Installed Base:** The acquired HVAC business was valuable not for its current revenue (under $200k) but because its phone number was physically displayed on thousands of installed units, creating a captive, long-term lead source.
- **High MOIC through Forced Pivots:** Corey achieved a massive Multiple on Invested Capital (MOIC), turning a total investment of $25,000 ($10k self-funded + $15k SBA required) into a $3.2 million exit in under four years.
- **The Tradesman Advantage:** Having deep, operational knowledge of the trade (the "full gamut" from apprentice to general manager) provided an immediate, non-obvious advantage over most searchers who lack industry expertise.
- **Speed and Necessity Drive Action:** His ability to acquire and build the business was accelerated by necessity—he had to "hit the ground running" after his original plan failed, forcing him to act quickly.
- **Due Diligence Focus Areas:** For potential buyers, the team at LCS highlighted critical assessment factors: low entry multiple, business buyer fit, quality of revenue, seller alignment, and industry tailwinds.
