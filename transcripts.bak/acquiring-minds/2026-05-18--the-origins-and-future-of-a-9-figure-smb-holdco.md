# The Origins and Future of a 9-Figure SMB Holdco — Acquiring Minds

## Summary
This episode of Acquiring Minds features an interview with Gantt Elmore and Matt Moldenhauer, partners at Elmore Companies, a multi-business holdco with $200 million in annual revenue and 20 portfolio companies. The core discussion centers around their approach to building and owning businesses through acquisition, emphasizing a “forever hold” strategy and the importance of compounding returns. A key theme is the family’s entrepreneurial history, tracing back three generations of acquisition-focused business building, starting with Gantt’s grandfather’s travel empire and continuing through his father’s diversification into education and staffing. The conversation highlights the unconventional financing methods employed by the family – reinvesting profits rather than seeking external capital – and the importance of operational expertise, exemplified by Matt’s prior experience operating a small business. The episode also introduces new resources for Acquiring Minds listeners, including the ETA database, a searchable archive of past episodes, and a webinar on revenue recognition in SMB transactions.

## Key Points
- **Compounding is Key:** The Elmore Companies’ success is built on a long-term, compounding strategy, with significant returns taking 7-15 years to materialize. This contrasts with the immediate gratification often sought by investors.
- **“Forever Hold” Strategy:** They intentionally hold onto acquired businesses indefinitely, focusing on operational improvements and long-term value creation rather than quick flips.
- **Operating Partner Focus:** Matt Moldenhauer’s background as an operator – having previously run a small business – is crucial to their strategy, emphasizing the importance of hands-on involvement and deep operational understanding.
- **Unconventional Financing:** The family’s approach to financing acquisitions was unique, relying on reinvesting profits from existing businesses rather than seeking external capital or relying on traditional equity financing. This highlights a bootstrapped, entrepreneurial mindset.
- **Generational Legacy:** The Elmore Companies’ history spans three generations, each building upon the successes and learnings of the previous, demonstrating a sustained commitment to acquisition-based business building.
- **Silver Tsunami:** The interview touches on the potential challenges of an aging business ownership demographic (“silver tsunami”) and the need for younger entrepreneurs to step up and take the reins.
- **ETA Database Launch:** The launch of the ETA database is a significant resource for Acquiring Minds listeners, providing a searchable archive of past episodes for targeted research and learning.
