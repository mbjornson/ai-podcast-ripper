# A Search Fund Journey to $350m — Acquiring Minds

## Summary
The episode features Jenna Wiggum, President of Abound Health, discussing her journey in the search fund space, specifically within the home care industry. Abound Health represents a significant success story, having grown its revenue from an initial $60 million to a projected $350 million by 2026, with EBITDA projected to reach $75 million. Jenna's background is highly relevant, transitioning from investment banking (healthcare) to operational roles, culminating in running a craft brewery and restaurant with full P&L responsibility. This experience cemented her desire to move beyond advisory roles and into hands-on operational leadership.

The discussion highlights the immense complexity and difficulty of scaling home care businesses, noting that much of Abound's growth has been inorganic, involving the acquisition of 14 separate agencies. While the financial metrics are impressive, Jenna emphasizes that the sector's true value lies in its mission—providing care to vulnerable populations—and that this mission must be treated with the gravity it deserves. The episode also promotes a related webinar focusing on the critical, often overlooked, relationship dynamics between the owner and the operator.

## Key Points
- **Inorganic Growth Caution:** While the headline numbers are massive, the growth in the home care sector is largely inorganic (Abound acquired 14 businesses). Listeners are warned to "tread carefully" and recognize that the industry is filled with small, stable agencies that resist massive scaling.
- **The Power of Mission:** The service provided by home care businesses is deeply valuable to society. The mission component is cited as a critical element that helps the operators navigate the difficult days of running the business.
- **Operational vs. Advisory Roles:** Jenna’s career pivot illustrates a common entrepreneurial frustration: the desire to be *in* the business (operations) rather than just advising *on* the business. She found fulfillment in roles where she could manage teams and make tangible decisions.
- **The Operator's Perspective:** The related webinar emphasizes that the owner-operator relationship is the core challenge in acquisitions. Success hinges on aligning expectations, building trust early, and establishing clear communication and accountability structures.
- **Unit Economics Failures:** The search fund journey was initially derailed by a failed business idea (craft butter) due to poor unit economics, demonstrating that market viability and financial modeling must precede passion or innovation.
