# How I Pulled Off a $52M Acquisition | with Acquiring Minds — The Sweaty Startup

## Summary
The episode centers on Nick Huber, detailing his journey from a self-storage entrepreneur to a multi-industry business owner, culminating in the acquisition of majority interest in the international recruiting company, Somewhere.com. Huber’s background establishes him as a highly credible operator who built wealth not through venture capital or cutting-edge tech, but through mastering "boring businesses" and operational excellence.

Huber’s core thesis challenges the prevailing media narrative that equates entrepreneurship solely with tech, VC, and Silicon Valley hype. He argues that the vast majority of generational wealth is built through "sweaty startups"—logistically challenging, local, and often overlooked industries. His experience demonstrates a pattern of starting small (storage units), scaling through intense operational difficulty, and then leveraging that operational expertise into larger, international ventures.

The discussion highlights the importance of continuous skill acquisition and the power of international scaling. He details building a massive, geographically diverse workforce (Bogota, Brazil, Mexico City, etc.) and establishing multiple revenue streams, including a cost segregation firm (RE Cost Seg) generating over $750,000 monthly, alongside his core self-storage and recruiting businesses.

## Key Points
- **The "Sweaty Startup" Thesis:** 95% of wealthy people who built generational wealth did not follow the tech/VC path. They succeeded by out-operating and out-executing in "boring businesses" (e.g., logistics, local services).
- **Operational Skill Over Idea:** Huber emphasizes that the goal of early businesses is not the money, but the *skillset* gained from solving brutally hard logistical problems. This operational mastery is the true value.
- **The Power of the Local/Boring:** The most common epiphany for people discovering wealth building is realizing that the path is not limited to tech giants, often stemming from observing wealth generation at local country clubs or in local services.
- **International Scaling Strategy:** His current portfolio includes 325 employees across 11 international locations (Bogota, Brazil, etc.), demonstrating a model for scaling operations globally using diverse, non-tech labor pools.
- **Financial Milestones:**
    *   Initial self-storage facility: $2.9 million all-in basis (now worth $10 million).
    *   Current self-storage portfolio: 63 properties.
    *   Cost Segregation Firm (RE Cost Seg): Generating over $750,000+ in monthly revenue.
    *   Acquisition: Closed the deal to acquire majority interest in Somewhere.com on May 1, 2024.
