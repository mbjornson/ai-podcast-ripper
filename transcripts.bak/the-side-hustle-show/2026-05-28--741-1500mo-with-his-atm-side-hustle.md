# 741: $1500/mo With His ATM Side Hustle — The Side Hustle Show

## Summary
The episode details a niche, high-return side hustle: deploying automated teller machines (ATMs). The guest, Will Butterton of VikingVendorsLLC.com, reports generating approximately $1,500 per month using a fleet of five machines, requiring only about an hour of work per week. While the concept appears simple—placing a money-making robot in high-traffic areas—the execution is highly complex, requiring specialized knowledge of banking regulations, transaction processing, and local business pitches.

Will's background as an engineer and father provides the context for this side venture. He emphasizes that the primary hurdles are not physical, but regulatory and logistical. The biggest initial challenge is securing a business bank account, as large national banks are wary of the industry due to historical associations with money laundering. Success hinges on navigating these banking and processing requirements to establish a legitimate, functional operation.

## Key Points
- **Profit Source is the Surcharge Fee:** The entire profit margin in an ATM business is the surcharge fee charged to the user (e.g., $3 per transaction).
- **Bank Account Difficulty:** Large national banks are often resistant to ATM businesses due to historical federal investigations (money laundering, etc.). It is critical to secure a local bank or credit union that explicitly allows ATM transaction processing.
- **Startup Costs:** A realistic starting investment per machine is approximately $4,500. This includes the ATM unit itself ($2,500–$3,000 for models like Gen Mega or Hyosung) plus supplies, parts, and initial cash loading.
- **Processor Selection is Paramount:** The most crucial step after banking is finding an ATM processor that does *not* take a percentage cut of the surcharge fee. This ensures the operator retains the entire profit margin.
- **Pitching Strategy:** The business model relies on pitching to cash-only businesses. The operator can offer a lower surcharge fee (a convenience to the business's customers) in exchange for avoiding profit-sharing agreements.
- **Scaling Warning:** The business quickly loses its "passive" nature as the fleet grows. Scaling requires constant attention to maintenance, cash replenishment, and operational reliability.

## Action Items
- [ ] **Legal/Finance:** Establish an LLC and obtain an EIN *before* purchasing equipment or seeking locations.
- [ ] **Banking:** Identify and approach local banks or credit unions (not large national chains) to secure a business bank account specifically authorized for ATM transaction processing.
- [ ] **Processing:** Research ATM processors and prioritize those that explicitly state they take 0% of the surcharge fee.
- [ ] **Investment:** Budget a minimum of $4,500 per machine to cover the unit cost, supplies, and initial cash load.
- [ ] **Location Scouting:** Focus on identifying cash-only, long-established, family-owned businesses that do not currently have an ATM installed.
- [ ] **Pitch Development:** Develop a low-surcharge fee pitch, emphasizing the convenience to the business's customers, rather than offering profit-sharing (which requires raising the surcharge fee).
