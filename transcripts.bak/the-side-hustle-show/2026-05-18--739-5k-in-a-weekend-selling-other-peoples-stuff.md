# 739: $5k in a Weekend Selling Other People’s Stuff — The Side Hustle Show

## Summary
This episode of “The Side Hustle Show” features Sue Antonoro, a long-time listener and estate sale specialist, discussing her business model of leveraging live shopping through Whatnot to sell other people’s belongings. Antonoro’s credibility stems from 10-20 years of experience in consignment, eBay, and estate sales, demonstrated by a recent two-part auction that grossed $17,000. The core of her business is efficiently clearing out estates and downsizing homes using a streamlined, online auction process facilitated by the platform Whatnot. The episode highlights the growing popularity of live shopping and the potential for individuals to build profitable side hustles by capitalizing on this trend.

## Key Points
- **Whatnot’s Explosive Growth:** Whatnot is experiencing rapid growth as a live shopping platform, offering sellers direct engagement with buyers and significantly higher sales rates (10x more than major marketplaces).
- **Buyer Engagement:** Whatnot buyers spend over an hour a day on the app, indicating strong engagement and repeat purchasing behavior, a key driver of seller success.
- **Efficiency & Streamlined Process:** Antonoro’s process focuses on speed and convenience for homeowners, minimizing their involvement and reducing the hassle of traditional estate sales. This includes photographing, cataloging, and listing items online within a week.
- **Auction Ninja Platform:** She utilizes Auction Ninja, a third-party platform, to manage the live auction, incorporating features like rolling closings (closing bids every 20 seconds) for increased engagement and competitive bidding.
- **Target Audience & Demand:** There’s a significant demand for estate sales services, particularly among older homeowners seeking a less intrusive and more efficient alternative to traditional sales.
- **Reserve Pricing Caution:** Antonoro advises against using reserve prices, as they can hinder sales, particularly with a motivated audience like the Whatnot community.
- **Reseller Market:** A significant portion of her buyers are resellers who are actively seeking inventory for their own businesses, driving up demand for high-value items.
- **High-Value Item Potential:** The auction ninja platform has demonstrated the ability to generate significant revenue from unique items, like a signed Barry Sanders Jersey, highlighting the potential for substantial profits.

## Tools & Resources
- **Whatnot:** A live shopping platform where sellers can sell directly to buyers in real-time. (whatnot.com)
- **Auction Ninja:** A third-party platform used to manage the live auctions. (auctionninja.com)
- **eBay:** Used for shipping and potentially for some resellers.
