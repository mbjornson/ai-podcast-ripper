# I Bought a Side Hustle and  Tripled Revenue in 1 Year (Greatest Hits) — The Side Hustle Show

## Summary
This episode of “The Side Hustle Show” features Jono, a guest who successfully tripled his revenue in one year by purchasing a profitable laundromat business. The core of the discussion centers around Jono’s strategy for finding, vetting, and scaling a small, local business – specifically, a self-service laundromat – as a side hustle. The episode highlights the importance of finding undervalued assets, leveraging personal skills, and focusing on quick wins. Jono’s approach is particularly appealing for listeners interested in tangible, income-generating businesses with relatively low startup costs. The episode also touches on the importance of due diligence, understanding market dynamics, and adapting to local customer preferences.

## Key Points
- **Live Shopping Opportunity:** The episode begins by introducing “Whatnot,” a live shopping platform, as a potential avenue for sellers, emphasizing real-time connections and higher sales volumes (10x higher than major marketplaces). This sets a broader context for Jono’s success – a focus on direct customer engagement.
- **Buying a Profitable Business:** Jono’s strategy was to buy an already profitable laundromat, rather than starting from scratch, allowing him to immediately generate revenue and build a customer base.
- **Two-Year Multiple Valuation:** Laundromats are typically valued on a multiple of annual income, with Jono’s purchase based on a two-year multiple of approximately $40,000 in annual revenue, costing him $83,000.
- **Due Diligence – Cash Flow Verification:** Jono employed a hands-on approach to due diligence, spending three to four hours a day observing and counting machine usage to verify the seller’s reported income. This highlights the importance of independent verification.
- **Quick Wins & Adaptations:** Jono’s immediate improvements included installing a pay-pass system (a critical change for the local market) and creating a professional website, demonstrating the value of adapting to customer needs and leveraging digital tools.
- **Leveraging Skills:** Jono’s background in marketing automation proved valuable in optimizing the laundromat’s operations and attracting customers.
- **Lease Security:** The security of a long-term lease is crucial in the laundromat business, as relocating equipment is a significant hurdle.
