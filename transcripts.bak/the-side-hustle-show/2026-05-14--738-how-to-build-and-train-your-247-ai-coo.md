# 738: How to Build and Train Your 24/7 AI COO — The Side Hustle Show

## Summary
This episode of The Side Hustle Show features Nick Rutherford, a 13-year veteran of the podcast, discussing his experience building and training an AI operator, nicknamed “Rocky,” using the OpenClaw software. The core idea is leveraging AI to automate tasks and significantly increase output, particularly for businesses already established. Rutherford’s credibility stems from his long-standing work in online education and his previous ventures like “Piano in 21 Days” and theonlinecourseguy.com. The episode highlights a shift in AI adoption, moving beyond simple chat interfaces (Level 1) to more sophisticated automation (Levels 2 & 3) and ultimately, personal AI operators (Level 4) capable of proactive assistance. The discussion emphasizes the importance of context and personalization for AI effectiveness.

## Key Points
- **Live Shopping & Whatnot:** The episode begins by showcasing the success of the live shopping platform Whatnot, where sellers can build direct relationships with customers and achieve 10x higher sales compared to traditional marketplaces.
- **AI Operator (Rocky):** Rutherford’s AI operator, Rocky, is a Mac-based agent built using OpenClaw, automating tasks like email management, customer support, SEO, and daily reporting.
- **Level of AI Use:** Rutherford outlines four levels of AI use, progressing from basic chat interfaces (Level 1) to automated workflows (Level 2 & 3) and finally, personalized AI operators (Level 4).
- **Automation with Claude:** The key to Rocky’s effectiveness lies in integrating it with Claude, allowing for complex automations and real-time assistance.
- **Context & Personalization:** Rocky’s ability to provide relevant customer support stems from its access to Rutherford’s entire business history, including past customer support emails and course materials. This emphasizes the need for AI to be trained on specific context.
- **Security Considerations:** Running the AI agent locally on dedicated hardware (like a Mac Mini) is crucial for security, preventing unauthorized access to sensitive data.
- **OpenClaw Software:** OpenClaw is a free, open-source software that enables the creation of Level 4 AI operators. It’s technically complex to set up, but Rutherford suggests using Claude to assist with the process.

## Tools & Resources
- **Whatnot:** A live shopping platform where sellers can connect with buyers in real-time. (https://whatnot.com/)
- **OpenClaw:** Free, open-source AI agent software. (https://openclaw.ai/)
- **Claude:** An AI assistant (likely referring to Anthropic's Claude model) used for automation and assistance.
- **Make & Zapier:** Automation platforms used to connect different apps and services.
- **Mac Mini:** Recommended hardware for running OpenClaw due to its compatibility and resource efficiency.

## Quotable
> “He is better at customer support than me because he instantly has access to everything.” – Nick Rutherford, highlighting the superior efficiency of the AI operator.
> “It's like hiring a new employee. When you hire a new employee, you're not going to just let them share your computer with you. You're going to have a computer and your employees going to have a computer.” – Nick Rutherford, emphasizing the importance of security when using AI.

## Action Items
- **Explore Live Shopping:**  Investigate the Whatnot platform to see if it aligns with your product or service offering.
- **Experiment with AI Agents:**  Research and potentially try OpenClaw to explore the possibilities of Level 4 AI operators.
- **Automate Repetitive Tasks:** Identify tasks in your business that could be automated using tools like Zapier or Make.
- **Learn about Claude:**  Familiarize yourself with Claude and its capabilities for AI assistance and automation.
- **Prioritize Security:** If implementing an AI agent, prioritize local hardware and security best practices.
- **Start Small:** Begin with a limited scope of tasks for the AI agent to manage, gradually expanding its responsibilities as you gain confidence.
