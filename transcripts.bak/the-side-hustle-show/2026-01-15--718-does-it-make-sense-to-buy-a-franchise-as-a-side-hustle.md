# 718: Does it Make Sense to Buy a Franchise as a Side Hustle? — The Side Hustle Show

## Summary
The episode explores the viability of acquiring a franchise as a low-risk side hustle model for building wealth, arguing that franchises offer a proven playbook and lower failure rates compared to starting an independent startup. The guest, Greg Moore (author of *Real Freedom*), emphasizes that while the concept is appealing, potential franchisees must approach it with extreme due diligence. He advises against relying solely on marketing materials, stressing that "semi-passive" operation requires deep investigation by speaking directly with at least ten current operators in the specific field.

The core strategy presented involves identifying essential, fragmented service industries (like HVAC, plumbing, and restoration) where market share is currently lacking. The goal is not just to open a single location but to build a scalable system—a regional or national presence—by hiring managers for multiple units, allowing the owner to transition into an oversight/investor role rather than a day-to-day operator.

## Key Points
- **Due Diligence Rule:** Never trust marketing material alone when assessing "semi-passive" potential; speak directly with at least 10 current franchisees operating in that specific business model.
- **Ideal Entry Point:** Focus on service industries (HVAC, plumbing, electrical, restoration) because they are essential daily needs and are highly fragmented, meaning no single player has a dominant market share.
- **Scalability Model (The "Shaq" Approach):** To achieve semi-passive income, the goal is to acquire multiple locations and then hire regional managers for groups of units (e.g., hiring a manager for every 7 salons) so that you only oversee operations and key metrics, rather than performing daily tasks.
- **Operational Non-Negotiable:** When evaluating service franchises, confirm they have an established call center system to field calls and schedule appointments; do not assume your personal time will be spent on lead generation or customer intake.
- **Risk Mitigation Stat:** Out of the estimated 4,000 franchises in the U.S., only about 5% to 10% are large enough to have 100 or more franchisees already, indicating a proven model and greater stability.

## Tools & Resources
- **FranchiseMaven.com:** The guest's platform for connecting potential investors with franchise opportunities.
- **Authority Brands / Neighborly Brands:** Examples of large, successful groups that consolidate multiple related services (e.g., Mr. Sparky, Molly Maids) under one umbrella, providing systemic power and support to new franchisees.
- **BELFOR:** Mentioned as a global leader in restoration services (including post-disaster cleanup), demonstrating the value of joining an established industry giant.
