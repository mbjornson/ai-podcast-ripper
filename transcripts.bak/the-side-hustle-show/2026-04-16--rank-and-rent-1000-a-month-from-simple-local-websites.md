# Rank and Rent: $1000 a Month From Simple Local Websites — The Side Hustle Show

## Summary
The episode introduces the "Rank and Rent" business model, a scalable side hustle where you generate leads for local service businesses and rent them out for a flat monthly fee. The model involves selecting a specific, underserved local niche, building a generic-sounding website optimized for that keyword (e.g., "window washing Livermore"), and achieving high Google rankings. Once ranked, you partner with a qualified service provider who pays you a recurring monthly income for the steady stream of leads, effectively treating your website as a digital rental property.

The core strategy revolves around identifying "low-hanging fruit"—niches and locations that are profitable but lack high competition. The speaker emphasizes that the value proposition is not just the leads, but the ability to consistently rank above established local competitors who often lack modern digital marketing skills. This approach is presented as a highly scalable model that can be replicated across different geographic areas and niches.

## Key Points
- **The Model:** Build a local SEO website for a service niche $\rightarrow$ Rank highly on Google $\rightarrow$ Partner with a service provider $\rightarrow$ Collect a flat monthly "rent" payment for leads.
- **Ideal Niche Selection:** Focus on blue-collar, phone-driven services (e.g., tree services, stump grinding).
- **Location Sweet Spot:** Target mid-sized cities with populations between **50,000 and 400,000**. This range offers enough call volume to justify a good price without the extreme competition of major metros or the low volume of small towns.
- **Critical Avoidances:**
    *   **High Competition:** Avoid saturated niches like lawyers, HVAC, or roofing.
    *   **Seasonality:** Avoid services that are only profitable for a few months (e.g., snow removal), as this makes securing stable, recurring payments difficult.
    *   **Licensing:** Try to avoid niches that require specific state licensing, as this adds legal complexity.
- **Competitive Edge:** The primary advantage is the SEO skill set, allowing the creator to outrank established local businesses (even those at the top for 20 years) relatively quickly.
- **Lead Value:** The goal is to secure a recurring, stable income stream, treating the leads like a predictable monthly bill rather than a one-time project.

## Tools & Resources
- **Wikipedia:** Useful for generating lists of U.S. states and cities with population sizes to narrow down target markets.
- **Thumbtack.com / HomeAdvisor:** Good sources for brainstorming and discovering obscure blue-collar service niches.
- **Google AdWord Planner:** A tool for estimating search volume and potential value of keywords.
- **Keyword Surfer (Free):** A Chrome extension for getting a general idea of keyword search volume.
- **Google Search Tactics:** When researching, look for local businesses in the Google Maps/Local Pack that have no website or few reviews, as this indicates a "break in the chain" and an easier opportunity to rank.
