# 723: How to Make 5-10x More Money from Amazon — The Side Hustle Show

## Summary
The episode shifts the focus of Amazon influencing from a high-volume, low-commission "review video game" to a targeted, high-leverage campaign model utilizing the Creator Connections program. The guest, Jared Bauman, highlights that the traditional method of simply uploading product reviews for generic commissions (2-3%) is saturated and yielding diminishing returns. Instead, creators should pivot to actively engaging with brands through this specialized platform.

Creator Connections allows influencers to participate in specific brand campaigns offering significantly higher commission rates—ranging from 10% up to 40%. The core strategy involves treating the process as a "messaging game" rather than a content creation volume game. Success requires proactive outreach, detailed communication with brands, and most critically, filtering opportunities based on product sales velocity, ensuring that high commissions are only pursued for products that are already selling well.

## Key Points
- **Strategic Pivot:** The old strategy of mass-producing review videos is obsolete due to market saturation; the new focus must be Creator Connections campaigns.
- **Commission Rates:** Campaigns offer significantly higher commission rates (e.g., 10% minimum, up to 40%) compared to the typical 2-3% standard Amazon affiliate rate.
- **The Sales Filter (Crucial Insight):** Commission percentage is irrelevant if the product does not sell. The absolute priority must be identifying products with proven sales history and high demand/velocity.
- **Proactive Outreach:** If a creator lacks existing campaigns for their niche, they should proactively message brands offering Creator Connections campaigns. Treat this as a business pitch, detailing expertise (e.g., "I have 1,500 videos on X topic") and outlining the content plan (YouTube + Amazon storefront within 1-2 weeks).
- **Status Signaling:** Leveraging your status level (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum) in initial outreach helps validate credibility and suggests longevity in the program.
- **The Volume Game Shift:** The "volume game" has transitioned from creating videos to sending highly targeted, professional messages to brands.

## Tools & Resources
- **Amazon Influencer Program / Creator Connections:** The primary platform for accessing high-commission brand campaigns.
- **ASIN (Amazon Standard Identification Number):** Essential for precise communication with brands; always include the specific product SKU when pitching a campaign.
- **Oink / Viral View:** Paid Chrome extensions recommended for helping creators identify products that are selling well and tracking sales velocity within the Amazon Influencer program.

>

## Action Items
- [ ] **Audit Existing Content:** Search Creator Connections for campaigns related to products or niches where you already have video content/expertise.
- [ ] **Implement Proactive Outreach:** Identify 5-10 brands with open Creator Connection campaigns and send detailed pitches, specifying the exact ASINs and outlining your planned distribution channels (YouTube, Amazon storefront).
- [ ] **Prioritize Sales Velocity:** Before accepting any campaign, manually check the product listing or use a tool (like Oink/Viral View) to confirm the product has strong sales history ("sold X units last month").
- [ ] **Develop Pitch Template:** Create a standardized outreach message that includes your status level, total video count, specific ASINs, and a clear commitment timeline for content creation.
