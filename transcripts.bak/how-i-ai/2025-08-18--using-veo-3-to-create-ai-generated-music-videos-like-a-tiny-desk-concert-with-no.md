# Using Veo 3 to create AI-generated music videos, like a Tiny Desk Concert with Notorious B.I.G. and Kurt Cobain — How I AI

## Summary
The episode shifts away from typical B2B AI discussions, focusing instead on how advanced generative AI can be used for highly creative and fun projects, specifically creating music videos. The discussion highlights that modern AI tools are not just replacements for human creativity but rather powerful extensions of our artistic toolkit, opening up new avenues previously constrained by physical limitations (like the inability to separate vocals from instruments in a mixed track).

The core insight is that AI's current state represents the next major evolution of "remix culture." Just as sampling was foundational to hip-hop and mixtapes, generative AI allows users to remix not just audio, but entire video aesthetics, character performances, and historical figures. The guest demonstrates a detailed workflow for creating an elaborate, high-quality music video (e.g., Notorious B.I.G. at a Tiny Desk Concert) using multiple specialized tools in sequence.

The conversation emphasizes that the most powerful results come from combining these tools—using one model's output (a still image) as the input for another (video generation/lip-syncing), creating an iterative, multi-stage creative pipeline.

## Key Points
- **AI is the next manifestation of remix culture:** The speaker argues that AI’s ability to disentangle audio tracks and generate new video assets makes it fundamentally similar in importance to how sampling was to hip-hop 40 years ago. This reframes AI from a mere technology into a cultural creative movement.
- **The power of multi-stage prompting/workflow:** The process is not single-tool magic; it requires chaining tools: Start with GPT-4o for initial image generation (prompt adherence), extract audio, and then use specialized video models (like Hydra or VideoGen) to animate the still frame while syncing custom audio.
- **GPT-4o's strength in prompt adherence:** The model is praised for its ability to accurately follow complex instructions and generate specific details—a critical feature over older diffusion models that struggled with text/spelling (e.g., correctly spelling "NPR" in the generated image).
- **Custom audio integration is underestimated:** A major insight is how useful it is to take custom, external audio (like a live cover band track) and apply it to an AI-generated video asset, allowing for creative control over both the visual style and the sound source.

## Tools & Resources
- **GPT-4o:** Recommended as the best general-purpose multimodal model for initial image generation due to its strong prompt adherence and fine-grained manipulation capabilities.
- **Hydra / Sync Labs / VideoGen:** Specialized tools used for taking a still frame (image) and generating an animated video that includes custom audio and lip synchronization.
- **Notion AI:** Highlighted as an essential "do-everything" tool, particularly its AI meeting notes feature, which is useful for summarizing action items from meetings or interviews.
- **AI Products Giveaway:** The host promotes a giveaway for free access to various AI tools (VZero, Replit, Lovable, Bolt, Cursor, ChatPRD).
