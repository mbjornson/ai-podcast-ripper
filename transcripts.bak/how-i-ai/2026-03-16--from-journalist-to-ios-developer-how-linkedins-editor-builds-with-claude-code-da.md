# From journalist to iOS developer: How LinkedIn’s editor builds with Claude Code | Daniel Roth — How I AI

## Summary
This episode features Daniel Roth, a former journalist who transitioned into iOS development using Claude Code, as he details his unconventional approach to building apps. Roth’s background as a business writer and editor provides a unique perspective, highlighting the shift in thinking required to embrace rapid iteration and experimentation. He describes a system centered around personalized app development, driven by user feedback and a willingness to build quickly, even if the initial results aren’t perfect. The core of his process involves using Claude Code to rapidly prototype ideas and build a community around his apps, showcasing a pragmatic and somewhat chaotic approach to software development. The episode is largely about the mindset shift required to build, rather than a deep dive into technical specifics.

## Key Points
- **Personalized Product Development:** Roth’s primary focus is building apps based on *his* specific needs and the needs of a small community, exemplified by the Commutely app for NYC train runners. This reflects a shift from traditional product development focused on broad market appeal to a highly tailored approach.
- **Role of the “VibeCoder”:** Roth identifies himself as a “VibeCoder,” prioritizing personal desires and user feedback over rigid PM or architectural constraints. He acts as a “picky customer,” driving the development process based on immediate needs and desires.
- **Shift in Thinking:** The episode emphasizes a fundamental shift in mindset – moving from a traditional, top-down approach to a more iterative, user-driven one. Roth’s journey mirrors the broader trend of empowering individuals to build and experiment with AI tools.
- **Leveraging Claude Code:** Claude Code is central to Roth’s workflow, acting as a rapid prototyping tool and a means of translating user requests into functional code. He utilizes Claude’s ability to generate code snippets and iterate quickly.
- **Building a Community:** Roth emphasizes the importance of building a community around his apps, using feedback to inform development and fostering a sense of ownership among users.
- **Lean Development & Modularization:** Roth advocates for a “lean builder” approach, prioritizing modular code and avoiding spaghetti code to maintain a manageable development process.
- **Marketing as Development:** Roth highlights the importance of marketing and distribution alongside development, recognizing that building an app is only half the battle.
