# “Vibe analysis”: How Faire’s data team uses AI to investigate conversion drops, analyze experiment results, and convert raw data into executive-ready insights — How I AI

## Summary
The episode details how modern AI tools are fundamentally changing the process of product analysis, moving the focus from manual data crunching to sophisticated context gathering. The discussion, featuring data experts from FAIR, highlights that the most valuable skill in analytics is no longer the ability to manipulate data, but the ability to ask the right questions, form hypotheses, and understand the full business context.

The core problem addressed is the sheer volume of internal data—spanning PRDs, Slack threads, Jira tickets, and codebases—that makes investigating a sudden drop in a key metric (like a conversion rate) nearly impossible without advanced AI assistance. AI tools allow users to perform "Enterprise AI Search," synthesizing information across disparate systems to quickly generate a list of potential causes and hypotheses.

Furthermore, the discussion emphasizes that AI democratizes technical access. Non-engineers can now use specialized tools to query a company's actual codebase, providing deep insights into *how* a feature was implemented, which is a critical step beyond simply reading a product requirement document (PRD).

## Key Points
- **Context is King:** The most important, and often most difficult, part of analysis is gathering the right context and knowing what questions to ask. This separates good analysis from bad.
- **Enterprise AI Search:** AI tools (like those integrated into Notion) can search across an entire organization's knowledge base (Slack, Notion, Jira, PRDs) to identify potential causes for a metric drop. For example, by asking what experiments or features launched between specific dates that could have added friction to the checkout process, the AI instantly generates a list of hypotheses, saving hours of manual document review.
- **The Codebase as a Data Source:** AI tools (specifically Cursor in deep research mode) allow non-technical product managers to query the actual product code base. This capability provides a level of technical detail that was previously restricted to engineers, enabling a deeper understanding of implementation changes.
- **AI Orchestration for Scale:** Using platforms like Zapier allows users to build complex, multi-step AI workflows that automate processes across an entire organization (e.g., automatically responding to leads with enriched data or drafting emails), moving AI from a novelty to a scalable operational asset.
- **Vibe Analysis:** The concept of "Vibe analysis" is emerging, suggesting that the analysis of product quality and usage must go beyond simple conversion rates to capture the overall user experience and feeling.

## Tools & Resources
- **Notion (Enterprise AI Search):** Used for querying internal documents (PRDs, launch announcements, etc.) across multiple sources (Slack, Jira, Notion) to generate hypotheses.
- **Cursor:** A specialized AI tool used for deep research directly against a connected codebase, allowing non-engineers to understand technical implementation details.
- **ChatGPT:** Used for general AI querying and research.
- **Zapier:** An AI orchestration platform used to connect disparate apps (nearly 8,000) to automate complex, multi-step business workflows.
- **Jira/Slack/Notion:** Mentioned as critical data sources that AI tools can now search simultaneously.
