# From Figma to Claude Code and back | Gui Seiz & Alex Kern (Figma) — How I AI

## Summary
This episode features a conversation between Gui Seiz and Alex Kern of Figma, discussing the evolving relationship between design and code, and how AI tools like Claude Code and the Figma MCP are streamlining workflows. The core argument is that traditional, linear design and development processes are becoming increasingly inefficient, particularly as product complexity grows. The duo highlights the shift towards a more fluid, iterative approach where design and code can be seamlessly integrated, driven by AI agents that bridge the gap between these disciplines. Their credibility stems from their direct involvement in building and scaling Figma, a leading design tool, and their practical experience navigating the challenges of modern product development.

## Key Points
- **MCP as a Connector:** The Figma MCP (Machine Code Processor) acts as a crucial connector, allowing designers to directly import code-based designs (like Figma states) into Figma for collaborative editing. This addresses the common problem of design files falling out of sync with the codebase.
- **Parallel Tracks & Divergent Truths:** Design and code often operate on separate “tracks,” leading to divergent sources of truth. The MCP aims to resolve this by providing a unified view of design and code assets.
- **AI-Powered Automation:** AI agents, like those offered by Optimizely Opal, are automating repetitive tasks like content creation and compliance checks, freeing up marketers to focus on strategic initiatives.
- **Shifting Resource Scarcity:** Previously, design and engineering were considered scarce and expensive resources. AI is democratizing access to design capabilities, allowing more people to contribute to the design process.
- **Increased Exploration Capacity:** The ability to quickly prototype and iterate thanks to AI reduces the risk associated with lengthy, linear workflows. This fosters greater divergence and exploration of ideas.
- **Workflow Reinvention:** Teams are constantly reinventing their workflows to adapt to the rapid pace of change in the software development landscape.
- **Sandwich of Code:** The “sandwich of code” – code, design, code – represents a new workflow where code is used to generate design elements, which are then refined in Figma.
