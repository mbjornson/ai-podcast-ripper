# Gamma’s head of design shares how his small team uses AI to synthesize feedback, generate on-brand imagery, and maintain design quality while serving users in 60+ countries | Zach Leach — How I AI

## Summary
Zach Leach, head of design at Gamma, details how his small team leverages advanced AI capabilities to manage the complexities of product development for a highly international user base. Gamma is an AI-powered presentation tool that serves users in over 60 countries, meaning the design and feature set must account for massive global diversity and linguistic variation.

The core challenge discussed is synthesizing actionable insights from large volumes of unstructured, multilingual customer feedback—specifically citing 550 individual responses collected over a single week regarding new features like AI image editing. Manually processing this data, especially across multiple languages (with up to 40% being non-English), was impossible.

Leach demonstrates how using advanced tools like ChatGPT's deep research functionality transforms raw feedback into structured, actionable data. This process allows the design team not only to identify what features are working or failing but also to pinpoint specific prompts and edits that resonate globally, enabling them to maintain high design quality while scaling their product internationally.

## Key Points
- **Scaling Insights from Volume:** The speaker processed over 550 individual pieces of multilingual user feedback in a week. Before AI tools, this would have required manual review or limited analysis (e.g., only reviewing the top 20 English responses).
- **AI as Deep Researcher/Data Analyst:** Advanced prompting allows for deep research on unstructured data files. The process involves asking the AI to "Analyze it... figure out what we're doing well, what we're not doing well," which goes far beyond simple keyword matching or basic scripting.
- **Multilingual Classification:** The AI can perform classification and summarization across multiple languages (e.g., Turkish feedback alongside English), providing a unified view of global user sentiment regardless of the source language.
- **Structured Data Extraction:** After deep research, the process allows for further prompting to classify data into structured formats (like spreadsheets) based on criteria such as rating or category, which can then be fed into other tools (like Gamma itself) to generate presentations and reports.
- **Art Direction Scaling:** AI image generation models (MidJourney) are used not just for creation but for maintaining consistent art direction and allowing designers to "explore rabbit holes of creativity" faster than traditional methods.

## Tools & Resources
- **ChatGPT/Deep Research Functionality:** Used for deep data analysis, classification, summarization, and cross-lingual sentiment analysis on large datasets (e.g., 550+ feedback responses).
- **MidJourney:** Utilized in the design workflow to ensure consistent art direction and rapidly generate creative elements for user experience improvements.
- **Gamma:** The product itself; an AI-powered presentation tool that serves as a platform for both generating content and displaying research findings (e.g., turning data into a presentation).

## Action Items
- [ ] **Develop Advanced Prompting Skills:** Practice using AI tools to perform complex data classification and trend analysis on large, unstructured datasets (e.g., customer feedback, survey responses) rather than relying on simple summarization prompts.
- [ ] **Implement a Global Feedback Loop:** If managing a product or service with international users, establish a systematic process for feeding multilingual user input into an AI model for automated cross-lingual sentiment analysis and categorization.
- [ ] **Integrate Generative AI in Design Workflow:** Experiment with tools like MidJourney to move beyond simple image generation and use the output as part of a structured art direction system that maintains brand consistency while allowing for rapid creative iteration.
