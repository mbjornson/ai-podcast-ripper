# The exact AI playbook (using MCPs, custom GPTs, Granola) that saved ElevenLabs $100k+ and helps them ship daily | Luke Harries (Head of Growth) — How I AI

## Summary
Luke Harries, Head of Growth at ElevenLabs, details how his team has implemented sophisticated AI workflows to automate marketing, content generation, and product launch processes. The core thesis is that in an era of exponential software production, success hinges not on building features, but on efficient distribution—a concept he terms "Vibe Marketing." He demonstrates a highly structured process for turning raw customer interviews into polished assets (case studies, blog posts, tweets) using interconnected AI tools, saving the company significant time and agency costs.

The discussion highlights moving beyond simple content generation to orchestrating entire business workflows. Luke emphasizes that modern marketing requires integrating multiple systems—transcription, summarization, tone enforcement, and multi-format output—all managed through custom AI layers. This approach allows companies to treat every piece of content (even a case study) as a full "launch," maximizing its reach across various channels while maintaining brand consistency.

## Key Points
- **The Shift from Product Focus to Distribution:** The most critical insight is that product development alone is insufficient; success depends on getting the product into the market and ensuring people know about it. Luke frames the role of the AI CMO as automating the entire launch process, including ad campaign setup (e.g., Google Ads) and evergreen channel testing.
- **The Case Study Automation Funnel:** The team uses a specific three-step workflow: 1) Record/Interview using **Granola** (transcription/context capture); 2) Summarize/Enrich the data; 3) Feed the summary into a highly customized **GPT** ("11 Labs copy editor") that enforces brand voice, tone, and format rules before generating multi-format assets (blog post headers, tweets).
- **Process Efficiency in Keynote Prototyping:** ElevenLabs utilizes its own Studio Flow to prototype high-stakes internal events. Instead of expensive rehearsals, they load a script into the tool and generate audio prototypes with different voices/accents. This allows them to quickly test timing ("Do we have enough content?") and narrative flow before committing resources.
- **The Power of Custom GPT Instructions:** The effectiveness of their process relies heavily on building a custom GPT that is fed not just instructions, but *examples* (past blog posts, tweets) and a detailed "Tone Voice Guide." This moves the AI from being a general writer to a specialized, brand-compliant editor.
- **Financial Impact:** By automating these processes, they have saved tens of thousands of dollars in agency costs and significant internal time spent on manual content iteration and meeting preparation.

## Tools & Resources
- **Granola:** A transcription/note-taking tool used to capture raw interview data for case studies or meetings. It excels at auto-summarization and context enrichment (e.g., pulling job titles from an email).
- **Custom GPTs:** Customized chatbots that are given specific instructions, tone guides, and examples of desired output. This is key for maintaining brand voice consistency across all content types.
- **ElevenLabs Studio Flow:** A proprietary tool used to generate audio prototypes of scripts/keynotes with various voices and accents, ideal for pre-event testing.
- **Orkis Conductor (Sponsor):** An enterprise orchestration layer designed to manage complex, event-driven workflows involving APIs, microservices, human actions, and autonomous agents—a solution for modernizing legacy business processes.

## Action Items
- [ ] **Audit Your Content Workflow:** Map out how you currently create a case study or blog post. Identify every manual step (transcribing, summarizing, drafting, editing for tone) and determine where AI could automate it.
- [ ] **Develop a "Tone Guide" Prompt:** Create a detailed prompt for your own GPT/AI assistant that includes: 1) Your company's mission statement; 2) 3-5 examples of perfect past content (blog posts, tweets); and 3) Specific rules (e.g., always use American English spelling, maintain an expert but approachable tone).
- [ ] **Experiment with Audio Prototyping:** If you have a presentation or internal training script coming up, test using AI voice cloning/prototyping tools to check timing and flow before involving expensive human resources.
- [ ] **Focus on Orchestration (Enterprise Level):** For complex business processes that involve multiple systems (e.g., "When X happens in CRM, trigger Y workflow in marketing automation, then notify Z team"), research process orchestration platforms like Orkis Conductor to move beyond simple point-to-point integrations.
