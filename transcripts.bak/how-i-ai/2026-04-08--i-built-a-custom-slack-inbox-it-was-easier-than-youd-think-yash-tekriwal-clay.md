# I built a custom Slack inbox. It was easier than you’d think. | Yash Tekriwal (Clay) — How I AI

## Summary
This episode features Yash Tekriwal, a tech enthusiast and early adopter, discussing his experience building a custom Slack inbox using perplexity computer and open claw. The core problem he addresses is the overwhelming deluge of notifications in Slack, which he estimates to be 100-150 daily, with only 30-40 truly requiring his attention. Yash’s approach is driven by a desire for a more intentional workflow, prioritizing action-oriented notifications and minimizing distractions. He highlights the importance of categorizing notifications by urgency and type, advocating for a system that mirrors the way he wants to manage his time and tasks. The episode also touches on the broader issue of AI’s role in knowledge management, contrasting the potential for AI to simply answer questions with the need to build deterministic tools.

## Key Points
- **Notification Overload:** Yash experiences 100-150 Slack notifications daily, with 60-80% being FYI messages. This highlights a common pain point for busy professionals.
- **Kanban-Inspired Categorization:** He utilizes a Kanban-style approach to categorize notifications into “action required,” “need to read,” and “FYI” categories, mirroring a more organized workflow.
- **Deterministic Tooling:** Yash advocates for building deterministic tools (like his Slack digest) using code and APIs rather than solely relying on AI to answer questions. He believes this offers greater control and predictability.
- **Open Claw as a Coding Assistant:** He leverages open claw (an AI assistant) to automate the process of reverse-engineering Slack’s notification system and building the custom digest feed.
- **Discord as a Threading Platform:** Yash uses Discord, with its threading capabilities, to organize and manage the conversations and threads generated by his automated digest.
- **System Thinking:** The episode emphasizes the importance of “system thinking” – understanding the underlying processes and data that drive a system (in this case, Slack’s notification system) to build more effective solutions.
- **AI for Tool Building vs. Question Answering:** Yash distinguishes between using AI to build tools (like the Slack digest) and using AI to simply answer questions, arguing that the latter is often unreliable.

## Tools & Resources
- **Slack:** The primary platform for the problem and solution.
- **Perplexity Computer:** Yash’s custom-built tool for categorizing and prioritizing Slack notifications.
- **Open Claw:** An AI assistant (formerly Clay) used for coding and automating tasks.
- **Discord:** Used for organizing conversations and threads generated by the Slack digest.
- **Jarvis:** An open claw-powered agent within Slack that helps to build the digest.
- **Guru:** An AI-powered knowledge verification platform (mentioned briefly as a potential solution for inaccurate AI responses).
