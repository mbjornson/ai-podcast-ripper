# How to get your whole team excited about AI (and actually using it) | Brian Greenbaum (product designer at Pendo) — How I AI

## Summary
The episode discusses the strategic challenge of getting an entire organization, particularly product and design teams, excited and proficient in adopting AI tools. Brian Greenbaum, a product designer at Pendo, shares his personal journey and the structured approach he used to initiate this transformation. His background as a designer who became proficient in using AI tools (like Cursor) for rapid prototyping provides the core evidence for his claims.

Greenbaum's central thesis is that AI adoption requires more than just providing tools; it requires a proactive, organized effort to upskill the entire product organization—including PMs and designers—to leverage AI for efficiency and better decision-making. He frames this initiative not just as a technical necessity, but as a unique, high-impact leadership opportunity for early adopters.

The discussion provides a step-by-step plan for initiating an AI transformation, emphasizing that the first person to raise their hand and lead the effort gains significant professional and organizational influence.

## Key Points
- **The "Inception Phase" is Critical:** The initial stage of AI adoption is about generating excitement and energy across the team. This requires proactive communication and demonstrating tangible, impressive results (e.g., building a working prototype in hours using AI tools).
- **AI Upskilling Must Be Cross-Functional:** The need to uplevel skills must extend beyond just designers to include Product Managers (PMs) and the entire product organization.
- **The Two Pillars of Justification:** When advocating for AI investment, focus on two key areas:
    1. **Efficiency/Optimization:** How the team can use AI tools to get more done in fewer hours and with fewer resources.
    2. **Thought Leadership:** Positioning the company as an expert in the AI space within its vertical, which is crucial for market differentiation.
- **The Career Advantage of Initiative:** Leading an AI adoption initiative is described as a "promo making work" opportunity—a unique, high-impact leadership role that is rare and highly valuable for career growth.
- **Technical Capability:** Tools like Cursor were highlighted for their ability to rapidly generate working, code-based prototypes (PDFs, QR codes, etc.), overcoming the limitations of traditional, static prototyping tools like Figma when dealing with data-driven features.

## Tools & Resources
- **Cursor:** An AI-powered coding tool used by Greenbaum to quickly build a working prototype, demonstrating the power of AI for non-developers.
- **Google AI Studio (ai.dev):** The platform recommended for starting to build with Google's AI models.
- **Gemini 2.5 Family of Models:**
    - **2.5 Pro:** Best for reasoning over complex tasks.
    - **2.5 Flash:** Ideal balance between performance and price.
    - **2.5 Flash Lite:** Best for low-latency, high-volume tasks.

## Action Items
- [ ] **Develop a Proactive AI Initiative:** Identify a cross-functional group (PMs, Designers, Engineers) and propose leading an internal initiative to explore AI adoption, even if you are not formally assigned the role.
- [ ] **Master AI Prototyping:** Experiment with AI coding tools (like Cursor) to build functional, data-driven prototypes that go beyond the limitations of static design tools (like Figma).
- [ ] **Frame the Business Case:** When presenting AI ideas, structure the justification around two points: measurable efficiency gains (doing more with less) and establishing the company as a thought leader in the AI vertical.
- [ ] **Explore Google AI:** Start building small side projects using Google AI Studio and the Gemini 2.5 family of models to gain practical, hands-on experience.
