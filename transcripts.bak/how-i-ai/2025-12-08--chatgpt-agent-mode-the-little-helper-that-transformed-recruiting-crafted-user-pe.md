# ChatGPT agent mode: The “little helper” that transformed recruiting, crafted user personas, and solved parking nightmares | Michal Peled (Honeybook) — How I AI

## Summary
The episode focuses on "Agent Mode" in ChatGPT, an advanced feature that moves beyond simple text prompting, allowing the AI to perform multi-step actions and interact with external websites as if it were a human user. The guest, Michal Peled, a technical operations engineer at Honeybook, demonstrated how this capability can automate complex, repetitive professional workflows, using the example of a recruiter sourcing candidates on LinkedIn. The core discussion centers on how to structure prompts to transform the AI into a specialized "little helper" that replicates the specific, nuanced steps of a human expert.

Michal emphasizes that the true power of the agent lies in its ability to handle tasks that require logging into specific platforms and navigating complex user interfaces, rather than just searching publicly available data. The process is less about the AI's intelligence and more about the user's ability to accurately codify a professional's step-by-step workflow into a detailed prompt. This approach transforms the AI from a chatbot into a functional, autonomous digital assistant capable of handling the "mundane, thoughtless, manual repeating work" that consumes professional time.

## Key Points
- **The Power of Codifying Workflow:** The most effective way to build an agent prompt is to interview a Subject Matter Expert (SME) and ask them to detail their job step-by-step. The goal is to codify the process into a simple, repeatable prompt that can then be automated at scale.
- **Structured Prompting is Critical:** A successful prompt must contain three distinct elements: 1) **Role Definition** ("You are an IT recruiter"), 2) **Task Definition** (The goal, e.g., "Find up to five LinkedIn profiles"), and 3) **Specific Restrictions/Instructions** (The non-negotiable criteria, e.g., "Candidates must be from Israel," "Active in LinkedIn within the last three months").
- **Agentic Capabilities vs. Simple Search:** Agent Mode allows the AI to perform actions (clicking, logging in, navigating) rather than just generating text based on search results. This is crucial for proprietary or restricted data sources like LinkedIn.
- **The Agent as a Co-Pilot:** Agents do not have to run autonomously. The prompt can instruct the AI to pause and ask for human input ("Hey, wait, when you get to this point, let me take the next step"), making it a collaborative tool rather than a black box.
- **Accessibility and UX:** The technology's broader impact is making complex digital information and websites more accessible to non-technical users, including older generations or those unfamiliar with advanced filtering/UX design.

## Action Items
- [ ] **Process Mapping:** Identify one repetitive, manual task in your current job (e.g., data entry, research, initial screening) and map out every single step required to complete it.
- [ ] **SME Interview:** Interview a colleague or expert in your field and ask them to walk you through their most complex workflow, step-by-step, without skipping any details.
- [ ] **Prompt Engineering:** Structure a detailed prompt using the three-part model: 1) Role, 2) Task, 3) Specific Restrictions/Criteria.
- [ ] **Experiment with Agent Mode:** Test your mapped workflow using ChatGPT's agent mode (or similar automation tools) to see if it can replicate the steps autonomously.
- [ ] **Skill Focus:** Develop the skill of "workflow codification"—the ability to translate a human process into a precise, machine-readable set of instructions.
