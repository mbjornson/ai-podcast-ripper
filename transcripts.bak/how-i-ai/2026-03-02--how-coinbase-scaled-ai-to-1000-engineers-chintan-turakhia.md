# How Coinbase scaled AI to 1,000+ engineers | Chintan Turakhia — How I AI

## Summary
This episode features Chintan Turakhia, Senior Director of Engineering at Coinbase, discussing the challenges and successes of scaling AI adoption within a massive, 1,000+ engineer organization. The core argument is that simply introducing AI tools isn't enough; a fundamental shift in mindset, leadership conviction, and hands-on involvement are crucial for driving real impact. Turakhia emphasizes the importance of "adapt or die" in the face of rapidly evolving technology and the need for engineering leaders to actively engage with the tools they advocate for. The episode highlights the pitfalls of superficial AI adoption – checking the box without genuine integration – and advocates for a more strategic, iterative approach. A key takeaway is the need for engineering leaders to reconnect with coding and to foster a culture of experimentation and learning.

## Key Points
- **Scale & Skepticism:** Deploying AI at scale within large engineering organizations is challenging, leading to initial skepticism and a tendency for superficial adoption (e.g., "hello world" implementations).
- **Leadership Conviction is Critical:** A single, highly-convincing leader who is hands-on with the tools is essential for driving adoption. Telling alone isn't enough; engineers need to see the value firsthand.
- **Shift from Meetings to Code:** Engineering leaders need to prioritize coding and hands-on work over meetings and abstract strategy, fostering a culture of experimentation and learning.
- **Iterative Approach:** Successful AI adoption isn't a single event but a series of iterative steps, starting with low-hanging fruit (e.g., unit tests, linting) and gradually building confidence.
- **Model Maturity Matters:** The quality of the underlying AI models (e.g., large language models) is paramount. Early attempts with immature models often fail to deliver on their promise.
- **Deep System Access is Key:** AI tools require deep access to company systems (codebases, internal documentation) to be truly effective. This raises security concerns and necessitates enterprise-grade features.
- **Work OS as an Enabler:** Platforms like Work OS provide drop-in APIs that allow AI tools to seamlessly integrate with enterprise systems, addressing security concerns and accelerating adoption.
