# “I’m incapable of doing my job without AI”: How this top PM uses Claude + ChatGPT as his second brain — How I AI

## Summary
The episode features Amir Quine, an AI Product Manager at monday.com and a top performer in the field, who outlines how he has become highly dependent on advanced AI tools like Claude and ChatGPT to manage the overwhelming cognitive load of modern product management. His core thesis is that PMs suffer from extreme context switching—juggling multiple initiatives, requirements, and stakeholders' asks simultaneously. Instead of relying solely on human memory, Amir uses custom GPT "brains" to silo conversations and maintain comprehensive project context across dozens of files and threads.

Amir demonstrates a sophisticated use case: gathering unbiased market intelligence for building AI Agents within monday.com. Recognizing that internal company narratives are often biased, he used AI to programmatically scrape vast amounts of external data (34,000 rows from Reddit) to understand what customers *actually* want regarding the future of AI agents in SaaS—a critical step for making unbiased product decisions.

## Key Points
- **The Context Switching Problem:** PM roles inherently require holding "a million different threads" and managing constant context switching across diverse initiatives, which is unsustainable for human memory alone.
- **Siloing Knowledge with AI Agents:** The solution is to create independent, fully loaded conversational "brains" (Custom GPTs/AI agents) that act as external memory banks, allowing the PM to offload complex project context and historical data into a structured system.
- **Programmatic Market Research:** Instead of manually performing limited searches on platforms like Reddit or Twitter, the process involves defining a broad, ambitious problem ("I want to know everything everyone is saying about X"). The AI (Claude) then narrows this scope, identifies accessible data sources (e.g., Reddit API), and generates a step-by-step technical guide and executable script for scraping thousands of conversations.
- **Data Volume as Insight:** Successfully scraped 34,000 rows of conversation data from Reddit, providing an unbiased, quantitative view of market interest that superseded internal assumptions or limited manual research.

## Tools & Resources
- **AI Platforms:** Claude (specifically used for generating technical guides and writing scraping scripts) and ChatGPT/Custom GPTs (used for building the "brains" to hold context).
- **Data Source:** Reddit API/Scraping methods (demonstrated as a way to gather massive, unbiased user feedback).
- **Productivity Framework:** The concept of creating "independent fully loaded brains" or knowledge silos for specific projects.

## Quotable

## Action Items
- [ ] **Skill Development:** Practice defining massive, ambiguous business problems ("What do people *actually* care about?") and using advanced LLMs (like Claude) not just for answers, but for generating technical implementation plans (e.g., "Give me a step-by-step guide on how to scrape X data").
- [ ] **Career Move:** Implement a "Context Brain" system in your current role: For every major project or initiative, dedicate a custom GPT/knowledge base that ingests all relevant PRDs, meeting notes, and stakeholder feedback, making it the single source of truth for that project.
- [ ] **Tool to Try:** Experiment with creating Custom GPTs that are fed large amounts of proprietary company data (e.g., 20 files worth of historical product documentation) to act as a dedicated "Project Memory."
- [ ] **Mindset Practice:** When facing a complex problem, resist the urge to rely only on internal knowledge or stakeholder consensus; instead, use AI tools to programmatically gather external, unbiased data (e.g., Reddit, niche forums) to validate assumptions.
