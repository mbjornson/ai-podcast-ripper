# What Claude Design is actually good for (and why Figma isn’t dead, yet) — How I AI

## Summary
This episode of “How I AI” explores the emerging landscape of AI-powered design tools, specifically focusing on Anthropic’s Claude Design and its potential impact on established platforms like Figma. The guest, Claire Vo, a product leader and AI obsessive, provides a practical, hands-on assessment of Claude Design, arguing that it’s currently most effective as a prototyping tool, particularly for replicating existing design systems. She highlights Claude Design’s strength in handling design system imports, a common pain point for designers using traditional prototyping tools. The episode also touches on the broader trend of structured design systems being adopted by AI tools, driven by Google’s design.md standard. The host emphasizes the importance of enterprise-grade security and access controls for AI tools, advocating for solutions like Work OS to bridge the gap between AI capabilities and business needs.

## Key Points
- **Claude Design’s Niche:** Claude Design excels at prototyping and replicating existing design systems, particularly for marketing landing pages and websites. It’s not positioned to replace Figma for full design workflows.
- **Design System Import Advantage:** The core strength of Claude Design lies in its ability to seamlessly import and utilize design systems, addressing a frequent complaint about existing prototyping tools. It’s able to extract design system components and documentation effectively.
- **Structured Design Systems are Key:** The rise of standards like Google’s design.md is crucial for enabling AI tools to understand and utilize design systems consistently, leading to more predictable and high-quality UI generation.
- **Figma’s Continued Relevance:** Despite Claude Design’s capabilities, Figma remains a strong contender due to its speed of iteration and immediate responsiveness – features lacking in Claude Design’s current iteration process.
- **Enterprise Readiness is Critical:** AI design tools require robust security features (authentication, access controls, audit logs) to gain traction in enterprise environments, necessitating solutions like Work OS.
- **Iterative Feedback Loops:** The episode highlights the importance of quick feedback loops in design, particularly when using AI tools, contrasting Claude Design’s slower iteration cycles with the responsiveness of traditional design tools.

## Tools & Resources
- **Claude Design:** Anthropic’s web-based design tool focused on prototyping and design system replication.
- **Figma:** The established design and prototyping platform.
- **Work OS:** A platform providing drop-in APIs for enterprise-ready AI applications.
- **Google design.md standard:** Google’s initiative to standardize design system descriptions for AI tools.
