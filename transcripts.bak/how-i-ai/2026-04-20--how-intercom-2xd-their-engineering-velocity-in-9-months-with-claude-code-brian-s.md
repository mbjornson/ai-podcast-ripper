# How Intercom 2x’d their engineering velocity in 9 months with Claude Code | Brian Scanlan — How I AI

## Summary
This episode features Brian Scanlan, co-founder and CTO of Intercom, discussing how the company doubled their engineering velocity in nine months using Claude Code. Scanlan emphasizes a shift in mindset – moving beyond simply automating tasks to leveraging AI for genuine creative problem-solving and accelerating development workflows. The core strategy involves a “go all in” approach, preparing the team and codebase, and fostering a high-trust environment where engineers are empowered to experiment with AI tools. Intercom’s success highlights the potential for AI to fundamentally transform engineering organizations, moving beyond incremental improvements to significant velocity gains. The episode also subtly promotes Silligo’s AI operationalization platform, showcasing its capabilities in automating business processes and driving measurable results.

## Key Points
- **2x Velocity Increase:** Intercom achieved a 2x increase in engineering throughput within nine months by fully embracing Claude Code. This demonstrates the potential for AI to dramatically accelerate development cycles.
- **Mindset Shift:** The key wasn't just using AI for automation, but for unlocking creative problem-solving. Scanlan argues that imagination was previously the bottleneck, and AI now removes that constraint.
- **“Go All In” Strategy:** Intercom’s success hinges on a comprehensive approach: preparing the team, preparing the codebase, and fostering a high-trust culture.
- **Backlog Zero:** The ability to tackle previously unachievable tasks is now a realistic goal, driven by AI’s capabilities.
- **Measuring PRs:** Intercom uses pull requests as a key metric to track adoption and velocity, highlighting the importance of defining and tracking meaningful outcomes.
- **Time Off for AI Exploration:** Scanlan suggests giving employees time off to experiment with AI tools like cloud code, arguing that this can lead to unexpected breakthroughs and increased skill levels.
- **Product-Oriented Organization:** Scanlan advocates for treating the engineering organization like a product, focused on outputting innovation on demand.
- **Data-Driven Approach:** Intercom meticulously tracks telemetry data (Honeycomb, Snowflake, S3) to understand how AI is being used and identify areas for improvement.

## Tools & Resources
- **Claude Code:** The core AI tool used by Intercom for code generation and assistance.
- **Cloud Code:** Google’s cloud-based IDE for developing and deploying applications.
- **Honeycomb:** A product analytics platform used for tracking and analyzing Intercom’s data.
- **Snowflake:** A cloud data warehouse used for storing and analyzing Intercom’s data.
- **S3:** Amazon’s Simple Storage Service, used for storing session data.
- **Silligo:** An AI operationalization platform that automates business processes.
- **Silligo Aura:** Natural language interface for Silligo, simplifying AI integration.
