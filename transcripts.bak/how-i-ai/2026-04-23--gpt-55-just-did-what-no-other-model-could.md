# GPT 5.5 just did what no other model could — How I AI

## Summary
This episode centers around Clara Vo’s experience testing GPT 5.5 and GPT 5.5 Pro, highlighting its significant advancements over previous models, particularly in coding and problem-solving. Vo, a product leader and AI obsessive, emphasizes that GPT 5.5’s increased capacity, efficiency, and autonomy allow it to tackle complex tasks previously beyond the reach of other models. The core argument is that GPT 5.5 represents an “intelligence tax” – the cost of accessing this enhanced capability, justified by the speed and quality of results. The episode explores the high cost of the models ($5M/million input tokens and $30/million output tokens for 5.5 Pro), and the need for developers and software engineers to leverage this intelligence. A key takeaway is the model's tendency to “think” extensively, requiring significant processing time, and the potential challenge of finding practical applications for this intelligence outside of highly technical domains.

## Key Points
- **Significant Performance Increase:** GPT 5.5 demonstrates a substantial leap in capabilities compared to previous models, particularly in complex coding tasks and problem-solving.
- **Increased Efficiency & Token Efficiency:** The model is more efficient in terms of token usage, delivering more output with fewer inputs, a crucial factor given the high cost of usage.
- **Higher Intelligence & Autonomy:** GPT 5.5 exhibits a higher level of intelligence, capable of independently solving problems and managing its own thought processes, reducing the need for constant human oversight.
- **ROI Focus:** Vo advocates for evaluating AI tools based on their return on investment (ROI), specifically focusing on speed and efficiency gains.
- **"Intelligence Overhang":** The episode introduces the concept of “intelligence overhang,” referring to the significant processing time GPT 5.5 requires to solve problems, suggesting a potential mismatch between the model’s capabilities and the needs of certain users.
- **Use Cases:** The episode demonstrates practical applications in:
    - Teaching a second grader subtraction concepts via a custom app.
    - Architecturally reviewing and remediating technical debt in a code base.
    - Validating data migration solutions for complex, unstructured data.
- **Cost Considerations:** The high cost of GPT 5.5 Pro ($5M/million input tokens and $30/million output tokens) is a significant barrier to entry, particularly for smaller projects or individual users.

## Tools & Resources
