# How Stripe built “minions”—AI coding agents that ship 1,300 PRs weekly from Slack reactions | Steve Kaliski (Stripe engineer) — How I AI

## Summary
This episode features Steve Kaliski, a Stripe engineer, discussing Stripe’s “minions”—AI coding agents—and their impact on development workflows. The core idea is to leverage AI to automate repetitive tasks and accelerate development cycles by creating isolated environments and prompting agents to resolve issues based on Slack reactions or other triggers. Kaliski emphasizes the importance of developer experience (DX) and how investing in it directly benefits AI agents, creating a virtuous loop. The episode highlights Stripe’s approach to reducing friction in the development process, particularly in large organizations where traditional workflows can be cumbersome. The guest’s credibility stems from his direct involvement in building and deploying these agents at Stripe, a company known for its engineering-focused culture and innovative tooling.

## Key Points
- **Minion Concept:** Stripe is using AI agents (“minions”) to automate development tasks triggered by Slack reactions or other inputs. These agents operate within isolated development environments seeded with context.
- **Reduced Friction:** The primary goal is to eliminate friction in the development process, a common issue in large organizations where coordination and communication overhead can slow down progress.
- **Parallel Execution:** Minions can run in parallel, making isolated changes simultaneously, significantly increasing development velocity.
- **Developer Experience (DX) is Crucial:** Kaliski argues that investing in DX for human engineers directly benefits AI agents, as agents rely on well-documented and easily accessible tools and information. A good DX setup dramatically increases the agent’s success rate.
- **Activation Energy Reduction:** The agents lower the “activation energy” required to start work, making it easier for developers to initiate tasks and maintain momentum.
- **Goose Harness:** Stripe utilizes “Goose,” an open-source agent harness, to manage and execute the minions.
- **Sophisticated System Prompts:** Kaliski notes the importance of well-crafted system prompts for the agents, emphasizing the need for clear instructions and constraints.
- **1,300 PRs Weekly:** Stripe is currently landing approximately 1,300 PRs per week with no human assistance beyond review, showcasing the scale of automation.
