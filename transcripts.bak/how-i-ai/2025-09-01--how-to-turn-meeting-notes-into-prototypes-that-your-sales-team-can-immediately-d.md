# How to turn meeting notes into prototypes that your sales team can immediately demo to customers | Anjan Panneer Selvam (Acolyte Health) — How I AI

## Summary
The episode details a revolutionary workflow for product development, demonstrating how to transform unstructured meeting conversations and raw notes into functional prototypes in minutes or days—a massive acceleration from previous timelines of months. The core discussion centers on using AI as an intermediary layer that normalizes human "product sense," translating vague ideas (like "we need a journey builder") into precise, structured prompts suitable for immediate prototyping.

The guest, Anjan Panneer Selvam, CPTO at Acolyte Health, highlights his experience in B2B enterprise applications and notes that AI is fundamentally changing the product leadership role. He argues that the traditional separation between Product Management (PM) and Engineering is blurring, leading to a unified "CPTO" function. This shift requires leaders to be adept at rapid idea capture and articulation rather than relying on complex documentation processes.

A major theme is the move toward frictionless information capture. The discussion emphasizes using wearable AI recording devices (like the Limitless Pendant) that allow for continuous, non-intrusive transcription during natural conversations, ensuring no valuable insight is lost to friction or memory failure.

## Key Points
- **The Product Development Timeline Collapse:** Historically, building a complex feature could take months of documentation and design permutations. With this AI workflow, the cycle time has been drastically reduced to minutes (for prompt generation) to days (for initial production/prototype).
- **AI as the "Product Sense Normalizer":** The most valuable skill for PMs is not writing perfect PRDs, but *articulating* the idea clearly in natural language. AI's role is to take this varied human input and structure it into a standardized, actionable prompt that development tools can understand (e.g., turning a "brain dump" into a structured canvas builder requirement).
- **The Value of Wearable Hardware:** Traditional meeting recording relies on desktop devices or phones, which are often intrusive. The use of wearable devices like the Limitless Pendant allows for seamless, non-intrusive transcription during natural conversation flow, making real-time idea capture possible in any setting.
- **The Rise of the CPTO Role:** AI is facilitating a convergence of roles (CTO/CPO). This unified leadership position requires individuals to manage both product vision and technical feasibility simultaneously, eliminating "false conflicts" between departments by keeping all stakeholders aligned on a single source of truth derived from meeting transcripts.

## Tools & Resources
- **Limitless Pendant:** A wearable AI recording device that provides high-quality, non-intrusive transcription directly from conversations, making it ideal for capturing spontaneous ideas in meetings.
- **ChatGPT/GPT-4:** Used as the central processing engine to take raw, unstructured meeting transcripts and generate structured prompts (V0 or "lovable" prompts) ready for prototyping tools.
- **LovableRV0:** A tool mentioned that takes the AI-generated prompt and directly builds a functional prototype, bypassing manual design/documentation steps.
- **Notion AI:** Recommended for its utility in capturing meeting notes, summarizing action items, conducting research mode searches, and drafting documents across various team workflows (especially stand-ups and customer interviews).

## Action Items
- [ ] **Skill Development:** Practice rapid, unstructured "brain dumping" of ideas. Focus on articulating the *desired outcome* and *user flow* using natural language, rather than attempting to write a formal PRD first.
- [ ] **Tool Implementation (Productivity):** Integrate AI meeting note tools (like Notion AI) into your daily workflow immediately for stand-ups, 1:1s, and customer interviews to ensure immediate action item extraction and summary generation.
- [ ] **Career Mindset Shift:** Adopt a "CPTO" mindset—viewing yourself as the single point of ownership responsible for aligning product vision with technical feasibility across all stakeholders.
- [ ] **Experimentation:** Test using AI (ChatGPT) by pasting in raw, unstructured meeting transcripts and prompting it to generate a structured feature requirement or user journey map prompt.
