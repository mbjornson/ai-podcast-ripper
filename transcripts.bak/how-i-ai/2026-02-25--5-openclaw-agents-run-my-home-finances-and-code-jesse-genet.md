# 5 OpenClaw agents run my home, finances, and code | Jesse Genet — How I AI

## Summary
This episode features Jesse Genet, a homeschooling mom who has leveraged Open Claw, an AI agent, to streamline her home, finances, and coding workflows. Genet’s credibility stems from her practical application of Open Claw within a complex, real-world environment – managing a busy household, homeschooling four children, and pursuing personal projects. The core discussion revolves around layering Open Claw onto existing tools like Obsidian, a second-brain note-taking app, to automate tasks and unlock new possibilities. Genet highlights the frustration of manually logging homeschool lessons and the potential of AI to analyze and synthesize information from various sources, ultimately informing curriculum development and personalized learning experiences. The episode emphasizes a shift from traditional, structured learning to a more adaptable, AI-assisted approach.

## Key Points
- **Layering AI on Existing Tools:** The key insight is not about building a completely new system but about strategically layering AI agents like Open Claw onto existing tools like Obsidian to augment human capabilities. Genet’s journey started with Obsidian, a popular second-brain note-taking app, recognizing its potential for organization but acknowledging the time constraints of a busy parent.
- **Automation of Repetitive Tasks:** Open Claw automates tasks like logging homeschool lessons, extracting key concepts from books, and generating curriculum plans, freeing up time for more strategic thinking and engagement with her children.
- **Photo-Based Workflow:** Genet utilizes a photo-based workflow, taking pictures of books, lessons, and materials, and then using Open Claw to analyze and extract information from these images. This is a surprisingly effective way to integrate visual learning into the AI process.
- **Niche Use Cases & Personalization:** Genet’s homeschooling approach is highly personalized and focused on niche areas, such as phonics instruction, demonstrating the potential of AI to cater to individual learning needs and specific challenges.
- **Data Synthesis & Curriculum Planning:** Open Claw isn't just about logging; it's about synthesizing data from multiple sources to inform curriculum planning, suggesting activities and adapting to individual student progress.
- **Obsidian as a Foundation:** Obsidian is presented as a crucial foundation for this workflow, acting as a central repository for all learning materials and allowing Open Claw to access and process information effectively.
