# Shopping with Claude: How to find quality brands, automate returns, and buy things that last 100 years | Nicole Ruiz — How I AI

## Summary
The episode addresses the profound mental overhead associated with modern online life, particularly the administrative burden of purchasing goods—from navigating returns and finding quality materials to sifting through paid ads and knockoff brands. Nicole Ruiz, a busy parent focused on sustainable consumption, details how she is using AI (specifically Claude) to build a structured system for high-quality shopping that emphasizes longevity and ethical sourcing. Her goal is not just to buy things, but to ensure they "last for a hundred years," moving beyond the transient nature of fast fashion and mass-market goods.

Nicole’s method involves creating an "anti-to-do list" within an AI project. This system consolidates trusted vendors and establishes rigorous purchasing criteria that filter out low-quality direct-to-consumer (D2C) brands and random third-party sellers. The process is designed to automate the complex vetting required for durable, natural, or locally sourced items, effectively turning a chaotic decision-making process into a repeatable, structured query.

The core takeaway is that AI can be used not just for information retrieval, but for *process orchestration*—taking an invisible checklist of quality checks (e.g., return policy, material composition, brand history) and making it visible, systematic, and actionable every time a purchase decision needs to be made.

## Key Points
- **The "Anti-To-Do List" Concept:** The most valuable insight is treating complex life processes (like shopping or household maintenance) as systems that need documentation. By formalizing the invisible checklist of quality checks—natural fiber, local sourcing, return policy, durability—the mental load associated with these tasks is significantly reduced and made repeatable.
- **Systemic Vetting Over Transactional Buying:** The focus shifts from simply finding a product to vetting the *brand's history*. Nicole uses Claude to surface critical data points like whether a brand was recently acquired (and if reviews have subsequently declined), providing an immediate warning signal that transcends standard review scores.
- **Mandatory Data Points for AI Output:** To ensure quality, the user prompts the AI to structure results with specific, non-negotiable fields: Product Name, Photo, Price, Materials (must be upfront), Care/Maintenance Notes, and a brief note on the brand's trustworthy history. This prevents superficial browsing based only on aesthetics or low price.
- **The Longevity Standard:** The goal is to buy items that are built to last decades, requiring vendors who stand behind their product for multiple generations, rather than supporting trendy D2C brands that underinvest in quality due to advertising costs.

## Tools & Resources
- **Claude (Anthropic):** Used as the primary AI agent to consolidate vendor lists and execute complex, multi-criteria purchasing queries within a dedicated "Project" environment, maintaining memory and context separate from general use.
- **Apple Notes:** Used as the initial repository for consolidating trusted vendors and establishing the foundational list of high-quality brands before feeding them into the AI system.
