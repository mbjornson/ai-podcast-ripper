# The internal AI tool that’s transforming how Stripe designs products | Owen Williams — How I AI

## Summary
This episode features Owen Williams, a design manager at Stripe, discussing an internal prototyping tool called “protodash” that’s transforming how Stripe designs its products. The core problem he addresses is the difficulty of rapidly prototyping complex data dashboards in tools like Figma, often hampered by the need for manual data setup and state management. Protodash aims to solve this by providing a code-based environment that mimics a real dashboard experience, allowing designers and PMs to quickly iterate and validate ideas. A key insight is the shift in roles within Stripe – PMs are increasingly involved in design prototyping, driven by the accessibility of this tool. The episode highlights a pragmatic approach to problem-solving, leveraging Owen’s engineering background to lower the technical barrier to entry for designers.

## Key Points
- **The Prototyping Pain:** Designers at Stripe struggled to efficiently prototype data dashboards in Figma due to the need for manual data setup, complex state management, and the lack of a realistic environment.  This resulted in "uncanny valley" prototypes that felt jarring and didn’t accurately represent the final product.
- **PMs in the Design Loop:** A surprising trend is the increased involvement of PMs in design prototyping, facilitated by the accessibility of protodash.
- **Lowering the Technical Barrier:** Owen’s engineering background led him to build protodash with the goal of simplifying the prototyping process for designers, focusing on tools like NPM run dev and minimizing the need for deep coding knowledge.
- **Code-Based Prototyping:** Protodash is built using React and provides a realistic dashboard environment, allowing designers to quickly iterate and validate ideas without the friction of traditional design tools.
- **Dev Box Infrastructure:** Stripe utilizes Dev Box infrastructure, allowing designers to instantly access pre-configured servers for prototyping, further streamlining the process.
- **Shift from Demos to Interactive Prototypes:** The tool enables a shift from presenting static mockups (demos) to interactive prototypes, facilitating more effective design reviews.
- **Data Prototyping Challenge:** The difficulty of prototyping data-heavy dashboards, particularly with considerations like internationalization and complex business models, was a significant pain point addressed by protodash.
