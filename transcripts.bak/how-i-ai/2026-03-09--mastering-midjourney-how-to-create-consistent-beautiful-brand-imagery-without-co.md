# Mastering Midjourney: How to create consistent, beautiful brand imagery without complex prompts | Jamey Gannon — How I AI

## Summary
This episode with Jamey Gannon of “How I AI” focuses on mastering Midjourney for consistent, brand-aligned imagery, moving beyond the chaotic, iterative prompting process. Gannon’s credibility stems from his experience as an AI creative director, having spent “10 gajillion hours” refining workflows within Midjourney and related tools. The core argument is that a structured approach – primarily utilizing mood boards and style references – is crucial for predictable and high-quality brand assets, particularly for businesses seeking to leverage AI for visual content creation. Gannon advocates for a shift in mindset, moving away from relying solely on raw AI generation and embracing a more deliberate, directed process.

## Key Points
- **Structured Workflow is Key:** The most significant takeaway is the need for a tightly controlled process, moving beyond random prompting to achieve consistent brand imagery. Gannon emphasizes this is about “managing the chaos” of AI generation.
- **Mood Boards as Visual Language:** Mood boards are presented as a critical tool, acting as a visual language to communicate the desired aesthetic to Midjourney, leveraging the LLM’s ability to interpret images (“a picture is worth a thousand words”).
- **Style References (S-Refs) Superior to Mood Boards:**  Gannon argues that using style references (S-Refs) – directly importing images as visual guides – yields significantly better results than traditional mood boards for achieving consistent brand aesthetics.
- **Intuition and Experimentation:**  Gannon’s experience (3 years with Midjourney) plays a key role, highlighting the importance of intuition and iterative adjustments within the process, particularly when tweaking S-Refs.
- **Language & Prompting Limitations:** The episode touches on the limitations of AI prompting, suggesting that a lack of designer/photographer language can hinder the process, advocating for techniques like querying ChatGPT for explanations of discrepancies.
- **Specific Go-To Prompts:** Gannon identifies specific prompts (“theory, real female model,” “cats”) that consistently produce desirable results, emphasizing the value of targeted experimentation.
- **Contrast & Color Control:** The episode highlights the importance of controlling color and contrast, noting that Midjourney’s default output can sometimes produce washed-out images, requiring adjustments through S-Refs.
