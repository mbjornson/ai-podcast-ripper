# Quests, token leaderboards, and a skills marketplace: The elite AI adoption playbook | John Kim (Sendbird) — How I AI

## Summary
This episode features John Kim, founder and CEO of SendBird, discussing the company’s ambitious approach to AI adoption, centered around a “quest” system and a token leaderboard. The core idea is to empower employees, particularly marketers and sales teams, to become builders and proactively create AI-powered solutions within the company, rather than simply receiving AI tools. Kim emphasizes a shift from a traditional, engineering-driven product development model to one where creativity and immediate needs drive innovation. The conversation highlights the importance of fostering a culture of experimentation, rapid prototyping, and “fail fast” learning, fueled by readily available AI assistance. SendBird has developed an internal platform, the “Automators” platform, to facilitate this process, allowing employees to submit quests for AI-powered automation and leveraging AI itself to accelerate development.

## Key Points
- **Quest-Based AI Adoption:** SendBird’s strategy revolves around a “quest” system where employees can propose AI-driven solutions to specific problems, essentially turning the company into a “delight ass shop” – a place where fun and creative projects are prioritized.
- **Token Leaderboard & AI Gauges:** The company tracks AI adoption through a token consumption leaderboard, measuring “AI gauze” based on daily token usage (over 100 million tokens/day). This creates a competitive element and encourages experimentation.
- **Empowering Marketers & Sales:** A key focus is shifting the role of marketers and salespeople from passive recipients of technology to active builders, leveraging their domain expertise to create impactful solutions.
- **Internal Automation Platform (“Automators”):** SendBird has built an internal platform, the Automators, to manage quests, facilitate collaboration between employees, and provide a framework for AI-assisted development.
- **AI-Powered Quest Creation:** The platform allows AI to assist in the creation of PRDs and even coding for quests, significantly accelerating the development process.
- **Low-Risk Experimentation:** The emphasis is on “fail fast” learning, with the understanding that not all quests will succeed, but the potential for valuable insights and innovation outweighs the risk.
- **Context Window & Creative Energy:** The use of AI, particularly with tools like OpenAI, Perplexity, and Cursor, is intended to unlock “creative energy” and enable rapid prototyping of marketing materials and other creative assets.

## Tools & Resources
- **SendBird’s “Automators” Platform:** An internal platform for managing quests and facilitating AI-assisted development.
- **OpenAI (GPT models):** Used for PRD creation and code generation within the quest system.
- **Perplexity AI:** Used for research and information gathering.
- **Cursor:** Another AI tool potentially utilized within the workflow.
- **Stripe:** Integrated for payment processing (e.g., for the swag store).
- **Git Hub:** Used for code repository management.
