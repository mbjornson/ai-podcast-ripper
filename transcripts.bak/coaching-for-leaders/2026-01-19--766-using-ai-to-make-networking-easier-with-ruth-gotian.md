# 766: Using AI to Make Networking Easier, with Ruth Gotian — Coaching for Leaders

## Summary
The episode discusses the challenge of professional networking in the modern, virtual landscape, arguing that while AI can handle the "legwork," genuine connection requires intentional, consistent human effort. The guest, Ruth Gotian, is a highly credible expert, noted as a former Chief Learning Officer and an emerging management thinker ranked by Thinkers50. She reframes networking not as a series of transactions, but as the slow, cumulative process of building trust through small, non-demanding interactions.

Gotian emphasizes that the assumption that trust cannot be built virtually is false. Instead, she provides actionable strategies for moving beyond passive consumption (lurking) to active engagement (commenting, sharing, and curating content) to build a recognizable, authentic professional persona. The core message is that consistency and thoughtful, non-transactional "showing up" are the most powerful tools for building a valuable network.

## Key Points
- **The 99:1 Rule:** This rule governs online communities (like LinkedIn), stating that 99% of users are lurkers (they consume content but do not engage), 9% are occasional interactors, and less than 1% are content creators. The goal is to move from lurker to thoughtful engager.
- **Trust is Built, Not Given:** Trust is not formed in one conversation; it is built over time through small, consistent actions and interactions.
- **Intentionality Over Spontaneity:** Since the pandemic, networking has become less spontaneous. To compensate, one must be highly intentional about making connections, even through simple gestures like taking a picture of a book or T-shirt and sending it to a contact.
- **The Power of Curation:** To elevate your status in a network, do not just consume content. Become a curator by summarizing valuable external content (e.g., "Listened to the Coaching for Leaders podcast with Dave Stachowiak. Here are three things I learned") and linking it back to the source. This establishes you as an information hub.
- **Authenticity Sells:** People are more attracted to your authentic struggles and relatable experiences than they are to polished, perfect wins.

## Tools & Resources
- **Platforms:** LinkedIn (Primary focus for professional engagement and implementing the 99:1 rule).
- **Content Strategy:** The podcast itself (Coaching for Leaders) is recommended as a source of content to summarize and share.

## Quotable
> "I truly believe that trust can be formed... but the challenge is that, it's formed over time. It's these little things that you do over time that help breed and infuse trust."
>

So you now, as the poster of that summary, become the curator of information, and you have just elevated yourself to a new status."

## Action Items
- [ ] **Implement the "Curator" Strategy:** Commit to summarizing a piece of valuable content (like a podcast episode or article) weekly on LinkedIn, offering 3 key takeaways and linking back to the source.
- [ ] **Practice Non-Transactional Outreach:** Instead of reaching out with an "ask," send a picture of something random (a book, a shirt, an article) that reminded you of a contact, simply stating, "Thought of you."
- [ ] **Shift Online Behavior:** When engaging on professional platforms, move beyond simple "likes." Commit to writing thoughtful comments (more than two words) or sharing content with your own reflection to move up the engagement ladder.
- [ ] **Focus on Vulnerability:** When creating content, focus on sharing struggles and relatable challenges rather than only professional wins, as this builds deeper connection.
