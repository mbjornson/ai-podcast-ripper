# 780: Moving From Self-Sabotage to Self-Mastery, with Shirzad Chamine — Coaching for Leaders

## Summary
The episode addresses the common leadership challenge of self-sabotage, proposing a shift from merely surviving to truly thriving. Shirzad Chamine, a leading expert in Positive Intelligence and author of the bestseller *Positive Intelligence*, introduces a powerful framework comparing the inner self to a "Jedi Mind Training" concept. He posits that every person contains an "Inner Jedi" (the extraordinary, potential self) and "Inner Darth Vaders" (the saboteurs).

Chamine explains that these saboteurs are not moral failings, but rather "agents of survival"—old operating systems developed in childhood to gain love, acceptance, and security. While these mechanisms were necessary for survival, they are now running the leader's mind, causing self-doubt, anxiety, and professional sabotage. The core message is that recognizing these saboteurs is the first step toward mastery, normalizing the struggle as a universal human condition, especially among high-achieving leaders.

## Key Points
- **The Saboteur Mechanism:** Self-sabotage occurs when natural, profound strengths are overused and abused. The saboteur takes a strength (e.g., attention to detail) and converts it into a weakness (e.g., over-perfectionism, wasting time on things that are "good enough").
- **The 10 Saboteur Factors:** Based on factor analysis research involving over a million people, Chamine identified 10 common saboteur profiles (e.g., The Judge, The Controller, The Stickler, The Avoider, The Pleaser, Hyperachiever, Restless Victim).
- **Leadership Profile:** For senior-level leaders, the most common saboteurs are typically the **Controller**, the **Stickler** (over-perfectionism), the **Hyperachiever**, and the **Hyperrational**.
- **The Survival vs. Thriving Distinction:** Saboteurs are designed to help you *survive* childhood trauma or insecurity. The goal of self-mastery is to move beyond mere survival and achieve *thriving* by strengthening the "Sage" (Inner Jedi) voice.
- **Normalization is Key:** The most profound relief for high-achievers is realizing that self-doubt and saboteurs are not personal flaws, but a common, universal human condition.

- [ ] **Redefine "Good Enough":** Practice intentionally accepting "good enough" in low-stakes situations to counteract the tendency toward paralyzing over-perfectionism.
