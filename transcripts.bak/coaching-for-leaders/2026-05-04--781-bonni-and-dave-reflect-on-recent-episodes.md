# 781: Bonni and Dave Reflect on Recent Episodes — Coaching for Leaders

## Summary
This episode of “Coaching for Leaders” is a reflective discussion between Dave Stachowiak and his wife, Bonni Stachowiak, focusing on recent podcast episodes and listener feedback. The core of the conversation centers around episode 750, featuring Margaret Andrews, where she posed six questions for leaders to explore their self-understanding. Dave and Bonni selected two of these questions to delve into, aiming to provide actionable insights for listeners. The episode highlights the importance of self-awareness, understanding the impact of one’s actions on others, and the value of feedback in leadership development. Bonni’s background as a leadership consultant and her extensive experience in team dynamics lend credibility to her perspectives. The episode leans heavily on anecdotal evidence and personal reflections, offering a conversational and relatable approach to leadership principles.

## Key Points
- **Listener Engagement & Relevance:** The episode acknowledges a significant response to episode 750, suggesting that the questions resonated deeply with listeners, likely due to their broad applicability. This highlights the value of addressing common leadership challenges.
- **Self-Understanding as a Foundation:** The core of the discussion revolves around Margaret Andrews’ assertion that understanding your differences is key to effective leadership. Knowing your strengths and weaknesses allows for better self-leadership and, consequently, better leadership of others.
- **Success Definition - Zig Ziglar’s Perspective:** Dave shares his interpretation of Zig Ziglar’s quote – “You can have everything in life you want if you’ll just help enough other people get what they want” – as a guiding principle for professional success, emphasizing a focus on helping others achieve their goals.
- **Feedback Loops & Retrospectives:** Bonni stresses the importance of establishing clear metrics for success and conducting regular retrospectives to assess progress and gather feedback. This highlights the need for continuous improvement and adaptation.
- **Team Dynamics & Shared Vision:** Bonni’s personal experience in team settings, particularly her emphasis on the importance of a shared team vision and a supportive team culture, underscores the critical role of relationships in leadership.
- **Vulnerability & Trust:** Bonni’s reflection on her vulnerability during a university presentation reinforces the idea that trust and openness are essential for effective feedback and collaboration.
