# 759: The Way to Build Collective Power, with Ruchika T. Malhotra — Coaching for Leaders

## Summary
The episode challenges the deeply ingrained, default assumption that professional and social success is a zero-sum competition—a "winner take all" scenario. Instead, the discussion centers on the concept of "uncompeting," which is defined as the intentional choice to reject competitive frameworks in favor of building collective power. The core argument is that by shifting focus from individual resource acquisition to mutual contribution, more value can be created for everyone involved.

The guest, Ruchika T. Malhotra, is a leading expert in global inclusion strategy, founder of Candor, and author of *Uncompete*. Her background provides a strong foundation for this discussion, as she has worked with major organizations and written on topics like inclusion and systemic change. She frames the ability to uncompete not just as a soft skill, but as a necessary structural shift required for sustainable, long-term success in both business and personal relationships.

The conversation emphasizes that leadership requires modeling this shift. Leaders must actively dismantle the scarcity mindset—the belief that resources (promotions, recognition, opportunities) are limited—and instead build systems that invest in collective talent development.

## Key Points
- **The Shift from Zero-Sum to Collective Power:** The default setting of modern society and business is often zero-sum thinking. The goal is to consciously move away from the mindset of "fighting for the same opportunities" toward a model where inviting others to the table creates more for everyone.
- **Uncompete as an Intentional Choice:** "Uncompete" is not merely a passive lack of competition; it is an active, deliberate choice to opt out of the competitive framework. This choice is necessary because the competitive mindset is often visceral and automatic.
- **Prioritizing the Long View:** Successful relationships and professional collaborations (like the example of the guest and her friend, Iko Bethea) are built on a long-term view, where mutual support and shared success are valued over securing an immediate, short-term win.
- **Leadership by Example (Modeling Anti-Scarcity):** Leaders have a unique opportunity to model uncompeting behavior. Instead of pitting employees against each other for a single promotion, leaders should design systems that promote the most qualified person while simultaneously investing in the development of all other talent.
- **The Power of Genuine Celebration:** True leadership involves being genuinely excited for the wins of peers, rather than reacting with jealousy or scarcity. This modeling behavior is crucial for shifting organizational culture.

## Action Items
- [ ] **Mindset Shift:** When faced with a professional challenge or opportunity, consciously reframe the question from "What do I need to win?" to "How can we structure this to create mutual success?"
- [ ] **Relationship Audit:** Identify 2-3 professional peers in your industry and initiate a conversation focused purely on mutual support and long-term collaboration, explicitly setting aside the expectation of immediate rivalry.
- [ ] **Leadership Practice:** Practice genuinely celebrating the successes of peers, especially in public forums (meetings, social media), to model an anti-scarcity mindset.
- [ ] **Reading:** Check out *Uncompete* by Ruchika T. Malhotra to deepen understanding of the framework.
- [ ] **Skill Development:** Focus on developing "long-term view thinking" in career planning, prioritizing sustained relationships and collective growth over quick, individual wins.
