# David Rutherford Show: The Insurgency Inside the Republican Party | Eric Schwalm — The David Rutherford Show

## Summary
Eric Schwalm, a Chief One Officer with three decades of experience in Special Forces and intelligence, argues that the political situation within the Republican Party is not a simple "war," but a deep-seated "insurgency." His analysis focuses on the strategic failures and inertia of the "old school Republicans" (Rhinos), who are allegedly engaging in stall tactics rather than actively fighting for their political goals. Schwalm contends that this inaction—seen in legislative gridlock or election efforts—is not accidental; it is a calculated effort to undermine the populist, America First movement (MAGA).

Schwalm's core value proposition is his ability to apply advanced military operational art and intelligence analysis to civilian political groups. He teaches a structured methodology for assessing any organization, whether it's a political party, a corporation, or a rival group. This framework moves beyond surface-level conflict to identify the true center of gravity, the critical vulnerabilities, and the ultimate end state of the opposing force.

## Key Points
- **The Insurgency Thesis:** The current political struggle is characterized by stall tactics from the established Republican guard. Their goal is to show that the populist movement is losing momentum, thereby allowing them to seize power and maintain their status quo, even if it means helping the opposition.
- **The SAMSS Analytical Framework:** Schwalm employs a rigorous, multi-layered system derived from the School of Advanced Military Studies (SAMSS). To analyze any group, one must systematically identify:
    1.  **Center of Gravity (CoG):** The single most vital element that, if removed or denied, causes the entire structure to collapse (e.g., for ISIS, the CoG was ground control).
    2.  **Critical Capabilities/Requirements:** What the group needs to function.
    3.  **Critical Vulnerabilities:** The weaknesses that can be exploited.
    4.  **Commander's Intent/End State:** The ultimate, stated goal of the group.
- **The "Think Like Them" Principle:** Effective analysis requires adopting the perspective of the opposing force. Instead of simply reacting to what they are doing, you must understand *why* they are doing it and what they believe their ultimate goal is.
- **The Danger of Comfort:** Schwalm draws a parallel between military field-grade officers who become comfortable and stop dreaming, and political elites who become protective of their positions. This comfort leads to inaction and strategic failure.
