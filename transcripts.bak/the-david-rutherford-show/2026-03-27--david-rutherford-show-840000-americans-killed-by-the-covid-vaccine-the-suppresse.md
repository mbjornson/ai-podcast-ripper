# David Rutherford Show: 840,000 Americans Killed by the Covid Vaccine — The Suppressed Science — The David Rutherford Show

## Summary
The episode features an intense discussion with Nick Holscher, an epidemiologist from the McCullough Foundation, who argues that the COVID-19 vaccination program constituted a "catastrophic medical experiment." Holscher's central thesis is that the vaccines were profoundly harmful, leading to massive, underreported death tolls. He presents multiple estimates of excess deaths in the United States, ranging from 470,000 to 840,000, attributing these fatalities directly to the mass vaccination program rather than COVID-19 itself.

Holscher further argues that the entire program was not a public health response but a pre-planned initiative. He details a timeline suggesting that the technology (mRNA) and the pathogen were developed through U.S. research efforts, referencing institutions like the Barrack Lab and government programs like DARPA. He strongly challenges the scientific definition of a vaccine, asserting that the mRNA shots are fundamentally a gene therapy, not a traditional vaccine, and that the definition was deliberately altered to facilitate their deployment.

## Key Points
- **Massive Underreported Death Tolls:** Three independent estimates of vaccine-related deaths in the U.S. are cited:
    *   470,000 deaths (based on Pfizer data by Florida Surgeon General Joel Dappo).
    *   Up to 600,000 deaths (based on extrapolating VAERS data, multiplying the 20,000 reported deaths by a conservative factor of 30).
    *   Up to 840,000 deaths (using the CDC Wonder data, representing excess deaths since 2021).
- **Gene Therapy vs. Vaccine:** Holscher claims the mRNA shots are not vaccines but "gene therapies." They utilize a lipid nanoparticle to deliver modified messenger RNA, which provides genetic instructions to the body to produce proteins, fundamentally changing the body into a "non human protein factory."
- **Planned Pandemic Initiative:** The program is presented as having been planned years in advance, citing:
    *   DARPA's Project Barda (2012), which aimed to develop pandemic countermeasures using mRNA.
    *   Pre-pandemic simulations involving figures like Bill Gates and Johns Hopkins (2021).
    *   The documented work of U.S. researchers (e.g., the Barrack Lab) on chimeric coronaviruses and "gain of function" activities since the 1990s.
- **The Definition Shift:** The argument posits that the definition of a vaccine was intentionally changed so that these gene therapies could be marketed and accepted as traditional vaccines.
