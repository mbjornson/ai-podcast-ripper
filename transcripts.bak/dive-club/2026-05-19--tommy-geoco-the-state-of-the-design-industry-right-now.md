# Tommy Geoco - The state of the design industry right now — Dive Club

## Summary
This episode of Dive Club features Tommy Geoco, a design strategist and consultant, discussing the evolving state of the design industry, particularly the increasing influence of AI and the rise of “builders” within design teams. Geoco’s credibility stems from his extensive network of design leaders and his focus on practical, hands-on experimentation. A key takeaway is the shift towards a more system-oriented approach to design, driven by the adoption of vibe coding and the emergence of roles like brand engineers. The episode highlights a growing trend of designers building their own tools and workflows, often leveraging AI as a starting point, and emphasizes the importance of adaptability and embracing new skillsets. Geoco’s perspective is grounded in observation and data, particularly his recent prototyping survey, which reveals surprising insights about designer satisfaction and workflow adoption.

## Key Points
- **AI as Ambient Fluency:** AI is transitioning from a specific tool to a foundational element of design workflows, moving beyond highlighted language and becoming more integrated.
- **Vibe Coding & Satisfaction:** Designers actively using vibe coding (building custom tools and workflows) report higher levels of satisfaction with their work, suggesting a preference for hands-on, iterative experimentation.
- **Shift in Workflow Roles:** The majority of design workflow is now dominated by design engineers, lead principles, and managers, with junior and mid-level designers often sidelined. This suggests a potential bottleneck in knowledge sharing and team inclusivity.
- **Social Media as Discovery Engine:** Social media (specifically platforms like Instagram) is now the primary way designers discover new trends and experiment with approaches, replacing traditional, more structured feedback loops.
- **Rise of “Brand Engineers”:** Companies are increasingly hiring individuals with the skills to build and maintain design systems and brand guidelines, reflecting a move towards industrializing design processes.
- **Builders are Emerging:** There’s a growing trend of designers becoming “builders,” creating custom tools and systems to streamline their workflows, often leveraging AI for initial exploration.
- **The AI/Human Spectrum:** Design is moving along a spectrum from purely probabilistic (AI-driven exploration) to deterministic (manual, critical thinking) design, requiring designers to adapt their skillsets.
- **No-AI Days for Critical Thinking:** Geoco incorporates dedicated "no-AI" days to force a return to fundamental design thinking and to build richer context for AI prompts.
