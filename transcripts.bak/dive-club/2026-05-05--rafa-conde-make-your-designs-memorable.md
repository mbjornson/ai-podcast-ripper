# Rafa Conde - Make your designs memorable — Dive Club

## Summary
This episode of Dive Club features Rafael Conde, a design engineer at Retro, discussing the importance of creating software that evokes emotion and memorable experiences. Conde’s background as a former layout podcast contributor and his early fascination with games like Draw Something and Metal Gear Solid shaped his design philosophy. He emphasizes the value of injecting personality and “humanity” into products, drawing inspiration from experiences that create surprise, frustration, and ultimately, a strong connection with the user. The conversation highlights the shift in design thinking towards more engaging and interactive experiences, moving beyond purely functional interfaces. Conde advocates for prototyping with tools like Papers.ai and Descent to rapidly explore design concepts and create moments of delight.

## Key Points
- **The Power of Emotional Connection:** Conde argues that software should aim to elicit emotional responses from users, drawing a parallel to the impact of games like Metal Gear Solid, which deliberately broke the fourth wall and created moments of frustration and triumph.
- **Origin Stories Matter:** The episode begins with Conde’s personal story of how Draw Something sparked his passion for design, emphasizing the importance of understanding the “why” behind a designer’s work.
- **Surprise and Delight as a Core Principle:** Conde advocates for intentionally creating “surprise and delight” moments in design, mirroring the unexpected elements in games like Metal Gear Solid. He suggests that frustrating users intentionally can be a catalyst for creativity.
- **Rapid Prototyping is Crucial:** Conde recommends using tools like Papers.ai and Descent to rapidly prototype design concepts, emphasizing the importance of exploring multiple variations quickly.
- **AI-Powered Exploration:** Conde highlights the potential of AI tools like Papers.ai to generate diverse design explorations, accelerating the prototyping process and uncovering unexpected solutions.
- **Beyond Traditional UI:** Conde challenges the conventional approach to UI design, advocating for experiences that feel more like games or interactive stories, prioritizing emotional engagement over purely functional interfaces.
