# Andy Zhang - From early Figma to design engineer at Windsurf — Dive Club

## Summary
The episode chronicles Andy Zhang's career trajectory, detailing his pivotal experience at Figma and his current role as a design engineering lead at Windsurf. Andy recounts how he transitioned from studying pure software engineering to focusing on design because he wanted to be closer to the user experience—a shift that defined his professional path. His time at Figma is highlighted not just for its prestige, but for the *mindset* it instilled: one where curiosity drives learning and mentorship encourages deep dives into complex topics across disciplines (e.g., designers discussing compilers).

Andy emphasizes that true growth comes from an environment that allows employees to pursue their intellectual curiosity rather than simply completing assigned tasks. He describes his role as a hybrid generalist, blending the meticulous detail of design with the technical rigor of engineering. The core takeaway is that mastering complex concepts requires not just knowledge, but the ability to unpack them into simple, understandable components—a skill demonstrated by senior engineers and designers alike.

## Key Points
- **The Power of Curiosity over Assignment:** Andy's most formative experience was realizing he had the "choice of pursuing [his] curiosity," rather than simply being given work. This suggests that job environments that foster intellectual autonomy are key to professional development, regardless of title.
- **Depth in Detail is a Feature, Not a Bug:** The story of debating whether to use "delete" or "remove" for a file recovery mechanism illustrates the norm at early Figma: an extreme commitment to detail, where even copy and physical analogies required hours of deep consideration. This level of rigor should be applied to every product element.
- **The Value of Hybrid Generalism:** His career path—from SWE to design/UX to design engineering lead—demonstrates that modern tech roles benefit from being "hybrid generalists." The ability to speak the language of both code (CRDTs, compilers) and user empathy is highly valuable.
- **Learning Through Open Dialogue:** Complex concepts (like how multiplayer works or CRDTs) were taught through informal, open conversations at lunch tables, not formal training sessions. This highlights that institutional knowledge transfer often happens in casual, interdisciplinary settings.

## Tools & Resources
- **Raycast:** A universal portal/launcher for AI and workflow streamlining on the computer (mentioned as a quick keyboard shortcut tool).
- **Genway AI:** An AI tool designed to simulate user research by running interviews with global participants based on defined Ideal Customer Profiles (ICPs), allowing rapid idea validation.

- [ ] **Career Move:** Seek out roles or projects that explicitly grant autonomy and allow you to pursue self-identified areas of curiosity, rather than just fulfilling assigned tickets.
