# Gabe Valdivia - How to Thrive as an Independent Designer — Dive Club

## Summary
The episode features Gabe Valdivia, an independent designer who specializes in building his practice around the *craft* of design itself, rather than merely using it as a "stepping stone" to a final product or company artifact. His background involves transitioning from traditional in-house roles (like at Facebook) to independent consulting, allowing him to work with early-stage and later-stage startups across diverse verticals (healthcare, insurance, consumer/B2B).

The discussion centers on the evolution of his freelance model over two years. Year one was focused on sheer volume—taking on as much work as possible to prove viability and satisfy creative hunger. Year two has seen him refine a sophisticated, scalable system: moving beyond direct client engagement (Option 1) to deploying "designers in residence" within companies (Option 2). In this model, he acts as a design manager and coach, allowing junior designers to interact directly with clients under his mentorship, effectively scaling his impact while maintaining quality control.

A significant portion of the conversation addresses the shift in defining professional success. Valdivia notes that initially, success was purely tied to financial metrics (MRR/income), leading to an "infinite race" and constant anxiety. He has since consciously shifted focus toward non-monetary incentives, such as mentoring others, creating opportunities for peers who are laid off, and increasing selectivity in client work to ensure alignment with his personal mission and professional pride.

## Key Points
- **The Value of Process Over Product:** Frame your expertise around the *practice* or *craft* of design itself, not just the final deliverable. This elevates you from a service provider (who makes things) to a strategic partner (who improves processes).
- **Scaling Through Apprenticeship/Residency:** To scale beyond direct billable hours, implement a "Designer in Residence" model. You act as the manager and coach, deploying junior talent who interact with clients directly. This allows you to multiply your impact while maintaining quality through mentorship.
- **The Danger of Financial Metrics:** Do not define professional success solely by income or MRR. Relying on money creates an unsustainable "infinite race" mentality. Recognize this metric as a trap that replaces one form of anxiety (insecurity) with another (the need to earn more).
- **Redefining Success Incentives:** Replace financial metrics with qualitative, self-defined incentives:
    *   **Mentorship/Opportunity Creation:** Tracking how many peers you help bridge employment gaps or find permanent homes.
    *   **Mission Alignment:** Being highly selective about clients and projects where you can genuinely align the work's mission with your personal values and professional pride.
    *   **Power/Impact:** Focusing on enabling founders who are positioned to be powerful in the world (a high-level, impact metric).
