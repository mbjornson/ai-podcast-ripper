# Carl Rivera - Shopify’s big bet on design and craft as the differentiator — Dive Club

## Summary
Carl Rivera, Chief Design Officer at Shopify, discusses how design must evolve from being a mere feature of an e-commerce platform into its primary strategic differentiator in the age of AI. He frames this as a massive opportunity: reimagining 10% of U.S. commerce (Shopify's market share) from first principles. Rivera argues that while Shopify is incredibly functional, it lacks a memorable, design-led experience—a gap he aims to fill by leading the company’s vision toward what the "perfect e-commerce platform" looks like when built today.

The core of his strategy involves treating design not just as an aesthetic layer, but as a powerful tool for organizational alignment and future imagining. He emphasizes that instead of simply adding AI features on top of existing infrastructure (a layered implementation), Shopify must build genuinely *native* AI experiences from the ground up. To support this ambitious vision, he is restructuring the organization to be flatter, more fluid, and highly centralized, moving away from traditional, calcified team silos.

To execute this new model, Shopify acquired Molly Studio, a strategic design agency. Rivera uses them as a working example of how high-caliber, independent craftspeople (ICs) can assemble into temporary teams to own entire user journeys across multiple surfaces, rather than being confined to one product area. This move is designed to elevate the company's overall "talent density" and ensure that design leads the conversation, not just participates in it.

## Key Points
- **Design as the Core Differentiator:** Shopify’s initial success (since 2006) was driven by its ease of use and superior design. Rivera notes that while the platform is functional, the experience itself isn't what users remember for its design—this must change to maintain a competitive edge.
- **The Native AI Imperative:** The future requires moving beyond "smacking AI on top" of existing capabilities. Design must guide thinking toward truly *native* AI experiences, where interfaces are conceived from scratch knowing that advanced generative capabilities exist from day one.
- **Design as an Organizational Tool:** Rivera positions design not as a deliverable, but as a strategic mechanism ("a tool and a strategy") to drive high-fidelity conversations across the company. By creating a visual "North Star" experience, it allows diverse stakeholders to debate *what* the future means, rather than getting stuck in endless documents or briefs.
- **The Fluid Org Model:** To support massive vision work, he is dismantling traditional, siloed team structures. The ideal structure is flat and fluid, allowing area leaders to assemble highly talented Individual Contributors (ICs) into temporary teams that own entire user journeys end-to-end, rather than just single product surfaces.
- **Generative Spaghetti on the Wall:** The approach must be non-precious and iterative. Instead of spending months in a closed "chamber" to produce one perfect answer, the process needs to be highly generative—a rapid, messy exploration that embraces ambiguity and constant iteration.

## Tools & Resources
- **Granola:** An AI tool mentioned for its ability to take notes during calls and meetings, making it useful for capturing context and ideas in real-time (Recommended by the podcast host).
- **Lovable:** A platform highlighted as a preferred choice for AI prototyping because of its integrated voice mode functionality, allowing users to build ideas simply by having a conversation.

## Action Items
- [ ] **Adopt Design Thinking as an Organizational Strategy:** When facing complex business problems, don't start with KPIs or documents. Instead, use a visual "North Star" prototype (a clickable mock-up) to force high-fidelity conversations and align diverse stakeholders on the *experience* first.
- [ ] **Shift AI Implementation Mindset:** Stop thinking about how existing tools can be augmented by AI. Start asking: "What does this interface look like if it was built from ground zero, knowing that generative AI capabilities exist?" (Focus on native integration).
- [ ] **Practice Fluid Teaming:** If leading a project, structure the team around *user journeys* and end-to-end experiences, rather than assigning people to specific product surfaces or functional silos.
- [ ] **Develop Voice/Conversational Prototyping Skills:** Experiment with tools that allow you to "brain dump context" via voice (like Lovable) for rapid ideation, as this is faster and more natural than traditional typing methods.
