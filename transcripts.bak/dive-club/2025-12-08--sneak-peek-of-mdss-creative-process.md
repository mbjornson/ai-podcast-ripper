# Sneak peek of MDS’s creative process — Dive Club

## Summary
The episode provides a deep dive into the creative process of designer Matt Smith (MDS), using the development of the "Shift Nudge" website as a case study. Rather than presenting a clean final product, MDS walks the listener through his Figma file, showcasing dozens of discarded ideas, random inspirations (from Instagram or church services), and micro-iterations. The core discussion is not about the aesthetics themselves, but the internal, psychological struggle of the designer: the tension between creating a "cool" design and maintaining a cohesive, professional identity.

The conversation frames the design process as a struggle to define self—a professional portfolio must showcase work, not just how "zany and cool" the website is. MDS details the meticulous, often time-consuming nature of design decisions, such as pixelating a logo or debating the exact spacing of a paragraph indent. This process serves as a metaphor for any complex creative or professional endeavor, highlighting the difficulty of moving from boundless exploration to a focused, timeless execution.

## Key Points
- **The Identity Struggle:** The most significant challenge in design (and by extension, career/personal branding) is balancing creative freedom with defined identity. The goal is to make the site a showcase of *work* (portfolio products) rather than just a showcase of *design skill* (the website itself).
- **The Power of Micro-Detail:** MDS spends significant time discussing minute details, such as the proper handling of paragraph indents or the precise negative space required for a pixel icon. This emphasizes that mastery often lies in the adherence to "timeless principles of typography, layout and color," rather than adopting the latest trend.
- **Validation Over Assumption:** The discussion introduces the concept of using AI (Genway AI) for rapid market validation. Instead of guessing what customers want, the process involves drafting questions, selecting an Ideal Customer Profile (ICP), and having an AI agent conduct interviews on your behalf to get actionable insights quickly.
- **The Cost of Exploration:** The process is shown to be highly iterative, involving jumping between styles (e.g., pixel art, animated gradients, clean serif) and spending disproportionate time on elements that ultimately "didn't ship." This illustrates the need to manage the time sink of perfectionism.

## Tools & Resources
- **Raycast:** A highly recommended productivity application/extension described as an "extension of my fingertips" for improving computer efficiency.
- **Genway AI:** An AI tool designed for market validation. It allows users to draft questions and select an ICP, then runs simulated interviews with a panel of global participants to provide actionable insights.
- **Figma:** The primary tool used to demonstrate the creative process, showcasing both final and discarded ideas.

## Action Items
- [ ] **Implement AI Validation:** Use tools like Genway AI to move beyond assumptions. Before committing significant time or resources to a project, validate your core ideas by simulating customer interviews with a defined ICP.
- [ ] **Master Micro-Skills:** Dedicate time to mastering the foundational, often overlooked details of your craft (e.g., typography, negative space, spacing). True mastery is often found in the adherence to timeless principles, not the latest trend.
- [ ] **Optimize Workflow:** Adopt productivity tools like Raycast to minimize friction in your daily digital workflow, treating it as an extension of your physical capabilities.
- [ ] **Define Your Core Identity:** When starting a major project (business plan, portfolio, etc.), resist the urge to "go wide." Force yourself to define the single, unifying theme or core principle that must guide all decisions, even if it feels restrictive.
