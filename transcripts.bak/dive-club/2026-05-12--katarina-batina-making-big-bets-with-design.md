# Katarina Batina -  Making big bets with design — Dive Club

## Summary
This episode features Katarina Batina, Design Director at Shopify, discussing the strategy behind building the Shop app, a premium consumer experience. Batina emphasizes a “big bet” approach – deliberately disrupting conventional commerce interfaces by prioritizing brand storytelling and a more “romantic” shopping experience. The core idea is to move away from data-driven micro-optimizations and instead create a platform that allows merchants to truly express their brand and engage customers in a more meaningful way. A key element of this strategy involves leveraging AI, particularly for content generation and personalization, alongside empowering designers to experiment with new concepts and data access.

## Key Points
- **Disrupting Commerce Norms:** Shopify is intentionally moving away from density-focused interfaces (maximizing product views per scroll) and instead aiming for a “window shopping” experience, mirroring the engagement of offline retail.
- **Merchant-First Vision:** The entire strategy is rooted in a merchant-centric approach, recognizing the importance of merchants having control over their online store and brand storytelling.
- **AI as a Creative Tool:** AI is being used not just for optimization but for content generation (video creation, merchant posts) to inject life and interest into the shopping experience.
- **Data-Driven Experimentation:** Designers are given direct access to production data through tools like Harness/PI and Claude Code, allowing them to prototype and test ideas directly within the live environment, challenging conventional data constraints.
- **Shop Store Evolution:** The “Shop Store” is evolving from a basic online storefront to a richer, more dynamic experience, leveraging SVG imagery and brand storytelling to create a more immersive shopping environment.
- **Audacious Bets:** Shopify’s approach involves taking calculated risks and building things even when initial data suggests they might not work, relying on a team willing to embrace experimentation.
