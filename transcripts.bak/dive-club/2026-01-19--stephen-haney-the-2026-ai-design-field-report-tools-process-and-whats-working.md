# Stephen Haney - The 2026 AI Design Field Report (tools, process, and what's working) — Dive Club

## Summary
The episode analyzes the current state of design tooling and the impact of generative AI on the design workflow, moving beyond the hype cycle often seen on social media. Stephen Haney, founder of the design tool Paper, shares findings from speaking with top teams at companies like Shopify and Notion, arguing that the future of design is not about replacing human creativity, but about optimizing the process of exploration and prototyping. He addresses the debate over whether designers should code, concluding that while technical skills are powerful, the fundamental roles of design (problem space expansion) and engineering (building) remain distinct specialties.

Haney presents a "continuum" model for product development, charting the journey from initial, low-fidelity vision (the founder/CEO role) through product design (defining flow and "how") to highly detailed engineering (pixel precision and specific backend requirements). The core message is that AI is a powerful accelerator for the middle stages—specifically, creating stateful and interactive prototypes—but designers must understand where they fit on this spectrum to maximize their value.

## Key Points
- **Design is an Expansion of Problem Space:** Design's primary function is exploring the problem space, which requires studying prior art, visual experimentation, and communication—a role distinct from engineering.
- **The Continuum of Fidelity:** Product development is a linear progression from low-fidelity ideas (visionary role) $\rightarrow$ defining flow/experience (product design role) $\rightarrow$ pixel-perfect implementation (design engineering role).
- **AI Excels at Stateful Prototyping:** AI tools (like Cloud Code/Cursor) are revolutionary for creating interactive, stateful prototypes (e.g., showing how focus moves when a modal opens), which is significantly more complex and limited in traditional tools like Figma.
- **The "Double Diamond" Focus:** While the full project lifecycle is complex, the most common and valuable work for designers is often in the second diamond (the refinement/solution phase), where the problem is already defined and the focus is on iteration and improvement.
- **The Friction of Local Development:** The shift toward local development environments (using cloud code/Cursor) is creating new friction points for sharing and collaboration, which is a key challenge for the industry to overcome.
- **UX as a Tooling Gap:** Historically, the critical aspect of User Experience (UX)—such as ensuring proper focus flow when opening modals—has been a conceptual deliverable rather than a measurable, tool-supported requirement, and AI is beginning to solve this.

## Tools & Resources
- **Paper:** Stephen Haney's design tool, built around the insights of AI-assisted design and the product continuum.
- **Mobbin:** Highly recommended resource for designers, used by over 1 million designers (including those at Airbnb and Uber) for real-world UI pattern inspiration.
- **Framer:** Mentioned for its ability to provide seamless custom domain connections, making it a strong portfolio platform.
- **ShapeUp:** Recommended project management tool (via the Basecamp group) for managing complex project workflows.
- **Cursor/Cloud Code:** AI-powered coding environments that are enabling designers to prototype stateful interactions directly, bridging the gap between design and code.

## Action Items
- [ ] **Develop "Problem Space" Thinking:** Practice framing design challenges not as visual fixes, but as open questions requiring exploration (the "search" phase).
- [ ] **Master Stateful Prototyping:** Learn to use AI-assisted coding tools (like Cursor or similar cloud code environments) to build interactive prototypes that demonstrate complex user flows and state changes, rather than relying solely on static mockups.
- [ ] **Adopt the Continuum Mindset:** When starting a project, identify where you are on the fidelity spectrum (Visionary $\rightarrow$ Product Design $\rightarrow$ Engineering) and tailor your deliverables accordingly.
- [ ] **Utilize Mobbin Daily:** Use Mobbin as a primary source of inspiration to study how top companies handle specific UI patterns and flows.
- [ ] **Build a Professional Portfolio with a Custom Domain:** Use a platform like Framer to ensure your portfolio reflects a professional, finished product, not just a collection of screens.
