# Luis Ouriach - How are design systems changing? — Dive Club

## Summary
The episode explores how the rapid advancement of Large Language Models (LLMs) and AI-driven code generation is fundamentally redefining the purpose and structure of design systems (DS). Luis Ouriach, a Designer Advocate at Figma, argues that DS have shifted from being a mere "quality bar" maintained by a siloed team to becoming the absolute centerpiece of all successful engineering work. Because modern tools rely heavily on structured inputs to generate code, the quality and consistency of the underlying system are now mission-critical.

The core discussion centers on the breakdown of traditional professional silos. In the past, roles were strictly separated (PM owns PRD, Designer owns design, Engineer owns code). Today, the expectation is that all professionals—designers, product managers, and engineers—must contribute expertise across the entire product lifecycle, from initial documentation to high-fidelity prototyping. This necessitates a new level of cross-functional collaboration and process reinvention, particularly in large enterprises.

## Key Points
- **DS as the Centerpiece, Not the Side Project:** The purpose of a design system has shifted from simply raising the bar of quality to becoming the foundational input that dictates what AI tools (like LLMs) can successfully generate. Poorly maintained systems will lead to a "landfill" of unusable code.
- **The Documentation Imperative:** Following the initial hype cycle around tokens and refactoring, the current critical element is robust, consistent, and predictable documentation. Guidelines are now paramount, forming the foundation for all future workflows, regardless of the format.
- **Agentic Design Systems:** The industry is moving toward "agentic" systems, where automation is used not just for component generation, but for complex processes like token pipelines, documentation updates, and even automated testing (e.g., LLMs testing apps by navigating flows and fixing bugs).
- **The Collapse of Silos:** The most significant process change is the expectation that all roles contribute across phases. The ability to prototype, validate, and iterate must be accessible to non-engineers, and engineers must be able to contribute to PRDs.
- **The Warning Against Over-Zeal:** While process reinvention is exciting, teams must avoid becoming overzealous. It is crucial to maintain clear lines of responsibility and define minimum output expectations for each individual role, even when collaborating tightly.

## Tools & Resources
- **Lovable:** An AI tool mentioned as an advanced LLM capable of solving complex tasks, featuring intelligent planning, prompt queuing, and automated testing (it tests apps by opening its own browser, navigating flows, and fixing bugs).
- **Granola MCP:** A specific release/feature that allows users to connect AI tools directly to meeting notes, enabling the ability to find specific topics, pull out action items, and answer questions based on meeting history.

## Quotable
> "The system becomes the centerpiece of all successful engineering work because the more we rely on a tool to create our code, the more we rely on a system to tell that tool what to code."
>
> "We all collaborate tighter as an individual unit and not relying on those silos."

## Action Items
- [ ] **Skill Development:** Become proficient in cross-functional contribution. Practice skills outside your core role (e.g., if you are a designer, learn to structure a PRD; if you are a PM, understand basic component structure).
- [ ] **Process Mastery:** Advocate for "synchronous and asynchronous" collaboration rituals that treat the team as a single unit, rather than departmental silos.
- [ ] **Technical Focus:** Prioritize building robust, comprehensive documentation for any system or process you manage. Treat documentation as the primary output, knowing it will feed future AI tools.
- [ ] **Mindset Shift:** Adopt the mindset that your role is not defined by the tool you use, but by the expertise you bring to the *process*. Be prepared to "flex in and out" of different phases of development.
- [ ] **Tools to Explore:** Investigate AI tools like Lovable or similar LLM-powered testing/automation platforms to understand how they are changing the definition of "done."
