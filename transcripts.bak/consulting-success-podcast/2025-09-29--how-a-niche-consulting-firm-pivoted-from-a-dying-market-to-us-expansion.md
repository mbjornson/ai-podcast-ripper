# How a Niche Consulting Firm Pivoted from a Dying Market to US Expansion — Consulting Success Podcast

## Summary
The episode features Dr. Sherry Boudram and Lucas McCann, who discuss the high-stakes process of pivoting a niche consulting firm when the core market they serve begins to dry up or collapse. Their expertise centers on the highly regulated cannabis licensing industry in Canada and the US. They detail how their firm successfully transitioned from merely filing complex applications to becoming strategic advisors, a pivot that allowed them to dominate a market gap.

The discussion highlights that in highly regulated, complex industries, the greatest value often lies not in technical execution, but in *clarity* and *guidance*. They explain that the initial market process was so opaque and slow (requiring years for regulatory responses) that companies needed more than just an application filer; they needed a "sherpa" to navigate the regulatory labyrinth, manage investor expectations, and understand the underlying business strategy.

## Key Points
- **The Value of Leading Indicators:** The primary lesson is the necessity of watching data and spotting leading indicators that signal a market shift, providing a crucial 6-8 month head start for a strategic pivot.
- **The Advisory Pivot:** They successfully shifted their service offering from technical compliance (filing applications for cultivation, processing, or manufacturing) to comprehensive advisory services. This move allowed them to capture the high-value counseling aspect of the process, which was previously unavailable.
- **Addressing Market Opacity:** They capitalized on the "mysterious" nature of the regulatory body (Health Canada), which used poorly monitored inboxes and could take years to respond. This inefficiency created a massive gap that their deep industry knowledge and connections allowed them to fill.
- **Physical Presence as Trust Builder:** The strategy of establishing a physical, "boots on the ground" presence was critical for building unbreakable trust in a highly regulated, high-stakes industry.
- **Scaling from Zero:** The firm started at a "kitchen table" in 2018 and scaled rapidly, demonstrating that early growth can be fueled by solving acute, systemic pain points (like regulatory delays) rather than just meeting existing demand.
