# How Consultants With Less Experience Than You Are Charging More — Consulting Success Podcast

## Summary
This episode of the Consulting Success Podcast tackles a critical issue for experienced consultants: why they’re often undercharging compared to less experienced peers. The core argument is that most consultants focus on selling time, which clients readily minimize to reduce costs. The guest, Michael Ziperski, argues that the key to higher rates isn't more experience, but a shift in mindset – selling outcomes rather than effort. Ziperski’s credibility stems from Consulting Success’s model, where coaches have built high-six and seven-figure businesses, and the Clarity Coaching program offers personalized support and a global community. The episode emphasizes a growing market trend toward outcome-based pricing, accelerated by AI, and presents data suggesting a significant opportunity for consultants to reframe their value proposition.

## Key Points
- **Time vs. Outcome Pricing:** The fundamental problem is that consultants are still primarily selling hours, which clients can easily manipulate to lower costs. This is a significant disconnect from client needs.
- **80% Rate Increase Potential:** 80% of consultants who clarify their positioning can raise their rates within 90 days, driven by reframing value around outcomes.
- **Inflationary Fee Increases are Insufficient:** A typical 7% annual fee increase is simply inflation and not a sustainable growth strategy.
- **AI’s Impact:** The rise of AI is automating billable hour tasks, making the time-based pricing model increasingly obsolete.
- **Data-Driven Shift:** The market is moving towards fixed-price, outcome-based work, reflecting a change in client expectations.
- **The Fear Factor:** Consultants often hesitate to raise rates due to fear of losing a deal, rather than focusing on the true value they deliver.
