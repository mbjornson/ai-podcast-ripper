# Simplify Consulting, Grow Your Business with Abhijit Verekar — Consulting Success Podcast

## Summary
The episode centers on the strategic shift required for consulting businesses to thrive in a crowded, technology-saturated market, arguing that the biggest growth opportunity often lies not in scaling, but in simplifying the core value proposition. The discussion features Abhijit Varoukar, CEO of Avero Advisors, a 50-person firm specializing in public sector modernization for U.S. cities and counties. Varoukar's credibility stems from his ability to operate as an independent, third-party advisor—a role he has cultivated by deliberately avoiding the common pitfalls of product reselling or deep system implementation.

Avero's unique value proposition is positioned as the trusted guide that helps government leaders untangle complex, decades-old infrastructure problems. The core thesis is that most competitors (system integrators, software vendors) force clients into a "box" (i.e., "You need Dynamics 365"), while Avero focuses on identifying the client's fundamental business vision and strategic needs first. The conversation also addresses how to leverage modern tools like AI and micro SaaS to accelerate delivery and maintain high fees without losing the critical human element of strategic advisory.

## Key Points
- **The Power of Independent Advisory:** The most powerful differentiator in consulting is being the independent, third-party advisor. This position allows the firm to challenge the status quo and guide clients toward strategic solutions, rather than simply implementing a specific vendor's product.
- **Challenging the Client's Box:** Successful consulting involves being direct and counter-intuitive. Instead of accepting the client's stated problem (e.g., "We need a 365 configuration"), the advisor must pivot the conversation to the underlying strategic need ("What is your vision? How are you fundamentally changing your business processes?").
- **The Government Niche Advantage:** The public sector (cities, counties) is a high-margin, low-protected niche because government leaders are often not fully up-to-speed on market technology, making them highly receptive to objective, third-party guidance.
- **AI as an Accelerator, Not a Replacement:** AI and micro SaaS should be used to accelerate delivery and improve efficiency, allowing the firm to maintain high fees and authority without sacrificing the human strategic touch that defines true consulting.
- **Relationship Building in Regulated Industries:** Accessing high-level decision-makers (Mayors, CIOs) in regulated sectors requires more than traditional marketing. Success relies on a combination of thought leadership (white papers), targeted outbound efforts, and leveraging a long roster of existing clients for introductions.

## Action Items
- [ ] **Re-evaluate your core offering:** Shift your pitch from "We implement X software" to "We help you define the fundamental business process change required to achieve Y outcome."
- [ ] **Adopt the "Challenge First" framework:** In every client meeting, dedicate the first 10 minutes to questioning the client's assumptions and the limitations of their current technology/process, regardless of what they initially ask for.
- [ ] **Develop a thought leadership asset:** Create a white paper or detailed guide focused on a major pain point in your niche (e.g., "The 5 Strategic Shifts Local Governments Must Make to Survive the AI Era").
- [ ] **Focus on relationship density:** Prioritize getting introductions from existing, satisfied clients over cold outreach or mass marketing efforts.
- [ ] **Study the public sector sales cycle:** Understand that in regulated industries, the sales cycle is long and requires "staying power" and multiple touchpoints, not just a single pitch.
