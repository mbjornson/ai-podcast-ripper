# Is AI Killing Consulting? — Consulting Success Podcast

## Summary
This episode addresses the pervasive fear that Artificial Intelligence will eliminate the consulting profession entirely, challenging this narrative with a focus on adaptation rather than obsolescence. The discussion features Michelle, Head of Growth at Consulting Success, who draws upon her experience working with multiple seven and eight-figure companies to provide a nuanced view of AI's impact. She argues that history shows disruptive technologies (like the internet or fuel injection) don't eliminate entire industries; they fundamentally change them, creating new demands for expertise.

Michelle’s core thesis is highly actionable: AI will not replace consulting; rather, it will replace *consultants who fail to integrate and leverage AI* into their business models. She cautions against complacency, using historical parallels (like the shift from physical retail to e-commerce) to warn that those relying solely on decades-old methods risk being left behind by rapidly accelerating technology adoption.

The conversation concludes with a practical framework for applying AI across all major business functions—Sales, Marketing, Operations, and Fulfillment—to achieve massive efficiency gains and reduce reliance on manual labor.

## Key Points
- **AI is an Augmentation Tool, Not a Replacement:** While AI will automate "the heavy lifting" and much of the legwork (e.g., research that used to take weeks), it cannot replicate human expertise, creativity, or the ability to navigate the "messiness" and nuance of human client situations.
- **The Danger of Complacency:** The biggest threat is not AI itself, but the failure to adapt. Michelle warns against assuming that methods that worked for decades will continue to work indefinitely in a rapidly evolving technological landscape.
- **AI's Impact on Business Pillars:** AI can be applied across all four major business pillars (Sales, Marketing, Operations, and Fulfillment) to drastically increase efficiency. This capability allows businesses to achieve the output of large teams with significantly reduced manpower.
- **The "Know How" Advantage:** The ability to understand *how* AI impacts your clients' markets and your direct competition is paramount. Those who ignore this dual impact are doing a disservice both to their clients and themselves.
