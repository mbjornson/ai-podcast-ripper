# How Top Consultants Position Themselves to Close Faster — Consulting Success Podcast

## Summary
This episode provides a deep dive into the strategic positioning required for consultants to move from average practitioners to top-tier authorities who close deals faster. The core thesis is that success is not based on general competence, but on highly visible, specialized, and differentiated expertise. The discussion outlines a systematic approach to building authority, emphasizing that a consultant must become known for a specific niche, develop a unique methodology, and consistently prove their value through content and demonstrable proof.

The speaker, who is also promoting his "Inner Circle" community and "Clarity Coaching" program, frames these strategies as necessary steps for founders already generating significant revenue ($1M-$5M+). The advice is highly actionable, focusing on shifting the consultant's role from service provider to indispensable thought leader. The overall message is that visibility and proprietary systems are the primary levers for commanding higher fees and attracting ideal clients who don't even know they need help yet.

## Key Points
- **Specialization is Non-Negotiable:** Top consultants are specialists, not generalists. Attempting to serve too many types of people or offer too many services creates "a recipe for confusion" and severely limits recall when a need arises.
- **The Power of the Network:** The consulting business is relationship-based. To maximize referrals, one must cultivate a strong network and strategically find out where their *ideal clients* spend time, rather than simply attending industry-specific conferences.
- **Visible Authority (The Visible Expert):** To establish authority, one must be prolific with putting intellectual property (IP) into the marketplace. This means speaking at conferences, appearing on podcasts, and writing for trade publications. Visibility is the mechanism that elevates a consultant to a "top figure" in the client's mind.
- **Content as the Ultimate Sales Tool:** Prolific content creation (LinkedIn, articles, webinars) is the best way to "sell without selling." By giving immense value and demonstrating deep knowledge, the consultant positions themselves as the obvious, pre-vetted solution when the client realizes they have a mandate/problem.
- **Proprietary Differentiation:** Don't just work in a niche; create a unique, named methodology or process. This proprietary IP increases the "premium perception" because the client assumes the system "must work" if it has been thought out and formalized.
- **Proof Over Promises:** Never just talk about abilities. Top consultants demonstrate proof. This means actively building case studies, gathering testimonials, and, if starting out, leveraging past employment wins, patents, or awards to build immediate credibility.

## Action Items
- [ ] **Define Your Niche:** Immediately articulate the single, narrow area you want to be known for. Eliminate general service offerings.
- [ ] **Audit Your Network:** Identify 3-5 specific places where your ideal clients spend time (e.g., specific industry forums, non-consulting conferences, trade publications) and plan to show up there.
- [ ] **Develop Proprietary IP:** Formalize your current process into a named methodology (e.g., "The 3-Step [Your Niche] Framework"). This is your unique selling proposition.
- [ ] **Content Cadence:** Commit to a minimum weekly content output (e.g., 1 LinkedIn article, 1 podcast appearance, or 1 industry comment) to build visible authority.
- [ ] **Build the Proof Portfolio:** Systematically gather 3-5 detailed case studies and testimonials that quantify the results you achieved for clients. If new, pull 2-3 major wins from past employment to use as proof.
