# Use This AI "Teammate" Method To Justify 30% Higher Consulting Fees with Michel Fortin — Consulting Success Podcast

## Summary
The episode analyzes how Artificial Intelligence (AI) is fundamentally changing the consulting landscape, arguing that AI's role is not to replace consultants but to redefine their value proposition. The core thesis revolves around a cyclical pattern known as "High-Tech, High-Touch," where increasing technological automation necessitates an increased demand for genuine human connection and authenticity in both business operations and client interactions.

The discussion introduces two critical frameworks for modern consulting: the evolution of content authority (EAT 2.0) and the strategic shift from mere efficiency to true effectiveness. Consultants are advised that AI should be leveraged primarily to handle repetitive, low-level tasks, freeing up human time and cognitive capacity to focus on high-value activities like complex reasoning, creative problem-solving, and building deep client trust.

## Key Points
- **The High-Tech, High-Touch Cycle:** Technology adoption follows a predictable pattern: the more automated (high-tech) we become, the greater the human craving for authentic connection and community (high-touch). This trend is observed from the rise of social media to the current integration of AI.
- **AI's Role: Efficiency vs. Effectiveness:** AI should be viewed as a tool for *efficiency*—automating repetitive tasks like content creation or data handling. The true opportunity, however, lies in using that saved time to become significantly more *effective*, focusing on unique human insights and complex problem-solving capacity.
- **EAT 2.0 Framework:** Google's traditional authority framework (Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness - EAT) is evolving into a new standard: **Empathy, Authenticity, and Transparency**. Consultants must adapt their content and client communication to demonstrate these three elements to maintain online credibility.
- **AI Limitations (The Reasoning Gap):** Current AI models are primarily sophisticated *memory tools*, not reasoning engines. They struggle with complex problem-solving that requires deep inference or novel thought, presenting a current opportunity for human consultants to prove superior critical thinking and judgment.

## Action Items
- [ ] **Audit Your Workflow:** Identify the most repetitive, low-value tasks in your consulting practice (e.g., first drafts of reports, scheduling, basic data aggregation) and map them for immediate AI automation/delegation to gain time.
- [ ] **Shift Focus from Output to Insight:** When pitching services or creating content, stop emphasizing *what* you know (expertise) and start demonstrating *how* you connect with the client's emotional pain points (empathy) and how transparently your process is (transparency).
- [ ] **Develop "Human Proof" Content:** Revamp your online presence to actively showcase genuine human insight, personal stories of failure/learning, or unique qualitative data that AI cannot generate. This builds EAT 2.0 authority.
- [ ] **Practice High-Touch Connection:** Intentionally schedule time for non-transactional, in-person (or video) check-ins with key clients to reinforce the human connection that technology is making scarce.
