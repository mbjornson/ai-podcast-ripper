# How To Build Systems (so Your Consulting Business Runs Without You) — Consulting Success Podcast

## Summary
The episode provides a comprehensive guide on systematizing a consulting business to achieve true scalability and freedom. The core premise is that any business model that relies heavily on the founder's day-to-day involvement is inherently fragile and difficult to sell or grow beyond a certain point. Building robust systems allows the business to run autonomously, granting the owner time, flexibility, and peace of mind. The discussion emphasizes that systematization is not merely about efficiency; it is about extracting the founder's intellectual property—the knowledge "between their ears"—and making it accessible and repeatable for the entire team.

The speaker outlines a multi-faceted approach, covering everything from defining core service methodologies to mapping out operational procedures (SOPs) for sales, marketing, and internal operations. The advice is highly practical, focusing on process documentation, leveraging technology (like PM software), and fundamentally changing the founder's role from doer to architect of the system.

## Key Points
- **The Freedom Dividend:** A business that runs without the founder's constant involvement provides more freedom (e.g., taking longer holidays) and significantly increases the business's valuation and sellability.
- **Documenting Methodology is Foundational:** The first step is to document every step of your core service offering. This process allows you to identify and eliminate inefficiencies.
    - **Efficiency Example:** One client reduced their process from 15 steps to 6 steps, maintaining client outcomes while becoming significantly more profitable.
- **Scope of SOPs:** Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) must cover *all* business areas, not just service delivery. This includes marketing, sales, and general business operations.
- **Structured Training is an Investment:** Instead of expecting new hires to "hit the ground running," implement structured training that involves shadowing, participation in meetings, and gradual increases in autonomy. This upfront time investment pays massive dividends later.
- **Decision-Making Guidelines:** To prevent bottlenecks and slow the team down, establish clear guidelines defining what decisions each team member can make independently versus those that require senior approval.
- **PM Software as a Central Nervous System:** For growing teams and multiple clients, project management software (e.g., Asana, Monday.com) is essential for organizing overlapping schedules, timelines, and information flow. Crucially, the *use* of the tool must also be documented and standardized to prevent new inefficiencies.

## Tools & Resources
- **Project Management Software:** Asana, Monday.com (Essential for organizing multiple clients, overlapping schedules, and team communication).
- **Documentation Systems:** SOPs (Standard Operating Procedures) and documented methodologies/frameworks.
- **Training Model:** Shadowing and gradual autonomy (A structured approach to onboarding and skill transfer).

## Action Items
- [ ] **Audit Your Core Process:** Take your most complex service offering and map out every single step. Identify at least three steps that can be eliminated or combined to streamline the process (The 15-to-6 exercise).
- [ ] **Create SOPs for Non-Delivery Functions:** Document the exact process for two non-delivery areas (e.g., "How to onboard a new lead" or "How to run a client kickoff meeting").
- [ ] **Implement Decision Matrix:** For your team, create a simple matrix defining which decisions (e.g., budget overruns < $500, client scope changes) they can make autonomously, and which require your sign-off.
- [ ] **Standardize Tech Usage:** If you use Slack or a PM tool, document the "rules of engagement" for that platform. (e.g., "Use X channel for Y topic only," or "All task updates must be logged in the PM tool, not via email.")
- [ ] **Schedule Structured Training:** For your next new hire or junior team member, replace general task assignments with a structured training plan involving shadowing and defined checkpoints for increasing autonomy.
