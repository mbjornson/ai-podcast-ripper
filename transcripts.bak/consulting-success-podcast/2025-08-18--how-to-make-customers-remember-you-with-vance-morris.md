# How to Make Customers Remember You with Vance Morris — Consulting Success Podcast

## Summary
The episode focuses on building premium businesses and achieving high client retention by fundamentally redesigning the customer experience (CX). The guest, Vance Morris, is a consultant who leveraged his deep knowledge of Disney's world-class customer service principles. He initially applied these concepts to seemingly mundane home services—such as mold remediation and carpet cleaning—to position them as premium offerings in an otherwise commoditized market.

Morris’s core thesis is that true business growth isn't about acquiring new customers; it's about creating an "unforgettable customer experience" that builds immediate retention. He argues that the client journey must be treated like a production or a show, where every touchpoint—from initial contact to post-service follow-up—is designed for maximum emotional impact and perceived value.

His consulting practice now translates these principles into high-level bootcamps (including immersive Disney trips) for professionals across various niches, teaching them how to build systems that command premium pricing by making their services feel like an extraordinary event rather than a simple transaction.

## Key Points
- **The Retention Imperative:** The most critical area of focus is retention, not acquisition. Morris notes that while customer acquisition is "ridiculously expensive," the cost of keeping a customer through proactive efforts (mailings, newsletters) can be minimal ($22/year in his carpet cleaning example), making it an overwhelmingly profitable focus.
- **The 10% Problem:** Only about 10% of businesses he speaks to have thoughtfully structured systems for retention, and zero have allocated actual budget to it because the topic is "not sexy." This highlights a massive market gap.
- **Defining Premium Value:** Charging premium prices requires more than just raising rates; it demands supporting intangible value. This includes guarantees (like warranties) and, most importantly, *experiential* elements that turn service delivery into a production or "theater."
- **Proactive Retention System:** The retention system must begin *before* the client consumes the product or service. The entire customer journey—the setup, the anticipation, and the execution—must be extraordinary to ensure loyalty.
- **The Theater Principle:** Every employee (or the owner) must understand that they are "on stage" in their own production because the customers are watching. This mindset shift forces a higher level of performance and attention to detail than standard service delivery requires.

## Action Items
- [ ] **Audit Your CX:** Map out every single touchpoint of your service delivery process (from initial lead contact to post-service follow-up). Identify where the experience is merely functional versus extraordinary.
- [ ] **Implement "Theater":** Treat your business operations as a performance. Train staff and yourself to operate with the assumption that you are constantly being observed, forcing perfection in every interaction.
- [ ] **Prioritize Retention Budget:** Calculate your Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) vs. your potential retention cost. Allocate specific budget and time toward proactive loyalty efforts (e.g., personalized physical gifts or high-quality print newsletters).
