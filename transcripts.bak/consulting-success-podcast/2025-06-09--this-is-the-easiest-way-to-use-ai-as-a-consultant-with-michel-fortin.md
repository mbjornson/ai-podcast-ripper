# This is the Easiest Way To Use AI As A Consultant with Michel Fortin — Consulting Success Podcast

## Summary
The episode addresses the pervasive fear that Artificial Intelligence will render the consulting profession obsolete. The discussion, featuring Michelle Fortin, Head of Growth at Consulting Success, reframes this narrative by arguing that AI will not replace expertise, but rather it will replace outdated processes and manual "legwork." The core message is that mastery of AI is no longer optional; it is a mandatory operational requirement for modern consultants seeking to maintain relevance and scale.

The conversation emphasizes that the true value of a consultant lies in human elements—authority, deep experience, nuanced problem-solving, and creative thinking—which current AI models cannot replicate because they are limited by their training data. Instead of viewing AI as a threat, listeners must adopt it as an accelerant for efficiency, allowing consultants to drastically reduce required manpower while increasing output across all business pillars (sales, marketing, operations, fulfillment).

## Key Points
- **AI Replaces Process, Not Expertise:** The biggest misconception is that AI will eliminate consulting. In reality, AI excels at automating the heavy lifting and research—the time-consuming processes—freeing up human consultants to focus on high-level strategic thinking, client nuance, and complex problem framing.
- **The Two Pillars of AI Adoption (Mandatory Focus):** To future-proof your practice, you must analyze AI's impact in two distinct ways: 1) How does it change the market/industry for your *clients*? (Client value proposition). 2) How does it change your *direct competition* and your own business model? (Competitive advantage).
- **The Acceleration Curve is Non-Negotiable:** The adoption of AI is not a fad; its growth rate is accelerating rapidly, driven by fierce market competition. Ignoring this trend is akin to ignoring the internet or cell phones decades ago—it guarantees falling behind competitors who are actively integrating the technology.
- **Operational Efficiency via Automation:** AI can be applied across all four major business pillars (Sales, Marketing, Operations, Fulfillment) to automate processes that previously required large teams of bodies and time. This allows small firms to achieve massive scale with minimal overhead.
