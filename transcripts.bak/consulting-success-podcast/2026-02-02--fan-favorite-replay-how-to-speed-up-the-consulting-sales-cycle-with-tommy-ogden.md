# Fan Favorite Replay: How To Speed Up The Consulting Sales Cycle with Tommy Ogden — Consulting Success Podcast

## Summary
The episode features Tommy Ogden, a consulting leader, discussing his unique career trajectory that provides a powerful contrast between working at a massive corporate entity and building a successful boutique firm. Ogden's background includes founding Anaxis Consulting (a boutique IT advisory firm focused on oil and gas) which grew to $20 million in revenue with about 100 employees before being acquired by Accenture. After years within the large corporation, he eventually left to co-found ActiveEra, a new boutique firm that achieved multi-million dollar revenue within its first year.

The core discussion revolves around the strategic advantages of boutique firms over large consulting houses. Ogden highlights that while large firms offer immense scale and resources, they often come with bureaucratic hierarchies and rigid boundaries. He argues that true, rapid growth and momentum are often driven not by complex marketing tactics or sheer size, but by deep, hard-earned relationships, trust, and the freedom afforded by a flatter organizational structure.

## Key Points
- **Boutique Advantage:** The primary advantage of a boutique firm is the flat hierarchy and the ability to maintain direct access to leadership (e.g., calling a partner or managing director and getting an immediate answer). This contrasts sharply with the bureaucracy of large, public companies.
- **The Power of Relationships:** Ogden emphasizes that rapid business momentum is built through "deep relationships" and "hard-earned trust," rather than relying on sophisticated positioning tricks or marketing funnels.
- **The Danger of Scale:** Large firms, while impressive (e.g., Accenture having 743,000 people), create boundaries and hierarchies that can stifle personal passion and entrepreneurial initiative.
- **Industry Niche Focus:** The initial success of Anaxis Consulting was built by focusing intensely on a specific vertical (oil and gas/energy), demonstrating the power of deep domain expertise over broad generalism.
- **The Cost of Compliance:** A concrete example was given where Ogden was told to stop pursuing sales opportunities in Latin America because the firm was regionally focused on North America/Houston, illustrating how corporate boundaries can limit personal and business passion.

## Action Items
- [ ] **Develop Niche Expertise:** Identify a highly specific, underserved industry vertical (like oil and gas) and focus all efforts on becoming the undisputed expert in that niche.
- [ ] **Prioritize Relationship Building:** Shift focus from "marketing tactics" to cultivating deep, trust-based relationships with key partners and clients, viewing them as the primary engine for growth.
