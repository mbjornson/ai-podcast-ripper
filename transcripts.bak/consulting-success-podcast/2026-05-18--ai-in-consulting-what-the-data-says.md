# AI in Consulting: What The Data Says — Consulting Success Podcast

## Summary
This podcast episode, part of the Consulting Success Podcast series, features Michael, a consultant and founder of Consulting Success, discussing the findings of their October Business Barometer Survey. The survey investigates how consultants are utilizing AI, their perceptions of its threat, and where they’re spending their time and energy. The core message is that AI is a powerful tool, but its impact hinges on how consultants choose to implement and adapt to it. The episode emphasizes a proactive approach to understanding and leveraging AI, rather than fearing it. Michael’s credibility stems from Consulting Success’s business model – coaches have built high-six or seven-figure consulting businesses, and the Clarity Coaching program offers personalized support and a community of high-performing consultants. The episode’s primary signal is the data-driven insights into consultant behavior and attitudes toward AI, offering a practical perspective for those seeking to scale their consulting businesses.

## Key Points
- **AI Adoption is High:** 72.5% of consultants are currently using AI for client deliverables and proposal creation. This demonstrates a significant shift in how consultants are approaching their work.
- **Internal Tasks are a Secondary Use:** 11.8% are using AI primarily for internal administrative tasks, suggesting a need for consultants to explore more strategic applications.
- **7.8% are Aware but Not Implementing:** Almost 8% are aware of AI but haven’t begun to implement it, highlighting a potential gap between awareness and action.
- **AI is Not a Major Revenue Threat (for many):** Approximately 50% of respondents believe AI poses a minimal threat (0-10% impact), indicating a degree of confidence or a lack of understanding of AI’s potential.
- **Significant Threat Perception (for some):** 9.8% believe AI poses a threat of 51% or greater to their revenue, emphasizing the need for proactive planning and adaptation.
- **Older Consultants More Concerned:** The survey revealed that consultants with longer experience are more concerned about AI’s impact, possibly due to a greater awareness of potential disruption.
- **Proposal Win Rates are a Key Issue:** 50% of respondents have at least two proposals in a year that generate no revenue, indicating a potential weakness in their sales process.
- **Time Waste Identified:** Consultants are primarily wasting time on content marketing (33.3%), networking events (27.5%), writing ineffective proposals (17.6%), and managing junior team members (11.8%).
