# Your Consulting Business Can Feel Better with Elliot Begoun — Consulting Success Podcast

## Summary
The episode discusses the common struggle of high-achieving consultants who reach a seven-figure revenue milestone only to find that success feels like a job they can't escape, leading to depletion and boredom. The guest, Elliot Began, details his own journey, which involved moving from a traditional corporate career path—a path that left him feeling unfulfilled—to building a consulting practice from scratch. His initial attempts were broad and unfocused, aiming merely to replace his income. The core discussion centers on the necessary evolution of a consulting business model: moving past the frantic cycle of "constant funnel feeding" toward a more sustainable, depth-focused, and intentional advisory practice.

Began's success was not linear. He found his inflection point by strategically narrowing his focus (niching) and identifying specific industry ecosystems. He emphasizes that true growth comes not from casting a wide net, but from becoming the definitive, specialized voice within a contained, high-value environment. The overall message is a blueprint for scaling a business past its initial revenue ceiling by prioritizing depth and strategic positioning over sheer volume of clients.

## Key Points
- **The Danger of the Plateau:** Success can feel like a cage. The "hidden cost of building a model that requires constant funnel feeding" is burnout and a loss of fulfillment, even when revenue is high.
- **The Shift from Income Replacement to Intentionality:** Early business goals are often "myopic"—simply replacing income. True, sustainable business growth requires moving to a stage where the goal is *intentionality* (defining where and what you want to fish for).
- **The Power of Narrowing (Niching):** The most significant inflection point was realizing that narrowing the focus (niching) made his voice "much louder in a narrower environment." This is described as the "bigger fish in a smaller pond" scenario, which is more effective than being a generalist.
- **Ecosystem Building:** To amplify a message, do not just market; build within an existing ecosystem. Identify places (publications, events, podcasts) where your ideal clients and information sources naturally congregate. The ecosystem does the work of amplifying your message for you.
- **The Shift in Client Model:** The ultimate goal is to reduce client count while maintaining or increasing revenue, prioritizing "more depth, and more breathing room" over constant volume.

## Action Items
- [ ] **Audit Your Niche:** Identify the single most specific, high-value industry or problem you solve. Stop casting a wide net and commit to being the definitive expert in this narrow field.
- [ ] **Map Your Ecosystem:** Identify 3-5 specific, high-concentration sources (e.g., industry-specific Slack groups, niche podcasts, trade publications) where your ideal clients gather. Focus your content and networking efforts exclusively on these locations.
- [ ] **Define Your "Why":** Articulate your business goals not in terms of revenue replacement, but in terms of impact, mission, or desired lifestyle (intentionality).
- [ ] **Shift from Volume to Depth:** Review your current client roster and identify 1-2 clients where you can transition from transactional work to deep, strategic advisory work.
