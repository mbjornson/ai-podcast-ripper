# Pricing Consulting Services: A Deep Dive with Garrett Delph — Consulting Success Podcast

## Summary
The episode, featuring Garrett Delph of Clarity Ops, moves beyond general consulting advice to focus intensely on operational efficiency and scaling for established businesses. Delph's expertise stems from building and selling multiple businesses, allowing him to identify "hidden operational inefficiencies" that erode profits—a problem many successful consultancies overlook. His firm specializes in helping fast-growing companies tighten processes, eliminate waste, and reorganize for scale, ultimately leading to increased gross margins necessary to fund new growth initiatives.

The core discussion centers on the difficulty of marketing complex solutions when clients themselves don't fully understand the root cause of their problems. Delph emphasizes that success requires extreme specificity in both targeting and messaging. He shares his journey from initially being too broad with his client base—a mistake he "paid the price dearly" for—to adopting a highly defined, data-driven approach to acquisition and positioning.

## Key Points
- **The Operational Trifecta:** The goal of operational consulting is not just process improvement, but achieving a trifecta: 1) Eliminating waste/inefficiency/bloat; 2) Reorganizing for scale/growth; and 3) Achieving increased gross margins to fund future growth initiatives.
- **Highly Specific Ideal Client Profile (ICP):** Instead of general industry targeting, Delph uses quantitative thresholds as signals:
    *   Revenue threshold: Typically around $15 million+.
    *   Employee count barrier: Typically 75–100+ employees.
- **The Power of Specificity in Messaging:** The most critical lesson is that being too broad with your target market or service offering will lead to wasted time and resources ("paid the price dearly"). Success requires speaking a language so specific it resonates immediately with the ideal client.
- **Structured Networking Tactic:** When launching, Delph did not advertise broadly but approached his network asking for a concrete number of referrals (specifically, three people). This tactic provides an objective goal that is less "salesy" than asking for ten or more.

## Tools & Resources
- **Clarity Ops:** A boutique consulting firm specializing in operational waste elimination and process reorganization for scaling businesses.
- **consultingsuccess.com/grow:** The primary resource mentioned for booking growth sessions (Note: This is a promotional link, but the underlying concept of "growth systems" is useful).

## Quotable
> "The problem isn't your expertise it's clarity."
> "I paid the price dearly because I brought on the wrong customers... I would have been better off setting the boundaries out of the gate."

## Action Items
- [ ] **Define Your ICP by Metrics:** Stop defining your ideal client solely by industry or job title. Establish minimum quantitative thresholds (e.g., $X million revenue, Y+ employees) that signal a business is at the exact point where operational waste becomes critically expensive.
- [ ] **Audit for Operational Waste:** Conduct an internal audit focused specifically on "bloat" and process inefficiency, rather than just revenue streams. Identify 3-5 areas where current processes are draining time/money without adding measurable value.
- [ ] **Implement the 'Three Referral' Tactic:** When engaging your network or speaking to peers, ask for a specific, low-friction number of referrals (e.g., "Who do you know who is grappling with X problem? Can you think of three people?") rather than an open-ended request.
- [ ] **Sharpen Your Content Focus:** Ensure all marketing content is razor sharp and speaks only to the *specific* pain points your ICP knows they have, avoiding general industry advice.
